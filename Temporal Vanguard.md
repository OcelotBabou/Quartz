*[]*(**The dangers surrounding Abdurii are both numerous and varied. From the spies and saboteurs emerging from the Shadow Aisle Mountains to the south, to the twisted creatures of the Misty Swamps, the Temporal Vanguard is constantly on high alert. To the north, the perilous western fringe of the Dividing Forest is home to monstrous predators and raiding parties, while the imposing Gravenpeak to the west serves as a base for corrupted dwarves who launch calculated assaults. Further compounding these challenges are the relentless raiders from the Northern Badlands, testing Abdurii’s defenses at every turn. The city's ability to withstand and respond to these diverse threats makes it a linchpin in New Birania’s efforts to secure its western frontier**.)
