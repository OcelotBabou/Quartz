- **[[Avatolika]]**: A small, resilient kingdom defined by community strength and persistent border tensions.
- **[[Doulas]]**: On the verge of collapse, Doulas fights for survival after losing Neodoulas and facing relentless raids.
- **[[Mesokini]]**: The wealthiest and most influential kingdom, thriving on secrecy, diplomacy, and quiet dominance.
- **[[Mesoyi]]**: A quiet and peaceful mountain kingdom that values simplicity, isolation, and communal freedom.
- **[[Neodoulas]]**: An exploitative powerhouse built on forced labor and profit, feared by its neighbors and empowered by trade.
- **[[Orestia]]**: A southern kingdom caught between fading trade and shifting loyalties, walking a fine line between allies.
- **[[Pistos]]**: A faithful and disciplined kingdom, small in size but highly respected for its military and moral clarity.
- **[[Rarus]]**: A secretive kingdom that closed its borders after the Schism Wars, shrouded in silence and mystery.
- **[[Vasae]]**: A rich and marshy trade kingdom tested by piracy, refugees, and rising regional tensions.
