**Mission Brief**

**Background:**  
Recent intelligence from Western Siar'Morrin indicates a troubling increase in dissent within the alliance between Agroma and New Birania, alongside a rise in racially motivated incidents against non-human races, particularly elves. There's also a notable shift in public sentiment away from the Church of Statera. These developments are suspected to be interconnected, although to what end remains unclear. What is clear, however, is the destabilising impact these events are having on New Birania’s expansion efforts and the social and moral fabric of the region.

**Primary Objective:**  
Your mission, ...., is to infiltrate Hu Dao and investigate the potential links between these societal changes. Initial intelligence points to a potential operational base in Hu Dao, possibly connected to a recently uncovered local thieves guild.

**Specific Tasks:**

1. **Thieves Guild Connection:** Investigate the recently uncovered thieves guild in Hu Dao. Determine whether its emergence is merely coincidental with the rising tensions or if it plays a deeper role in the ideological propagation.
2. **Ideological Bases:** Identify any bases or operations within Hu Dao that may be serving as propagation points for the concerning ideologies. Uncover the objectives behind these bases, what end are they working towards?
3. **Local Contact**: Establish contact with our agent embedded within the Adventurers Guild in Hu Dao. This contact serves as a general observer and a communication link to New Birania headquarters. They will attempt to reach out upon your arrival to facilitate your mission.

**Operational Notes:**
- You will operate under the guise of a traveller joining a caravan to Hu Dao. You will need to create a cover that will provide you with the necessary mobility and plausible reasoning for your presence in the area.
- Exercise caution and maintain a low profile. Your role is to gather information and verify the nature of the activities without direct engagement unless absolutely necessary.
- You can report any important findings through our contact in the Adventurers Guild.
