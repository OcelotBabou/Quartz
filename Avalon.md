**Overview:** 

**Avalon** forms the fertile eastern edge of **[[Pilliga]]**, nestled in the foothills of the towering **[[Protoramus Range]]**. Crisscrossed with melt-fed tributaries and underpinned by deep aquifers, it boasts the richest soil in an otherwise harsh and arid land. In earlier ages, Avalon was the beating heart of Pilliga — home to the vast majority of its population and the seat of its agricultural wealth.

Though it still holds considerable influence and remains the stronghold of much of the region’s “old wealth,” Avalon’s dominance is slowly waning. In recent generations, more and more of its people have migrated toward the **[[Fluvitae River]]**, seeking to capitalise on the booming trade that flows along its banks.

**Description:** 

Avalon is a land of golden light and stubborn growth — a rolling expanse of farmland, weather-worn stone terraces, and thriving communities nestled at the feet of the Protoramus Range. From the misty base of the mountains, a web of snowmelt-fed tributaries spills across rich soil, feeding fields, orchards, and pasture in a way unmatched anywhere else in Pilliga.

Unlike most of the region, where only scattered farmsteads can endure the dry climate, Avalon’s steady water supply and fertile ground allow for towns — even cities — of a size unsustainable elsewhere. Its people live in close-knit communities bound by seasonal rhythms, drawing life from deep aquifers, meltwater channels, and the near-constant sunshine that fuels their crops.

Yet Avalon is not without hardship. Poor snowfall in the highlands can bring drought, turning green fields to dust and pressing its people to the edge. Dust storms from the west and sudden floods from the mountains are ever-present risks. Still, the Riverlands endure — their resilience rooted as deeply as the orchards and grain that have long sustained them.

**History:** 

Avalon’s isolation, separated from the rest of Siar’Morrin by the sweeping Pilligan plains, has long shaped its character. Over generations, this separation fostered a distinct culture — one of hardy, self-reliant people deeply attuned to the land. To outsiders, they may appear rough-spoken or boorish, but among themselves they share a strong camaraderie, quick wit, and a humour born from enduring both hardship and prosperity side by side.

For much of Pilliga’s history, Avalon served as the region’s true heart. Its fertile fields provided the grain, fruit, and livestock that sustained life across the plains, while drovers departed from here to drive herds across vast distances. The Riverlands’ towns and markets became natural gathering points for trade, and over time the wealth generated by this commerce concentrated here, forming families and estates whose influence still lingers.

Yet in recent generations, Avalon’s dominance has begun to wane. The flourishing trade routes along the Fluvitae River now draw people westward, lured by the promise of fresh opportunity and faster fortunes. While Avalon remains a bastion of the “old wealth” and agricultural stability, each year sees more of its younger generation leaving the foothills for the riverbanks, seeking a different kind of future.

**Significance:** 

Avalon remains the agricultural heart of Pilliga, producing the majority of the region’s grain, fruit, and livestock. Its fertile land and stable water supply make it one of the few places in Pilliga capable of sustaining large towns and cities, ensuring its continued relevance despite the growing pull of the Fluvitae River trade. Though its political and economic dominance has lessened, Avalon’s role as the region’s breadbasket — and as the keeper of its “old wealth” — still commands respect.
