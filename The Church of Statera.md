### 1. **Basic Info**

- **Name**: The Church of Statera
- **Symbol**: A balanced scale with a sword at its center.
- **Leader**:  [[Praesul Staterae]] [[]]
- **Headquarters**: [[Vitaurum]]
- **Primary Role**: To worship and disseminate the teachings of Statera, promoting balance and harmony across Siar’Morrin.
- **Notable Traits**: Extensive religious influence, commitment to peace and mediation.

### 2. **Overview**

The Church of Statera is the largest religious institution in Siar’Morrin, dedicated to worshipping Statera, the central deity and creator of all existence. Known for its widespread influence and doctrinal authority, the Church plays a pivotal role in the spiritual and daily lives of its followers. It champions a message of balance and harmony, reflective of its deity’s nature.

### 3. **History**

Founded centuries ago, the Church of Statera was established to unify various belief systems under a single, harmonious doctrine centered around Statera. Over the years, it has grown in power and size, spreading its teachings across the continent. As it expanded, the Church adapted local customs and traditions into its practices, which has sometimes led to the formation of various splinter groups, each interpreting Statera’s message differently, recreating the very issue it sought to rectify.
