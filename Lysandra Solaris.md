### Basic Information

- **Name**: Lysandra Solaris
- **Species**: [[Lux]] Elf
- **Gender**: Female
- **Age**: 2,127 years (one of the eldest surviving Lux elves)
- **Occupation**: Matriarch of [[House Solaris]]; Voice of the Sun, [[Clergy of the Sun God]]
- **Affiliation**: House Solaris, Clergy of the Sun God
- **Home Region**: Dovus, Shimmering Forest

### Physical Description

- **Appearance**:  

Lysandra is a vision of ageless grace, her features delicate yet sculpted with an almost statuesque stillness. Her golden hair, once bright as dawn, has paled to a shimmering silver-gold that glows faintly in sunlight, cascading in long braids adorned with solar rings. Her skin is smooth, luminous, and almost too perfect, with fine lines suggesting not age but timeless wear. Her eyes are radiant amber, glowing faintly as though touched directly by [[Gahrian]]’s light, though many find their unwavering gaze unsettling.

She dresses in flowing robes of white and gold, layered with ceremonial sun-cloth woven with threads of radiant silk. Upon her brow she wears the Crown of Solace, an ancient diadem said to have been blessed by [[Gahrian]] in the earliest ages of Staterum.

- **Height**: 188 cm
- **Weight**: 65 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: A halo-like radiance seems to shimmer faintly around her, most visible in darkness.

### Personality

- **Traits**: Inspirational, serene, visionary, increasingly detached from the mortal perspective.
- **Strengths**: Deep spiritual authority, unmatched oratory that inspires awe and obedience, and a commanding presence that unites Lux elves under her banner.
- **Weaknesses**: Out of touch with the struggles of “short-lived” races; her idealism blinds her to practical realities; refuses to acknowledge decline in House Solaris as anything but “temporary shadows.”
- **Goals and Motivations**: To preserve Lux elven devotion to Gahrian, and to maintain the Solaris legacy as the radiant heart of Lux culture. She seeks to ensure her people remain a beacon of light, even as the world changes beyond her understanding.

### Background

- **Early Life**: Born at the conclusion of the Age of War, Lysandra was raised in a time of prosperity and religious devotion. She was groomed from youth to serve both House Solaris and the Clergy, embodying their dual mission of rule and faith.
- **Education**: Trained as both scholar and priestess, she mastered divine liturgy, rhetoric, and diplomacy. Her understanding of Gahrian’s tenets is said to be unparalleled.
- **Key Events**: Survived the Age of Darkness by anchoring her people’s faith, serving as both matriarch and priestess. She led the rebuilding of Dovus in its aftermath and entwined House Solaris with the Clergy, ensuring survival through devotion.
- **Relationships**: Revered by Lux elves as a near-saint, respected by allies for her wisdom, but regarded with suspicion by other races who see her disdain for mortality as arrogance. Within her family, she is both a caring mother-figure and an impossible shadow to live up to.

### Skills and Abilities

- **Combat Skills**: None in the martial sense — though in her youth she trained with the sun-blades, she has long since set them aside.
- **Technical Skills**: Mastery of diplomacy, divine rhetoric, and ceremonial law.
- **Special Abilities**: She wields a near-limitless reservoir of divine power, able to unleash searing rays of light with one hand while mending all but the most fatal wounds with the other

### Equipment

- **Weapons**: Carries no blade, though in ceremony she bears a sun-staff tipped with crystal flame.
- **Relics & Regalia**: The Crown of Solace and Mantle of Dawnsight, heirlooms of House Solaris.
- **Personal Belongings**: A record-book of her personal reflections on Gahrian’s teachings, written across millennia — its pages are said to glow faintly in the sun.

### Significant Events

- **Major Plot Points**: Her survival through the Age of Darkness and continued leadership has made her an untouchable figurehead. However, her growing detachment from mortality risks alienating younger elves and allies.
- **Important Missions**: Oversees the spiritual guidance of Lux elves, safeguards the Voice of the Clergy, and maintains Solaris prestige within Dovus. She actively resists any suggestion that her family or faith are in decline.

### Quotes

- _“The sun does not falter because clouds obscure it; so too shall Gahrian’s light endure through ages of shadow.”_
