### 2. **Overview**

The Rift Watch is a specialized ranger unit tasked with monitoring and protecting the borders of [[the Western Scar]], a region scarred by the Age of Darkness ([[AoD]]). Their primary mission is to safeguard Siar’Morrin from residual demonic forces and prevent any resurgence of dark activities. Comprised of seasoned rangers and warriors, the Rift Watch is revered for their vigilance, bravery, and expertise in dealing with supernatural threats.

### 3. **History**

Formed in the aftermath of the AoD, the Rift Watch was initially a volunteer militia. Over time, it evolved into a formal ranger corps with specialized training to combat demonic entities and disrupt dark cultic activities. Their relentless dedication has been crucial in maintaining the fragile peace along the scar's volatile borders.

### 4. **Leadership and Structure**

The Rift Watch operates under the command of a Watch Captain, a position elected by the senior members of the watch based on merit and experience. Below the Watch Captain, the command is decentralized, with commanders leading small, agile teams responsible for different sectors of the Western Scar. This structure allows for rapid response and adaptability to threats.

### 5. **Culture and Beliefs**

Members of the Rift Watch are bound by a deep-seated commitment to protect their land and its people from the horrors that once emerged from the Western Scar. The Rift Watch is not only bound by their duty to protect but also by a deep spiritual commitment to [[Clypeus]], the god of protection. They believe that as followers of Clypeus, it is their divine duty to maintain a bulwark against the vices, standing as the god’s earthly representatives in his stead. Their motto, "Vigilance without falter," encapsulates their dedication and relentless watchfulness.
### 6. **Geographical Territory**

The Rift Watch’s jurisdiction spans the entirety of Siar'Morrin, although efforts are primarily focused on the borders of the Western Scar. They maintain a series of outposts and watchtowers along the scar, as well as hidden outpost through out Siar'Morrin to keep a watchful gaze over potential cults.
