## 1. **Overview**

The Aurivane Academy stands as the preeminent centre of arcane education in Staterum. Based in [[Vitaurum]], with campuses in [[Flown]] and [[Cragport]], the Academy is both a university and a research institute, producing generations of accomplished wizards, sorcerers, and scholars.

Its prestige is unmatched: six of the current Magus Incantare hail from its halls, and most noble families with an interest in magic send their heirs here to study. Within its walls, the pursuit of magical excellence is considered both an intellectual duty and a noble tradition.

- **Type:** Arcane Institute
- **Base of Operations:** Vitaurum (primary campus)
- **Core Purpose/Focus:** Education, research, and cultivation of the next generation of arcanists.
- **Reputation:** Prestigious, elitist, and deeply influential among nobility and scholars alike.
- **Symbol:** An open tome suspended within a seven-pointed star.

## 2. **History**

The Academy claims ancient origins, with records suggesting multiple iterations of arcane universities stretching back millennia. These institutions rose, fell, and were rebuilt, often destroyed during wars or by the collapse of patron kingdoms.

The modern Academy was rebuilt following the Age of Darkness ([[AoD]]), reconstructed almost entirely from fragmentary records and traditions of its predecessors. Though some question its claim of continuity with the “ancient Academy,” its rapid ascent to prominence—and its record of producing great magi—has silenced most critics.

## 3. **Structure & Organization**

The Academy mirrors a university, blending academic hierarchy with arcane tradition:

- **The Archmagister:** The ceremonial head of the Academy, elected from among the senior faculty.
- **The Convocation:** A council of senior professors who govern the institution collectively, each representing a discipline of arcane study (Evocation, Transmutation, Illusion, etc.).
- **Professors & Masters:** Educators and researchers, often celebrated figures in their field. Many remain at the Academy long after graduation to pursue private research under its name.
- **Students:** Divided into ranks by achievement: _Initiates, Adepts, Magisters,_ and _Graduates_. Advancement requires rigorous examinations and public demonstrations of skill.

## 4. **Culture & Practices**

Life at the Academy is rigorous and highly competitive. Students are expected to devote themselves entirely to study, research, and the disciplined pursuit of magical mastery. Traditions abound, from initiation duels to mark a student’s acceptance, to graduation trials performed before faculty and peers.

The Academy is also a hub of arcane research. Many graduates choose to remain as resident scholars, conducting experiments, developing new spells, or authoring treatises under the Academy’s name. Their collective output is one of the largest and most respected bodies of magical theory in Staterum.

Critics, however, view the Academy as elitist and overly entangled with noble families. The emphasis on prestige and patronage means that, while talent is valued, wealth and lineage often open doors more quickly than raw ability.

## 5. **Key Figures**

- **Archmagister Selvar Dane** – Current head of the Collegium, known more for his diplomacy with noble houses than his arcane prowess.
- **Professor Rhael Tinnovar** – Illusionist of great renown, whose radical theories have earned both accolades and accusations of heresy.
- **Magistra Olyenne Crestfall** – Strict head of Evocation, famed for her demanding standards and her role in training three of the current Magus Incantare.

## 6. **Geographic Influence**

- **Areas of Operation:** Students and graduates of the Collegium can be found across Siar’Morrin, serving as court wizards, advisors, and researchers.
- **Allies & Enemies:**
    - **Rival:** The **Elementum Arcanum**, whose militaristic origins and pragmatic focus clash with the Collegium’s academic elitism.

## 7. **Resources & Capabilities**

- **Military Strength:** None formally, though in times of crisis its professors and graduates are among the most skilled battlemages in Staterum.
- **Economic Power:** Significant, funded by noble tithes, student tuition, and royal patronage.
- **Special Assets:** Vast libraries of arcane theory, a network of alumni embedded in courts and guilds across Staterum, and the prestige of producing Magus Incantare.
