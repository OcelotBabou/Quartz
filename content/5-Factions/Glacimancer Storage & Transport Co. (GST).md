# 1. **Overview**

The Glacimancer Storage & Transport Co. (GST) is a rapidly expanding corporation that dominates the storage and transportation of goods across Siar’Morrin. Harnessing proprietary ice-based magics, GST has revolutionised the preservation of food, alchemical supplies, and perishable goods, making them indispensable to kingdoms and merchants alike. Their tight grip on these arcane techniques, coupled with aggressive expansion, has secured them both immense wealth and influence.

- **Type:** Business
- **Base of Operations:** Flown, Agroma
- **Core Purpose/Focus:** Magical storage and large-scale distribution of food and supplies across Siar’Morrin.
- **Reputation:** Respected for efficiency and innovation, but often viewed warily for monopolistic potential and considered a drain on resources by others. 
- **Symbol:** A crystalline snowflake encased in a shipping crate

## 2. **History**

The origins of the Glacimancer Storage & Transport Co. lie in crisis. When the trade routes through the [[Shadow Aisle Mountains]] were closed, commerce between eastern and western Siar’Morrin suffered immensely. The alternative paths were long and arduous, passing through some of the continent’s hottest regions. Spoilage of food and supplies became rampant, threatening the stability of markets and even the survival of communities dependent on long-distance trade from Agroma.

It was during this time that Gunnar Frostbreath, an entrepreneurial sorcerer from the [[Arctic Expanse]] saw an opportunity. Applying his mastery of ice magics, and techniques long utilised in Northern Siar'Morrin, he introduced the art of cold storage to merchants travelling [[Pilliga]]. This breakthrough quickly drew the attention of Flown’s merchant elite, who recognised its potential to restore the lifeblood of continental trade.

From these beginnings, the Glacimancer Storage & Transport Co. (GST) was born. What started as a niche service quickly evolved into a dominant enterprise, scaling up to serve not just merchants but entire kingdoms. Over the last two decades, GST has expanded aggressively, building depots and distribution hubs in every major city willing to host them. Their monopoly is enforced not only by their wealth but also by their secrecy, as the company’s preservation techniques remain locked within the guarded knowledge of its senior glacimancers.

## 3. **Structure & Organization**

GST operates as a corporate hierarchy, balancing arcane secrecy with merchant pragmatism to protect its trade secrets while maintaining efficient operations.

- **Elder Spokesperson:** he charismatic public face of the company, tasked with representing GST in politics, trade negotiations, and public discourse. This role is currently held by [[Elena Frostbreathe]], whose poise and diplomacy have elevated GST’s reputation.
- **Elder Council:** A closed circle of representatives drawn from the tribes of the Arctic Expanse. They oversee the company’s direction, safeguard its proprietary magic, and ensure that the profits and power of GST remain tied to their homeland.
- **Regional Directors:** Senior officials appointed to oversee the operation of GST’s distribution hubs in major cities. They are responsible for recruitment, local trade negotiations, and ensuring smooth logistical flow across their jurisdictions.
- **Field Agents & Crews:** The workforce responsible for the day-to-day business of GST—loading caravans, maintaining storage wards, and escorting shipments. Recruited almost exclusively from the Arctic Expanse, their loyalty is enforced by both cultural ties and the promise of prosperity, ensuring the company’s magical secrets remain closely guarded.

## 4. **Culture & Practices**

The culture of GST reflects the traditions of the Arctic Expanse, from which most of its workforce hails. Members of GST are renowned as paragons of work ethic. Crews are expected to work relentlessly until a task is completed—be it fighting the elements in a caravan trekking across a desert or maintaining the vast storage wards of [[Vitaurum]]. Yet when the work is done, they celebrate with the loud, exuberant revelry of the northern tribes, feasts and songs ringing long into the night. To outsiders, these displays often appear unruly, but within the company they are a point of pride, reinforcing bonds of kinship and accomplishment.

A central element of GST’s culture is its religious devotion, though this aspect is rarely spoken of to outsiders. Of particular importance is the Winter Solstice, during which the vast majority of the company undertakes a pilgrimage back to the Arctic Expanse. Operations are left in the hands of a skeleton staff, who are compensated generously for holding the line, while those who return north take part in sacred rites that reaffirm their bond to both homeland and faith.

This migration is one of the few times GST’s relentless momentum slows, creating a predictable gap in its otherwise seamless operations. Aware of the strain this places on the company’s growing network, Elena Frostbreathe has become determined to find a solution

## 6. **Geographic Influence**

- **Areas of Operation:** Based in Flown, with a growing network of storage and distribution hubs across Siar’Morrin. Current locations of note include:
	- [[Vitaurum]]: Set to become the central distribution hub of the continent, where goods arrive before being redirected to smaller cities and towns.
	- [[Westrun]]: Where Gunnar first introduced the concept of glacimancy to southern Siar'Morrin, and a major foothold along the East-West trade route.  
- **Allies & Enemies:**
    - **Rival:** Lord Farmus, Operates the largest shipping fleet along the [[Fluvitae River]]. Though he currently cooperates with GST, he makes little effort to hide his ambition to uncover the secrets of glacimancy and free himself from reliance on their monopoly. 

## 7. **Resources & Capabilities**

- **Military Strength:** GST maintains no standing army, but its workforce is composed of some of the continent’s most accomplished glacimancers. In times of crisis, their command of frost and preservation magics can be turned from commerce to defense, making them a deceptively formidable force. 
- **Economic Power:** Though still relatively new on the continental stage, GST’s wealth is growing at what many consider an alarming pace. Their monopoly on preservation and transport gives them leverage over markets and kingdoms alike, and if their growth continues unchecked, they are poised to become one of the wealthiest corporations in Siar’Morrin.  
- **Special Assets:** The company’s true power lies in its arcane innovations and infrastructure. GST’s proprietary glacimancy wards remain a tightly guarded secret, ensuring their monopoly. Their expanding network of fortified storage depots, caravan hubs, and distribution centers gives them reach across the continent.
