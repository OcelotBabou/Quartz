**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Jack
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 30
- **Occupation**: Bartender
- **Affiliation**: The Drunken Weasel
- **Home Region**: Hu Dao

**Appearance**

Jack presents himself with an air of someone who has stumbled into a wardrobe of fine clothing without the slightest clue on how to wear it properly. His suits, although expensive, often fit poorly—either too tight or too loose in all the wrong place. His hair, which he attempts to style in a manner befitting an intellectual, often ends up tousled as if he ran his fingers through it one too many times during a deep thought.

**Background**

Jack sees himself as the brains of the trio at The Drunken Weasel, although reality often tells a different story. He is 30 years old and fancies himself a thinker and philosopher, engaging—or more accurately, pestering—patrons with his attempts at 'smart' conversations. These interactions, while meant to be intellectual, often end up being one-sided as Jack's understanding of deeper topics can be quite superficial.

Despite his self-assured intellectual demeanor, Jack is notably gullible and particularly susceptible to flattery, which patrons and his brothers alike use to their advantage to sway him easily. 

While he and Jacob are both tasked with managing the bar front, Jack frequently shirks his responsibilities, leaving Jacob to handle most of the actual work. Jack’s distraction with engaging customers in conversation means that he often misses the finer points of bar management.
