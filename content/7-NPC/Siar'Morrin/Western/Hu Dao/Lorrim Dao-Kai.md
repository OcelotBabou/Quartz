**Appearance**

Lorrim Dao-Kai presents the visage of a life devoted to the soil—her skin bears the marks and lines of long days under the sun, and her hands are those of a true laborer, calloused and strong. Her hair, once a vibrant shade, now shows streaks of grey, which she wears with a dignified grace that speaks to her acceptance of aging as an inevitable, respected process. Typically clad in simple, practical attire, she favors garments that speak to both her status and her pragmatic nature; robust fabrics that can withstand the rigors of both fieldwork and formal gatherings.

**Background**

From what you have heard en route to Hu Dao, Lorrim Dao-Kai is highly respected as the Triarch of Cultivation, a position she has earned through a lifetime of dedication to the agricultural prosperity of Hu Dao. As a devout follower of Vilcren and an active worshipper within the Ascended Church, her faith deeply influences her approach to her duties. Known for her diligence and a no-nonsense attitude toward her work, Lorrim has always been more focused on practical outcomes than ceremonial displays. Of the three Triarchs, she appears to be the least enthusiastic about the upcoming festival. She believes that while the celebrations draw attention and visitors, the focus should instead be on practical matters, particularly in guiding the elves of the region away from their traditional practices towards the more progressive teachings of the New Gods, who she believes are more attuned to the current needs of the mortals of Staterum. Her stance is pragmatic yet controversial, garnering quiet support among the human populations of Hu Dao, while simultaneously facing stark opposition from the elven communities.
