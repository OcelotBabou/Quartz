**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Eugene Hoomin
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 17
- **Occupation**: Apprentice and Temporary Shop Manager
- **Affiliation**: Feng's Fantastical Freight
- **Home Region**: [[Hu Dao]]

**Appearance**

Eugene Hoomin is a slender teenager with a nerdy demeanor that is as apparent in his appearance as it is in his mannerisms. He is slightly awkward in stature, with a weedy build that does little to fill out his oversized shop apron. His hair is unkempt, often falling into his eyes as he pores over inventory lists or ancient catalogs left by his mentor. Eugene's glasses are perpetually slipping down his nose, adding to his distracted apperance. Despite the shop’s state of disarray, Eugene tries to maintain a semblance of order, at least in his personal presentation, though his efforts often come across as endearingly hapless.

**Background**

Feng's Fantastical Freight was once known for its impressive stock and ability to source rare items, largely thanks to the expertise of its owner, Feng Duan. However, since Feng's mysterious disappearance a year ago, his young apprentice Eugene has been left to manage the shop. Initially, Eugene tackled his new responsibilities with enthusiasm, determined to uphold the shop's reputation. However, as time has passed, the challenges of running such a specialized business without Feng's guidance have begun to overwhelm him.

The shop, once bustling and vibrant, is now a shadow of its former self. Many shelves lay bare, and dust is gathering on once meticulously maintained displays. Eugene is struggling to keep the business afloat, and the shop’s dwindling stock and waning reputation weigh heavily on his shoulders. Despite his best efforts, he is painfully aware of his limitations and the growing disarray around him.
