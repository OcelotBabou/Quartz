**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: 

Brialla has a poised and composed demeanor, with a gentle face that reflects both kindness and resilience. Her auburn hair, rich and lustrous, is typically styled in an updo that combines functionality with a touch of sophistication, ensuring it never impedes her duties while still framing her face beautifully.

Her eyes, a deep shade of hazel, are expressive and radiate warmth, often lighting up with laughter or narrowing in concentration as she oversees the troupe’s needs. Brialla’s skin is smooth and fair, dusted with a hint of freckles across her cheeks. 

Brialla’s wardrobe is carefully curated to balance aesthetics with practicality. She favors flowing blouses and skirts that allow for ease of movement, often in muted earth tones. For performances or special occasions, she might choose fabrics with subtle embroidery or beading, adding just enough embellishment to signify her role without overshadowing the performers. Accessories are minimal but meaningful, such as her cherished silver locket which rests near her heart, a gift from [[Faelar Silversong]]. 
- **Height**: 170 cm
- **Weight**: 58 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: Brialla often wears a silver locket, a gift from Faelar, which she treasures as a symbol of their partnership both in life and work.

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Maternal, perceptive, and patient
- **Strengths**: Strong organizational skills, excellent at multitasking, great listener
- **Weaknesses**: Sometimes overly cautious, can be too self-sacrificing
- **Goals and Motivations**: To ensure the troupe's success and stability.

**Background**

Brialla exudes a calm and nurturing presence, often serving as the emotional anchor for the troupe. Her approach to managing the troupe's daily operations is characterized by a blend of motherly care and meticulous organization, ensuring everyone feels supported and heard. Brialla's demeanor is patient and attentive, allowing her to manage the diverse personalities within the troupe effectively.

As the creative director and manager of the troupe, Brialla views her role as pivotal not just to the success of their performances but to the well-being of every member. She finds deep satisfaction in the seamless execution of a show, where every element from logistics to performance comes together perfectly. As the troupe approaches Hu Dao for their most anticipated performance yet, Brialla’s feelings are a mix of excitement and nervousness. The festival in Hu Dao represents a significant milestone — it will be the largest audience the troupe has ever faced. Success here could catapult their reputation to new heights, bringing more opportunities and greater challenges. Brialla is keenly aware that the scale of this event will test her skills like never before.

Brialla's relationship with her husband, Faelar, is foundational to her life and work. She balances his charismatic exuberance with her steady pragmatism. Brialla was immediately drawn to [[Tiana Illuminor]]'s youthful innocence, feeling a natural, maternal pull towards her. This connection has allowed her to provide Tiana with both guidance and emotional support, despite being nearly a decade younger than her.
