**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Lieve Moonsight
- **Species**: Lux Elf
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 83
- **Occupation**: Guard
- **Affiliation**: Unknown
- **Home Region**: Dusk Forest

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: 

Lieve embodies the elusive grace and mystique of the Lux Elves, his appearance as haunting as the shadowed woods he calls home. Lieve’s skin is a deep shade of grey, almost like the twilight hues of the Dusk Forest, seamlessly blending into the shadows. His hair, pitch black and silky, is always meticulously gathered into a tight bun atop his head, emphasizing the sharp contours of his face and his piercing, ever watchful eyes.

His facial features are sharply defined, with high cheekbones and a straight, narrow nose, typical of his elven heritage. Lieve's expression is habitually serious, loosing only when around [[Bradbury Raturian]]. His eyes, a striking shade of silver, seem to reflect the light of the moon, the only thing visible as he stalks the shadows on patrol at night. He dresses primarily in dark, form-fitting clothes that help him camouflage within the forest when out scouting. 
- **Height**: 180 cm
- **Weight**: 70 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: His attire is consistently dark, tailored to blend seamlessly with the forest when he is out scouting. The seriousness of his demeanor and the intensity of his gaze often set him apart in any gathering.

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Observant, reserved, and intensely focused
- **Strengths**: Excellent scout and tracker, skilled in stealth and combat
- **Weaknesses**: His reserved nature can make him seem aloof or detached, potentially hindering deeper relationships with others besides Bradbury.
- **Goals and Motivations**: Currently to ensure the safe transport of his caravan to [[Hu Dao]]

**Background**

Lieve Moonsight's story within the caravan has largely been unveiled thanks to his companion, Bradbury, who speaks of him with a mix of admiration and brotherly affection. The duo have worked together for the past eight years, a partnership that began under dramatic circumstances when Lieve saved a brash, younger Bradbury from a perilous misadventure in the Dusk Forest. 

Bradbury often assures others that beneath Lieve's reserved exterior lies a warm and friendly spirit, suggesting that once the pressures of their current journey ease upon their arrival in Hu Dao, Lieve might show a more relaxed side of his personality. Currently, his interactions within the caravan are notably sparse; he communicates effectively but minimally, choosing to spend most of his conversational efforts with Bradbury. However, he is occasionally seen engaging with the elf clerics, Elborn and Tiana, with whom he shares a tacit understanding and respect. Contrastingly, Lieve's interactions with the human merchants, especially Miranda Fleet, are marked by visible disdain.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Skilled archer, proficient with dual knives for close combat
- **Technical Skills**: Skilled in stealth and scouting
- **Special Abilities**: Enhanced night vision typical of Lux Elves, allowing him superior scouting capabilities in low-light conditions

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: A finely crafted longbow and a pair of slender, razor-sharp knives
- **Gadgets**: Small snares and traps, useful in hunting and gathering. 
- **Personal Belongings**: Unknown
