**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Eldrin Starwatcher
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 73 years
- **Occupation**: Magus Incantare
- **Affiliation**: Magisterium 
- **Home Region**: [[Dahri Empire]]

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Eldrin is an elderly wizard with a dignified bearing. His long, white hair flows like a waterfall, and his beard reaches down to his chest, both speckled with silvery strands. His eyes are a deep, reflective silver, mirroring the depths of his arcane knowledge. He typically wears robes of twilight blue scattered under an ornate cloak representative of his Dahri heritage.
- **Height**: 175 cm 
- **Weight**: 68 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: Carries an ancient staff crowned with a crystal orb that pulsates with a soft light. His hands, marked with intricate sigils, glow faintly when he performs divination.

![[Eldrin.jpg]]


**Personality**

- **Traits**: Wise, contemplative, and enigmatic.
- **Strengths**: Unparalleled expertise in divination magic, exceptional foresight, and the ability to decipher the most cryptic of prophecies.
- **Weaknesses**: His deep immersion in futures and possibilities sometimes detaches him from present realities. He can be overly cryptic, frustrating those who seek direct answers.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To guide the destiny of Siar’Morrin through his visions and to prevent calamities foreseen in his divinations.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Born under a prophesied comet, Eldrin was destined for the path of divination. However, born into the secular Dahri Empire, did not begin his journey into the arcane till later in his life. 
- **Education**: Educated in the Dahri Empire, Eldrin was schooled comprehensively in matters of the material world. This topic were of little interest to Eldrin, and as a young man he sailed to [[Vitaurum]] to pursue his interests in the arcane. 
- **Key Events**: Eldrin's reputation as a seer grew as he began to make accurate predictions that saved Vitaurum from several potential calamities. His abilities eventually earned him the title of Divine Magus, placing him at the forefront of the arcane community in Siar’Morrin.
- **Relationships**: (names of all Magisterium). He is also good friends with [[Ronan Stormsinger]], both sharing exceptional connection to the Gods. 

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: While not a combatant, he can use defensive spells to protect himself and his allies.
- **Technical Skills**: Master of arcane lore, especially in the interpretation of dreams and omens.
- **Special Abilities**: Can cast divination spells that allow him to peer into the future, read minds, and locate hidden objects or persons.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Rarely uses any, but his staff can channel powerful protective spells.
- **Gadgets**: Utilizes various magical artifacts and relics to enhance his divinatory powers.
- **Personal Belongings**: Keeps a personal library of prophetic books and rare manuscripts.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: His visions have directly influenced major decisions and policies in Siar’Morrin, shaping the realm's approach to forthcoming threats and opportunities. 
- **Important Missions**: In a significant shift from his active role in the arcane circles of Vitaurum, Eldrin has recently retreated to the quieter, more isolated region of [[Portum]]. His withdrawal from the bustling areas of Siar’Morrin was driven by a growing disillusionment with the way religious leadership was evolving. Eldrin observed an increasing trend where religious influence was being wielded as a tool for personal and political gain, rather than for spiritual enlightenment or the betterment of society.

**Quotes**

"In the patterns of stars and the whispers of the wind, the future unfolds its secrets to those who dare to look."
