**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Grimtooth the Raider
- **Species**: Gnoll
- **Gender**: Female
- **Age**: 38 years
- **Occupation**: Warlord 
- **Affiliation**: Nighthowl Clan
- **Home Region**: Donatus Stultitia

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Grimtooth is a towering figure even among gnolls, standing over seven feet tall with a heavily muscled build that intimidates friend and foe alike. Her fur is a deep black with streaks of gray, and her face bears the scars of many battles, with a notably missing left ear and a set of sharp, well-maintained teeth.
- **Height**: 220 cm 
- **Weight**: 110 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: Her missing left ear and the custom armor she wears, which is adorned with the bones and teeth of fallen enemies.

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Aggressive, cunning, and fiercely loyal to her clan.
- **Strengths**: Exceptional combat skills, strategic mind for warfare, and an unyielding strength in battle.
- **Weaknesses**: Her aggressive nature can sometimes lead to recklessness; she also has a deep-seated disdain for diplomacy, preferring to solve problems through force.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To raid an expand her clans territories into Agroma.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Born into the harsh environment of the Donatus Mirage Plains, Grimtooth was raised amidst conflict and survival of the fittest. Her prowess in combat was evident from a young age, leading her quickly up the ranks within her tribe.
- **Education**: Mentored by the previous warlord of the Nighthowl Clan, she learned not only the arts of combat but also the strategic elements of leading raids and managing warriors.
- **Key Events**: Rose to prominence after leading a series of successful raids against neighboring tribes and settlements, significantly bolstering the clan's resources and territory.
- **Relationships**: While feared by many within and outside her clan, Grimtooth commands deep respect and loyalty from her warriors. She is often consulted by [[Thorgash Bloodfang]] for military strategies leading into large scale raids.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Master of melee combat, proficient with a variety of weapons, particularly the great axe and spiked mace.
- **Technical Skills**: Skilled in battlefield tactics and siege warfare.
- **Special Abilities**: Known for her ability to rally her troops and instill fear in her enemies, sometimes attributed to a mystical aura of intimidation.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Wields a massive great axe known as "Bonecleaver" and carries a spiked mace as a secondary weapon.
- **Gadgets**: Wears heavy, custom-made armor that offers significant protection and intimidation factor.
- **Personal Belongings**: Keeps trophies from her raids, which she uses to decorate her armor and living quarters.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Her leadership in raids has not only expanded the clan’s territories but also instilled a sense of invincibility among her warriors, strengthening the clan's position in the region.
- **Important Missions**: Currently focused on planning a large-scale assault on Merweld City.

**Quotes**

"Our ancestors' spirits howl with the winds of the plains—listen, and you will hear their call to battle."
