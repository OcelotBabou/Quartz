### Basic Information

- **Name**: Sienna Galt
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Female
- **Age**: 19 years
- **Occupation**: Viceroy of Abdurii
- **Affiliation**: New Biranian Empire
- **Home Region**: New Birania

### Physical Description

- **Appearance**: Sienna Galt possesses a slight and graceful build, which belies the strength of her resolve and leadership. Her features are strikingly sharp and symmetrical, framed by long, dark hair that she often wears in a practical ponytail, allowing her keen, observant eyes to stand out prominently. Her complexion is fair, and her expression is typically composed, reflecting her thoughtful and analytical approach to life. Her unconventional beauty, characterized by a certain scholarly charm, makes her memorable to those she encounters.
- **Height**: 165 cm (5'5")
- **Weight**: 50 kg (110 lbs)
- **Distinguishing Features**: Notable for her rounded reading glasses.

### Personality

- **Traits**: Determined, resilient, and insightful beyond her years.
- **Strengths**: Quick-witted and politically astute; capable of navigating complex governance challenges despite her youth.
- **Weaknesses**: Sometimes perceived as inexperienced due to her age, which challenges her authority. Has a bit of an ego, due to unchecked success thus far.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To protect Abdurri and its surrounding lands in the face of ever growing threats. 

### Background

- **Early Life**: Orphaned at an early age, Sienna found refugee in the Codex Library of Abdurii, where she worked tirelessly in exchange for shelter. When she was still young she was discovered working within the library by King Alaric, who took her in as an adopted daughter. 
- **Education**: Mentored by local scholars in the Codex Library and eventually sponsored by King Alaric for formal education at Mercatura Academy.
- **Key Events**: Rose rapidly through the ranks due to her remarkable intellect and leadership qualities, appointed as Viceroy of Abdurii at an unprecedentedly young age.
- **Relationships**: Seen as a surrogate daughter by King [[Alaric Terra]], who has taken a personal interest in her development and career. Sienna and [[Caius Terra]] have a deep sibling-like bond that remains strong into adulthood. Sienna also views [[Harold Virent]] as a relic needing replacement for effective leadership of Zakrada.

### Skills and Abilities

- **Combat Skills**: Skilled with a rapier, yet lacks actual combat experience
- **Technical Skills**: Proficient in administrative management, legal affairs, and public policy.
- **Special Abilities**: Possesses a natural charisma that, despite her youth, can sway public and private opinions in her favor.

### Equipment

- **Weapons**: Carries the rapier "," a gift from her adoptive father King Alaric. 
- **Gadgets**: Utilizes advanced communication devices to manage administrative duties effectively.
- **Personal Belongings**: Always carries a token from the Codex Library—a small, antique bookmark given to her by the head librarian when she left with King Alaric. She also always wears a signet ring, symbolizing her connection to the Terra family. 

### Significant Events

- **Major Plot Points**: Sienna Galt was appointed as the Viceroy of Abdurii at an unprecedentedly young age, earning widespread recognition for her competence and strategic acumen. Under her leadership, Abdurii has developed robust defenses and innovative strategies to handle the myriad challenges it faces, from political tensions to external threats. Her effective governance and proactive measures have significantly bolstered the region's stability, enhancing her reputation as a capable and forward-thinking leader.
- **Important Missions**: Sienna is dedicated to restoring stability to Abdurii and its neighboring areas, focusing on mitigating the multitude of threats that jeopardize peace. Her strategic initiatives aim to neutralize these challenges, whether through diplomatic means or decisive action, to ensure a secure and prosperous future for her region.

### Quotes

- "Youth is not a liability but a reservoir of potential; it is our duty to harness it wisely."
