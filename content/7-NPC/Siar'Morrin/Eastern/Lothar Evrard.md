### Basic Information

- **Name**: Lothar Evrard
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 43 years
- **Occupation**: Monarch 
- **Affiliation**: Kingdom of Orestia
- **Home Region**: [[Orestia]]

### Physical Description

- **Appearance**: ·         Lothar is a man whose illness has aged him beyond his years. His once robust frame has become frail, and his pale complexion highlights the physical toll of his condition. His hair, though still thick, is greying prematurely, and deep lines of fatigue mar his face.
- **Height**: 175 cm 
- **Weight**: 60 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: His gaunt appearance and often fatigued expression are notable, reflecting his severe health struggles.

### Personality

- **Traits**: Thoughtful yet indecisive, Lothar is a ruler who genuinely cares for his people but struggles with the burden of leadership due to his illness.
- **Strengths**: Compassionate, well-intentioned, and earnest in his desire to see his kingdom prosper.
- **Weaknesses**: His chronic illness and indecision have compromised his effectiveness as a leader, leading to stagnation and frustration within his kingdom.
- **Goals and Motivations**: Lothar wishes to unify his kingdom and ensure a peaceful and prosperous future for his people, but his deteriorating health severely hampers these efforts.

### Background

- **Early Life**: Born into the royal family of Orestia, Lothar was destined for leadership from birth. He showed promise as a thoughtful and caring leader but was thrust into kingship unexpectedly early due to the untimely death of his parents in a tragic accident while travelling to Mesokini.
- **Education**: Received a comprehensive private education in governance, diplomacy, and military strategy, tailored to prepare him for his future role as king.
- **Key Events**: His reign has been marked by his struggle with a debilitating illness that has left him unable to decisively lead, causing significant political and social stagnation within Orestia.
- **Relationships**: His reign has strained many relationships within the royal court and among the citizenry, who grow increasingly discontented with the lack of clear direction and progress.

### Skills and Abilities

- **Combat Skills**: Limited by his illness, Lothar has not been able to maintain any significant martial prowess.
- **Technical Skills**: Knowledgeable in governance and diplomacy but often too ill to effectively apply his skills.
- **Special Abilities**: None beyond his royal authority.

### Equipment

- **Weapons**: None, as his physical condition does not allow him to bear arms.
- **Gadgets**: Relies on various medicinal concoctions and treatments to manage his illness.
- **Personal Belongings**: Carries a royal signet that symbolizes his authority but also serves as a reminder of the burdens of his crown.

### Significant Events

- **Major Plot Points**: Lothar’s reign is a period of growing discontent as his illness prevents him from addressing the pressing issues facing Orestia.
- **Important Missions**: His primary mission remains to stabilise and unify his kingdom, though his efforts are severely hampered by his health challenges.
