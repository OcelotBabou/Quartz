*[Mál-kard Té-rah]*

**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Malcard Terra
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 34 years
- **Occupation**: Co-Ruler of Pistos
- **Affiliation**: Diarchy of Pistos
- **Home Region**: [[Pistos]]

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Malcard possesses a lean and contemplative demeanor, his face marked by the thoughtful intensity of a seasoned strategist. His dark eyes are often shadowed by the weight of leadership, reflecting a depth of experience beyond his years. His posture is straight and commanding, hinting at an underlying strength despite his slender frame.
- **Height**: 188 cm
- **Weight**: 78 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: Heavy bags under his eyes are a testament to many sleepless nights spent strategizing, and his tall, slim build belies a surprising physical strength.

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Analytical, reserved, strategic.
- **Strengths**: Excellent at long-term planning and crisis management, adept at navigating the complex political landscape of the Shattered Kingdoms.
- **Weaknesses**: His reserved nature sometimes comes off as aloof or distant, making personal connections challenging.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To uphold the stability and prosperity of Pistos through thoughtful governance and strategic alliances.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Born into the ruling Terra family of Pistos, Malcard was sent to live in [[Vitaurum]] with his distant relative [[Albus Terra]].
- **Education**: Educated both in Vitaurum, attending both Fortis Academy and Marcatura academy. Although capable in combat, Malcrds strengths shone in more academic pursuits. 
- **Key Events**: Ascended to co-rulership after the death of his father, establishing the diarchy with his sister to ensure balanced governance.
- **Relationships**: Has a complex but supportive relationship with his sister, Seraphina, balancing her diplomatic approach with his strategic mindset.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Trained in the art of war, Malcard is skilled in both personal combat and military strategy.
- **Technical Skills**: Proficient in political negotiation and economic strategy, with a keen understanding of the geopolitical landscape.
- **Special Abilities**: Known for his ability to anticipate and counteract political maneuvers from rivals, almost as if he can predict their moves ahead of time.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Carries a ceremonial sword that signifies his status, however, prefers to rely on his magical abilities for defence.
- **Gadgets**: Channels his arcane powers through a relic amulet.
- **Personal Belongings**: His amulet, a family heirloom dating back to when his family still resided in Vitaurum. 

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Malcard's strategic leadership has been crucial in preserving Pistos' independence and integrity amid the ongoing political upheaval in the Shattered Kingdoms. His meticulous approach has been instrumental in shaping a well-organised and effective military defence for the kingdom. 
- **Important Missions**: Malcard is deeply engaged in diplomatic endeavors aimed at fortifying alliances and navigating the complex power dynamics among the Shattered Kingdoms. While his sister concentrates on resolving the conflict between [[Vesae]] and [[Avatolika]], Malcard dedicates his efforts to exposing and countering the schemes of [[Mesokini]].

**Quotes**

"Strategy is not just the art of planning; it's the craft of adapting plans to unfolding realities."
