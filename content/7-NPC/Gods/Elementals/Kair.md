## Basic Information

- **Name**: Kair
- **Domain**: Mountains, Peaks
- **Title**: The Mountain King, Envoy of Mountains
- **Alignment**: True Neutral
- **Symbol**: A towering mountain peak with a winding path
- **Primary Worship Area**: Mountain ranges, high altitudes, and places where the majesty of the mountains is revered

### Physical Description

- **Appearance**: 

Kair, known as the Mountain King, stands as a colossal figure whose very presence evokes the timeless and majestic essence of mountain ranges. His towering form is crafted from the minerals of the earth itself—rugged stone forms his body, each muscle and curve resembling the sheer cliffs and craggy landscapes of high peaks. His skin is a tapestry of rough granite interspersed with veins of sparkling quartz, and patches of snow and ice cling to him, highlighting the crevices and contours like the snow caps that adorn the highest summits.

His eyes, deep and ice-blue, mirror the clear, piercing skies that hang above the tallest mountains. They reflect a sharp, crystalline light, reminiscent of sunlight glinting off icy peaks. His gaze is both awe-inspiring and daunting, capturing the solitary and pristine beauty of the high altitudes.

Kair’s hair is a wild, flowing mane of snow and ice, ever-shifting and shimmering with a life of its own. It cascades around his shoulders like an avalanche, sparkling with frozen droplets that catch the light with every movement. Surrounding him, a perpetual mist and thin clouds form a cloak that billows and swirls, mimicking the mountain winds that sweep through rocky passes and over sharp ridges.
- **Height**: 500m 
- **Weight**: Unknown
- **Distinguishing Features**: His most notable features besides his towering height,  are his ice-blue eyes, and his hair made of ice and snow. 

## Personality

- **Traits**: Austere, solitary, stoic, and exacting. 
- **Strengths**: Immense strength and fortitude, said to be immovable. His stubbornness bolsters this, and his will is utterly unbendable.
- **Weaknesses**: Inflexibility and isolation. Kair’s judgments can be as unforgiving as sheer granite—slow to change once set. He struggles to value the swift and the subtle, often underestimating the force of small, persistent changes. 
- **Goals and Motivations**: Kair’s goals are ancient and foreign to the short-lived mortal races. If he still strives toward an end goal, it has long since been lost to time and stone. 

## Relationships

**Family**: 
- **[[Talam]]**: Father. Kair holds a deep respect for Talam, yet he feels that his efforts and contributions are often overlooked, leading to a sense of being undervalued despite his unwavering loyalty.
- **[[Siar]]**: Brother. On the surface, Kair maintains a cordial relationship with Siar, but internally, he harbors feelings of envy. Kair perceives Siar as the favored son, which stings given his own commitments and sacrifices.
- **[[Niphar]] & [[Eilear]]**: Brothers. With Niphar and Eilear, Kair's relationship is more distant and sporadic. He regards them as somewhat lax in their duties, believing they lack the necessary discipline to uphold the gravitas of their father's elemental legacy
- **[[Thear]]**: Brother. Kair shares a strong and positive relationship with Thear, feeling a protective instinct towards his younger brother. He has often acted as a guardian and mentor to Thear, taking pride in his role as the older sibling who helps guide and support him.
- **[[Agra]]**: Sister. His view of Agra is somewhat dismissive. While he harbours no ill will towards her, Kair does not see her on the same level as himself or his other brothers. 
**Rivals/Enemies**: 
- **[[Siar]]**: Though Kair rarely voices his envy, it shapes his cold demeanor and fuels his relentless pursuit of perfection, determined that even the winds must one day bow to the mountains.
- **[[Listunos]]**: Among Kair’s greatest affronts is the corruption of his sacred peaks by the insidious presence of Listunos. Legends tell that Listunos has taken up resident deep within the [[Talam Mountains]], domain of Kair. To Kair, this intrusion is nothing less than a desecration of purity—an infection festering in the heart of stone.
**Attitude towards Mortals**: 

To Kair, mortals are as fleeting as snow upon his slopes—here for a moment, then gone with the next thaw. Their struggles, triumphs, and empires mean little to one who measures time in the slow rise and fall of mountain chains. He neither loves nor hates them; he simply endures, as he always has.

## Mythology and Worship

- **Creation Myth**: 

In the dawn of the world's making, when the earth was still young and malleable, Talam, the Primordial of Earth, sought to impose structure upon the chaotic landmasses that swirled across the globe. To aid him in this monumental task, Talam decided to create progeny who would embody and oversee the various facets of his domain. From the mightiest elements of the earth, Talam forged his first and most powerful son, Kair, destined to become the Mountain King.

Kair was born from the heart of the oldest and most formidable mountains. Talam gathered the essence of granite, the resilience of basalt, and the purity of snow to craft a figure that would personify the strength and permanence of the peaks. As Kair rose from the earth, his form towered above the landscapes, his presence as imposing and majestic as the mountains he was born from.

From the moment of his creation, Kair was instilled with a profound sense of duty. Talam entrusted him with the guardianship of the world's mountain ranges, from the soaring heights where the eagles nest to the deepest caves where ancient secrets lay hidden. Kair worked alongside his father, shaping the peaks and carving the valleys that would define the landscapes of Staterum. With each ridge he formed and each gorge he etched, Kair imbued the earth with a sense of permanence and stability.
- **Major Cults and Religious Practices**: As a largely removed god, there is little active devotion to Kair. Most mortals see him as an indifferent guardian rather than an intercessor, a presence to be respected rather than revered. Only a handful of remote mountain communities along the Talam Range maintain small shrines or stone-hewn temples in his honour
- **Festivals**: Among the Goliaths who dwell along the eastern edges of **[[Nordholt]]**, one sacred festival honoring Kair endures: the Ascent of Stones. Held upon the death of a clan leader, this trial sees the strongest Goliaths carry the heaviest boulders they can bear to the summit of Mount Sumaenth. The climb is a grueling test of endurance, strength, and will—qualities Kair himself embodies. Those who reach the peak and lay their stone upon the summit cairn are said to have earned the Mountain King’s silent recognition, a sign that they possess the resolve and fortitude worthy of guiding their people. To be acknowledged by Kair in this way is considered the highest endorsement of leadership among the mountain clans.
- **Clergy and Temples**: Kair’s clergy are few and far between. Yet his image endures through architecture itself. Across Staterum, temples and sanctuaries built upon mointain slopes or high plateaus typically bear the mark of Kair’s silent influence. It has become custom among builders in high places to include a small dedication to Kair, lest they provoke his quiet displeasure. These offerings are understated—a single carved peak set into a wall, a spiral of stone at the threshold, or a shallow basin filled with meltwater placed to face the highest point nearby. Few who include such gestures truly expect his attention, yet all agree it is wise to show respect to the one whose patience holds the mountains aloft.

## Significant Historical Impact

- **Major Plot Points in World History**: 
	- **The Raising of the Talam Range**: In the age before humanity, when the world’s form was still fluid and the lands of Kair’Thear lay open and unbound, Kair undertook his greatest creation. With the slow, inexorable power of stone itself, he heaved the earth upward, forming the colossal Talam Range—a vast spine of mountains that divided the continent in two. This act, undertaken to gift his brother Thear a realm of his own, a distant haven in the far east where he could rule free from the meddling of their kin. The mountains rose higher than any before or since, their roots sinking deep into the bones of the world, their peaks piercing the realm of clouds.
