## Basic Information

- **Name**: Ooscara
- **Domain**: Blizzards, Ice, Glaciers
- **Title**: Queen of the Eternal Frost, Empress of the Arctic Expanse
- **Alignment**: Chaotic Neutral
- **Symbol**: A snowflake
- **Primary Worship Area**: [[Arctic Expanse]]

## Physical Description

- **Appearance**:

Ooscara's appearance captures the ethereal beauty and formidable presence of winter personified. Her skin is pale as the moonlit snow, and her eyes shimmer with the light blue of glaciers, piercing yet distant. Her hair flows long and white, cascading like a frozen waterfall, sparkling with frost that reflects the faint light of winter suns.

Her figure is tall and commanding, draped in robes that shimmer with a thousand shades of silver and blue, mimicking the ever-changing ice of her realm. The fabrics seem woven from the very essence of frost, flowing and swirling around her with every movement, as if caught in a perpetual gentle snowfall. Her crown, a delicate and intricate lattice of ice crystals, sits atop her head, proclaiming her sovereignty over the barren north lands. 

The air around Ooscara is perpetually cold, with tiny crystals of ice forming in her wake. Her presence brings the whisper of blizzards, and her touch can frost over the warmest hearts. Despite her chilling demeanor, there is a captivating beauty in her stern visage, like the awe one feels viewing untouched snowfields under the stark winter light.

## Relationships

**Family:**
- **[[Ooscie]]**: Mother. Ooscara harbors a deep-seated resentment towards Ooscie, although the origin of resentment remains unknown to all but Ooscara herself. 
- **[[Keldria]]**, **[[Onasu]],** and **[[Thalmon]]**: Elder Brothers. Ooscara views her elder brothers with disdain, considering them quarrelsome fools. Of the three, she harbors a slight favoritism towards Thalmon, perhaps because she sees in him a depth that resonates with her own solitude.
- **[[Typhial]]**: Youngest Brother. Typhial is the only sibling Ooscara truly loves, appreciating his loyalty and sense of duty that starkly contrasts with the tumult brought about by her other brothers. Despite her affection, she maintains a deliberate distance.

## Mythology and Worship

- **Creation Myth**: 

Ooscie, Goddess of all water, had three sons she loved dearly. However these sons had engaged in a brutal conflict, each desiring to be claim dominion over their mothers realm. Desperate to mend the fractures within her divine family, she sought counsel from her ancient siblings, having themselves once been embroiled in a fierce feud for dominance.

Talam, ever forthright, proposed a resolution through strength and confrontation, advocating a direct challenge to settle the discord. Lasair, believing in the virtues of independence, argued that the brothers should be left to resolve their disputes on their own, suggesting that interference might only deepen the rifts.

However, it was Gaoth Oir, the wisest and most cunning among them, who offered a novel solution. He proposed the creation of a new deity, one born of wisdom and fairness, who could mediate the conflicts of her siblings and restore balance. His plan was not merely to intervene but to enrich the pantheon with a presence that embodied harmony and understanding, or so he claimed. 

Moved by Gaoth Oir's insight and driven by a mother's desperation to restore peace among her children, Ooscie agreed to his proposal. Together, they combined their divine essences in a union of wind and water, from which Ooscara was born amidst a serene snowfall that blanketed the world in hushed tranquility.
