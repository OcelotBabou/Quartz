## 1. **Overview**

Affinity-based magic is understood by its practitioners as a living relationship between the mortal body and the forces permeating the world. These forces are commonly described through the **Living Octad**. At its centre sit the four stable **Core Affinities**, associated with the physical states and behaviours of matter. Around them moves the **Shifting Crown**, formed by four rarer and less stable **Outer Aspects**.

The Octad is not a collection of separate magical substances. It is a map of relationships. Every living person is believed to possess some position within this field, though in most people the resonance is too weak and undirected to produce a deliberate magical effect. Those whose resonance extends strongly towards one or more parts of the Octad are known as affinity wielders, Attuned or, in older days, sorcerers. 

## 2. **Natural Affinity**

Affinity is the natural tendency of a person’s body and spirit to recognise particular forces within the world. Most people possess only a faint, balanced resonance close to the centre of the Octad. They may feel unusually comfortable beside running water, possess an instinctive sense for storms or remain warm in cold weather, but their connection is too weak to consciously shape magic. 

An affinity wielder possesses a resonance that extends farther from the centre. The direction of that resonance determines which forces answer most readily to them. Its depth determines the strength of the connection, while its breadth influences the range of related expressions available to the wielder. 

Affinity is shaped by several interwoven factors, including inheritance, personality, environment, place of birth and exposure to powerful magical forces. Family lines often show recurring tendencies, while people raised near elemental shrines or sites of concentrated magic (active volcanoes, ancient springs, mountain peaks) display stronger and more distinctive affinities than those born in heavily urbanised regions. 

No single factor reliably determines the result. A child may resemble a parent, express a neighbouring affinity, awaken a connection unseen for generations or possess no usable resonance at all. Many scholars, rulers and bloodlines have claimed to understand or control this process, yet the formation of affinity continues to defy prediction. Whatever patterns mortals identify, the relationship between a person and the world remains too complex to be reduced to ancestry or environment alone.

## 3. The Living Octad

The octad represents affinity as a continuous and partly unstable field. At its centre lies undifferentiated mortal potential. The farther a person’s affinity extends from the centre, the more strongly they resonate with the corresponding force. 

The Octad is formed from two related structures:

- The stable inner arrangement of the four Core Affinities.
- The mobile outer ring known as the Shifting Crown.

## 4. The Core Affinities

The four Core Affinities form a stable inner diamond; fire stands opposite water, air stands opposite earth. Fire and Water represent opposing tendencies of energetic release and fluid regulation. Air and Earth represent opposing tendencies of expansion and structural stability. With the between them forming recognisable interstitial regions. These relationships can be observed repeatedly enough that even rival affinity traditions generally agree on their broad arrangement.

The Core Affinities are:

- **Fire**, associated with energy, plasma, heat, release and transformation.
- **Air**, associated with gases, pressure, motion, breath and transmission.
- **Water**, associated with liquids, circulation, flow and redistribution.
- **Earth**, associated with solids, structure, density and endurance.

The names are mortal conveniences. Fire is not merely flame, nor Earth merely stone. They represent broad behaviours through which physical reality expresses itself.

### Fire

Fire governs energised matter, heat, release, combustion and rapid transformation. Flame is its most visible expression, leading many cultures to regard Fire as the most naturally destructive affinity. Yet Fire is equally important to metalworking, cooking, glassmaking, preservation and the controlled transformation of materials. Fire can burn, but it can also temper, cauterise, accelerate, purify, illuminate and force one state into another.

Fire wielders commonly work through tension and release. They gather energy through movement, friction, rapid breathing and the drawing of heat from the surrounding environment. Many become restless or physically agitated while holding power, as though their bodies are seeking an outlet. They bounce between their feet, roll their shoulders or shake tension through their hands, growing more energetic as heat accumulates. The most dangerous moment is often the instant they become still. That sudden pause compresses the gathered energy before an explosive release.

In combat, a Fire wielder may gather heat through constant movement before releasing it as a concentrated burst, ignite an opponent’s equipment or force metal armour to become dangerously hot. In practical work, they can maintain precise furnace temperatures, temper tools, shape glass or draw damaging heat from stored food. While a skilled practitioner may manipulate the progress of energetic reactions, reducing wood to ash without there ever being a visible or evident flame. 

### Air

Air governs gaseous matter, pressure, breath, sound, motion and transmission. Wind is its most familiar expression, but Air also shapes differences in pressure, carries voices, redirects momentum and influences how things move through open space. Air wielders are valued as sailors, messengers, scouts and navigators, though concentrated pressure can be as destructive as flame or stone.

Air wielders rarely remain in one place. Their practice is built around outward movement, expanding breath and the constant adjustment of position. They turn, leap, duck and cross through the space around them, tracing paths for the air to follow rather than forcing it directly ahead. A ship’s windcaller may appear to dance along the deck, climbing rigging, shifting between masts and leaning into movements the wind has not yet made. Their arms open away from the body, their posture continually expands and contracts, and even when they pause their clothing or hair often continues moving. Air magic gives the impression that the wielder is always arriving somewhere and already preparing to leave it.

In combat, an Air wielder may redirect pressure to soften a fall, turn aside a projectile or strike with a sudden compression of air. In practical work, a ship’s windcaller can guide favourable currents through the sails, while miners and builders use controlled airflow to ventilate enclosed spaces. More abstractly, an Air wielder may use pressure and vibration to perceive spaces they cannot see, or carefully disturbed pressure around the inner ear may induce dizziness, nausea, disorientation or a false sensation of falling without leaving an obvious injury.

### Water

Water governs liquid matter, circulation, pressure, flow and redistribution. Its most familiar expressions are the movement of water and the regulation of bodily fluids, giving it a strong association with healing. Water can carry, cleanse, cool, dissolve and reshape, making its wielders valuable in medicine, irrigation, shipping, flood management and the handling of delicate materials.

Water wielders favour slow, continuous and flexible motion. Their gestures rarely end where they began; one movement folds into the next, tracing curved paths through the air or along a surface. Their weight shifts smoothly through the hips and shoulders, allowing force to pass around them rather than meeting it directly. They may guide a current with their fingertips, draw broad circles with both arms or mirror the pulse and breathing of a patient. Sudden motions are uncommon. Even forceful workings build through rhythm, gathering momentum through repeated redirection until the water is moving exactly where the wielder intends.

In combat, a Water wielder may redirect the force of a wave, wrap an opponent in a shifting restraint or use a narrow stream under pressure to cut or unbalance. In practical work, they can regulate irrigation, drain flooded land, preserve moisture in crops or stabilise bleeding and circulation within an injured patient.  A clever wielder may influence substances through the fluids carrying them. They might draw poison towards one part of the body for removal, separate pigment from dye, pull salt from soaked soil or trace a person’s passage through the moisture and oils they left behind.

### Earth

Earth governs solid matter, structure, density, minerals and the distribution of weight. Stone and soil are its most visible expressions, but Earth also concerns foundations, faults, pressure, balance and the relationships holding solid objects together. Earth wielders are essential to construction, mining, fortification and structural repair, though the same understanding can be used to collapse walls, split roads or direct tremendous force through the ground.

Earth wielders move very little. Their feet remain firmly connected to the ground, sliding or turning rather than lifting whenever possible. Long periods of stillness are interrupted by short, abrupt motions: a heel grinding into stone, a palm striking a wall, a shoulder shifting or a hand snapping into alignment. These movements can appear stiff, jerking or unfinished, because the wielder is not performing the full action with their body. They are correcting the angle of a much larger structure. Once the force begins moving, the wielder becomes still again, bracing themselves as the ground completes what their brief motion set in motion.

In combat, an Earth wielder may raise a barrier along an existing fault, anchor themselves against an impact or send a concentrated tremor through the ground beneath an opponent. In practical work, they can locate mineral deposits, strengthen foundations, redistribute the strain within a bridge or compact loose soil into a stable road. More abstractly, an Earth practitioner may transmit coded vibrations through connected stone, recover impressions of past impacts from a structure or make several separate walls behave briefly as one continuous body.


## 5. The Shifting Crown

Beyond the Core Affinities moves the unstable outer ring known as the **Shifting Crown**. The Crown contains four recognised Outer Aspects; light, dark, alpha and omega. Unlike the Core Affinities, the Outer Aspects do not appear to possess permanent positions. They drift around outside the inner diamond, drawing closer to different affinities at different times, places and circumstances. Scholars frequently depict them in different arrangements, and none has demonstrated that their preferred model is universally correct.

In one person’s affinity profile, Light may hover close to Fire, encouraging radiance, projection and the outward transmission of heat. In another, Light may rest between Air and Water, influencing mist, reflection, distant images or signals carried through the atmosphere. 

Nor is the Shifting Crown fixed within an individual’s affinity. Its position may respond to emotion, environment and immediate purpose. An Earth wielder who is cornered and afraid may draw Dark closer, concealing their presence within stone or opening a sheltered hollow beneath the ground. The same wielder standing in fertile soil may instead feel Alpha approach, encouraging roots through an earthen barrier or shaping a clay guardian capable of repairing itself with living growth.

These circumstantial movements do not erase the wielder’s natural disposition. Most practitioners experience one or two Outer Aspects more readily than the others, and these enduring inclinations usually exert the strongest influence over their art. The Crown may shift with need, but it does not move equally or effortlessly for everyone.

Additionally, as a general rule the outer aspects appear to resist their conceptual opposites, As Light draws closer, Dark often recedes. As Alpha becomes prominent, Omega may become harder to reach. This opposition is not always represented as a straight line across the Octad. The Crown may distort, tilt or become uneven. 

### Light

Light governs radiance, projection, revelation, visibility and outward expression. Illumination is its most obvious manifestation, but Light also concerns the transmission of images, the exposure of hidden qualities and the manner in which something presents itself to the wider world. It can reveal, guide and communicate, but can equally blind, overwhelm, distort or expose what was meant to remain private.

Light wielders commonly work through extension and focus. Their posture opens towards the world: hands spread, eyes remain fixed upon the target and movements travel outward in clean, deliberate lines. Many use reflective surfaces, polished metal, water or glass to divide and redirect their influence. Their magic often begins as faint radiance gathering around the eyes or fingertips before extending along a chosen path. Unlike the restless release of Fire, Light feels purposeful and direct, as though the wielder is drawing a line between themselves and something distant. Greater workings may require them to hold their gaze without blinking, gradually narrowing many scattered reflections into one clear expression.

In combat, a Light wielder may blind an opponent, produce misleading afterimages or expose the outline of someone hidden by magic or cover. In practical work, they can illuminate large spaces, transmit visible signals, inspect fine craftsmanship or reveal flaws concealed beneath a surface. A refined users can even harness its power for powerful beam like effects to deal effect. 

### Dark

Dark governs concealment, inwardness, stillness, absorption and the reduction of outward presence. Shadow is its most recognisable expression, but Dark does not merely remove light. It suppresses the signs by which something is perceived: sound, contrast, heat, magical residue and even the instinctive sense that something deserves attention. It can provide shelter, privacy and rest, but may also isolate, obscure and cause people or memories to be overlooked.

Dark wielders usually work by gathering inward. Their movements begin wide and collapse towards the body, as though drawing curtains, blankets or walls around themselves. Hands close, shoulders lower and breathing becomes shallow and quiet. Where Light reaches outward, Dark reduces the space a person occupies. The wielder may appear to fold into their own shadow, pulling silence and dimness from nearby corners until they form a contained shell. The strongest workings often involve near-total stillness; movement risks disturbing the carefully gathered absence.

In combat, a Dark wielder may disappear into a shadowed space, suppress the sound of movement or conceal the approach of allies. In practical work, they can create restful chambers, protect sensitive objects from observation, reduce distracting noise or preserve privacy during delicate negotiations and healing. More powerful wielders may extend this influence beyond physical concealment, subduing painful memories, entering or shielding dreams, or preventing others from perceiving and interacting with particular people, objects or places.

### Alpha

Alpha governs emergence, continuation, amplification, branching and the widening of possibility. Growth is its most familiar manifestation, but Alpha is not life itself. It encourages existing processes to begin, repeat, spread or become more than they previously were. It can promote healing, fertility and adaptation, but may also produce escalation, infestation, obsession and change that no longer recognises a proper limit.

Alpha wielders tend to work through repetition and expansion. A simple gesture is performed again, each time becoming broader, faster or more complex. Fingers may branch apart like roots, steps form widening spirals and breath builds in repeated cycles rather than a single release. Their magic rarely remains confined to the first point of contact. It spreads along relationships: from one root to another, one heartbeat to the next, one flame to nearby fuel. Alpha workings often appear to learn from themselves, with each repetition strengthening the pattern established before it. The challenge is not beginning the process, but deciding when and how it must stop.

In combat, an Alpha wielder may cause restraints to thicken as they are broken, amplify an ally’s existing strength or encourage a small injury to spread dangerously through an opponent’s body. In practical work, they can accelerate crop recovery, stimulate healing, propagate useful plants or help a community of organisms adapt to harsh conditions. In the hands of a powerful practitioner, Alpha workings may become self-sustaining, continuing long after their creator has departed or died. Without carefully imposed limits, these effects can spread, adapt and intensify beyond their original purpose, sometimes with catastrophic consequences.

### Omega

Omega governs conclusion, reduction, separation, exhaustion and the narrowing of possibility. Death and decay are among its most feared expressions, but Omega is not simply destructive. It allows processes to finish, harmful connections to be severed and unstable conditions to settle. It can end pain, infection, movement or magical influence, though careless use may also remove warmth, sensation, memory or qualities essential to identity.

Omega is perhaps the most enigmatic of the affinities, and the actions of its wielders are notoriously difficult to recognise or follow. Much of their channeling occurs internally, with only slight movements of the fingers, momentary twitches or interruptions in breath marking the release of power. This obscurity is intensified by the detachment from the world that many Omega wielders gradually develop. Observers may instinctively overlook them, the mind passing over their presence as though attempting to disregard an inconsistency it cannot understand. To those nearby, the magic therefore appears to come from nowhere: something simply stops behaving as it did before.

In combat, an Omega wielder may remove the momentum from a charging opponent, extinguish the heat within a weapon or sever the continuity of an ongoing magical effect. In practical work, they can halt bleeding, suppress pain, separate contaminants from water or end dangerous reactions before they spread. At its furthest extremes, Omega may disturb the principles upon which the world ordinarily depends. Gravity may cease to exert itself consistently, the boundary between life and death may temporarily lose its meaning, or time may advance in abrupt lurches and pauses rather than as an uninterrupted progression.

## 6. Affinity Profiles

Affinity is often visualised as a shape extending outward from the centre of the Octad. An ordinary person might be represented by a small circle near the centre, showing limited and relatively undirected magical potential. A strongly Fire-attuned person may possess a narrow oval stretching towards Fire. A broad Air–Water wielder may display a larger shape extending between both Core Affinities, representing an interstitial relationship such as glacimancy. A rare person possessing opposed Fire and Water resonance might be represented by a thin shape stretched tightly between those points. Their power is potentially formidable, but their body must endure contradictory pressures. The Shifting Crown is then drawn around this profile based of observations, or left out altogether if unclear. 

These diagrams are approximations. They do not perfectly describe a living person, nor can affinity be measured with complete consistency. They remain useful because they reveal several principles:

- **Direction** indicates the Core Affinities towards which the individual is oriented.
- **Distance** indicates the depth or strength of attunement.
- **Breadth** indicates the range of closely related expressions available to the wielder.
- **Distortion** may show an Outer Aspect being drawn towards or pushed away from the wielder’s resonance.

### Interstitial Affinities

Affinity does not stop at the named points of the Core diamond. Many wielders stand between neighbouring Core Affinities, expressing a blended resonance that cannot be accurately described as two separate powers. 

The most common place example is glacimancy. Glacimancers stand between Water and Air. They guide moisture, pressure and thermal movement through flowing forms, then abruptly lock the established pattern into place. Their magic is not simply Water hardened into ice, nor Air used to produce cold. It emerges from the relationship between the two: Water governs the movement, concentration and suspension of moisture, while Air controls the pressure, circulation and loss of heat surrounding it.
