From a young age, your extraordinary prowess in battle, and strong moral compass, set you apart, destined to leave your mark upon the world. After countless skirmishes, adventures and contracts, you managed to not only build a reputation for yourself, but for all of Ratfolk kind. However, a brutal ambush left you teetering on the edge of the abyss. Miraculously, as your spirit was fading, you felt the touch of the divine. Awakening you found yourself in the care of Eris' clergy. 

This near-fatal encounter was a turning point in your tale. Believing your days of adventuring behind you, life looked to be set for a quieter existence. Yet, the divine intervention left unanswered questions and an unshakeable feeling that your story was far from over. The tales of Eris, intertwined with the events that ended the Age of Darkness, have began to haunt your dreams.

Now the tug of destiny pulls at your restless spirit once more, a pull towards the divine and the secrets that disappeared alongside Eris. As whispers of unrest and shadows from the past stir once again, you must leave behind your brief respite and embark on a perilous quest to uncover truths that have remained hidden for too long.
