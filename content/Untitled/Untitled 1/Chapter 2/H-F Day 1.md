### Day 1 - Immediate Post-Escape and Arrival at the First Town

#### **Immediate Pursuit:**

- **Setup:** Just as the party puts some distance between themselves and Hu Dao, their relative quiet is shattered. The silhouettes of five Grekin—Gamyshynx's long range scouts—become visible against the moonlit sky. Their eerie shrieks pierce the air, signaling to more distant forces the party’s location.

#### **Grekin Attack:**

- **Combat Encounter:** The Grekin swoop down, attacking the party in a coordinated assault. Their goal is not just to harm but to delay the party, buying time for larger forces to converge on their position.
- **Tactics:** The Grekin use hit-and-run tactics, utilizing their flying ability to strike and then pull back into the safety of the dark sky, making them challenging targets.

#### **Options for Evasion:**

- **Stealth Option:** If the party chooses to hide instead of fight, they must succeed in a series of Stealth checks to evade the Grekin and any subsequent ground patrols at dawn. This option emphasizes caution and resource preservation.
- **Continued Flight:** If the party decides to keep moving through the night, they avoid immediate confrontation but at the cost of exhaustion and limited resources.

#### **Consequences of Actions:**

- **Successful Evasion:** The party avoids a direct confrontation but must deal with fatigue and the psychological toll of being hunted.
- **Combat Exhaustion:** Engaging the Grekin drains the party’s resources, setting a precedent for the campaign that careless use of resources could lead to dire situations.

#### **Arrival at the First Town:**

- **Desolate Roads:** As dawn breaks, the party notices an unusual absence of the usual morning traffic on the roads—no traders, travelers, or local traffic, adding an eerie layer to their journey.
- **Discovery of Destruction:** Upon arriving at, Bùyào, the first town along the Dao River, the scene is unsettling. The town appears abandoned at first glance, but signs of destruction become increasingly apparent. Massive misshapen footprints and swathes of demolished structures suggest the involvement of something as large and powerful as the Death Hulk.
- **Exploration and Clues:** As they explore, the party finds no bodies—though judging from the bloodshed, it is safe to assume not many escaped. 
- **Decision to Stay or Move:** If the party considers staying overnight to rest, they risk another attack by Gamyshynx's forces. The destruction and the foreboding silence suggest that lingering could be dangerous.

### Strategic Considerations for the Party:

- **Resource Management:** This first day tests the party’s ability to manage physical and magical resources wisely.
- **Psychological Impact:** Dealing with the constant threat and seeing the aftermath of such attacks may affect the party’s morale and decision-making.
- **Long-term Planning:** Decisions made during this first encounter could set the tone for how the party approaches future challenges, emphasizing stealth, combat, or a mix of both.
