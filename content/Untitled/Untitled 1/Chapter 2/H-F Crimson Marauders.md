#### **Overall Command**

- **Lieutenant:** The overall commander of the strike force. Skilled in both combat and strategy, the lieutenant oversees the entire operation, making tactical decisions in real-time based on the unfolding situation.

#### **Three Main Groups (Squads)**

- **Eastern Approach (Captain 1):**
    
    - **7 Footsoldiers:** The main frontline, engaging directly with village defenses.
    - **3 Archers:** Positioned to offer long-range support, targeting key defensive points or personnel.
    - **1 Brute:** Acts as the shock troop, breaking through or destabilizing strong points.
- **Western Approach (Captain 2):**
    
    - **7 Footsoldiers:** Similar setup and role as the eastern group.
    - **3 Archers:** Support with covering fire, could attempt to flank or find vantage points.
    - **1 Brute:** Works in tandem with footsoldiers to overwhelm defenses on this side.
- **Northern Approach (Captain 3):**
    
    - **7 Footsoldiers:** Tasked with creating a diversion or probing for weaknesses.
    - **3 Archers:** More defensively oriented, providing suppressing fire.
    - **1 Mage:** Uses spells strategically to disrupt enemy formations, create obstacles (like _Fog Cloud_), or deal damage (like _Fireball_).

#### **Scout Teams**

- **4 Scouts:** Lightly armed and agile, tasked with reconnaissance, finding alternate paths into the village, or creating openings for the main force. They could also perform sabotage missions like cutting supply lines or setting fires.
- **4 Mounted Scouts:** Lightly armed mobile force, tasked with ensuring no one escapes and relaying orders between groups. 

#### **Army Composition**

- **CM Footsoldiers (7 per squad):**
	- **Armor Class:** 14 (Leather Armor)
	- **Hit Points:** 22 (5d8)
	- **Speed:** 30 ft.
	- **Attack:** Longsword (+5 to hit, 1d8+3 slashing damage)
- **CM Archers (3 per squad):**
	- **Armor Class:** 13 (Leather Armor)
	- **Hit Points:** 18 (4d8)
	- **Speed:** 30 ft.
	- **Attack:** Longbow (+4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., 1d8+2 piercing damage)

	- **Armor Class:** 16 (Chain Mail)
	- **Hit Points:** 45 (6d10 + 12)
	- **Speed:** 25 ft.
	- **Attacks:**
		- Enforcer (+2 to hit, 2d8+4 bludgeoning damage), Double damage against structures.
		- Armoured Fists (+6 to hit, 1d6+4 bludgeoning damage)
- **CM Mage (1 per one squad):**
	- **Armor Class:** 12 (Cloth)
	- **Hit Points:** 27 (6d6 + 6)
	- **Speed:** 30 ft.

	- **Armor Class:** 15 (Leather Armor)

	- **Speed:** 35 ft.

	- **Armor Class:** 14 (Leather Armor)

	- **Armor Class:** 17 (Breastplate)
	- **Hit Points:** 52 (8d8 + 16)
	- **Speed:** 30 ft.
	- **Attacks:**
	    - **Multiattack:** The captain makes two attacks with his longsword.

	- **Armor Class:** 18 (Half Plate)
	- **Hit Points:** 65 (10d8 + 20)

	    - **Multiattack:** The lieutenant makes two attacks: one with his longsword and one with his dagger.

	    - **Dagger:** (+8 to hit, reach 5 ft., 1d4+4 piercing damage)
	- **Leadership (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest):** For 1 minute, allies within 30 feet can add a d4 to one attack roll or saving throw each round. 

#### **Strategy**

- **Initial Setup:**
    
    - All three groups approach simultaneously, maintaining formation to present a unified front. This coordinated movement ensures the village’s defenses are engaged on multiple fronts, dividing their focus and resources.
- **Engagement Strategy:**
    
    - **Eastern and Western Groups:** Perform a standard military assault with brutes leading to break through or tie down the main defensive points. Archers from these groups take higher ground or secure positions that allow effective coverage of the battlefield.
    - **Northern Group:** The mage begins with fog cloud in an attempt to hide approach.  Following this, footsoldiers engage to exploit the confusion, with archers attempting to sneak in to find better ground.
- **Scout Operations:**
    
    - Deploy before the main assault to gather intelligence and after the initial engagement to exploit identified weaknesses or adjust tactics based on village responses.
- **Lieutenant’s Role:**
    
    - Moves between groups to ensure coordination and adapt tactics as required. Might directly engage to exploit critical weaknesses or to bolster a faltering front.
