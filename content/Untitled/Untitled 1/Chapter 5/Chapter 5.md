Rhaegar side quest:
In Vitaurum, [[Vaerun]] will be working to train [[Biranian Guard]]. He set out to track down the wayward [[Lioren]] after a dispute with the mother. He tracked her to Vitaurum, but was running low on money or leads, so has been working here to earn money and try find info on her. Here Rhaegae will run into him (he tries hide it, but is very excited to see his son again), and he will seek his aid in tracking down Lioren, as well as inquiring into [[Veyna]]. A lead will guide them to search [[Abdurii]], here Lioren is living a life of luxury and will not want to go back home. She can either be convinced to go home, or they can convince Vaerun she is happier here, regardless she will mention Veyna came past awhile ago  and left a sending stone that can be used to communicate. If used Veyna will be reluctant to share info, but may be talking into sharing that her and a few select Rift Watch seek to infiltrate the Shadow Aisle Mountains, the dark rumor's surrounding it having concerned their captain ([[Corrin Thenos]]). Corrin may be one of the potential solutions/entry points to the SAM.
