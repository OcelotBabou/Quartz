Flown is in the midst of internal conflict, an ascended church splinter group has revolted against the main branch. After a failed ratfolk assassination attempt against Logar, Logar refused to condemn the ratfolk at large, claiming that this was the act of select few evil not indicative of the race at large. This angered the more anthropocentric factions of the Ascended Church, who broke off and are now aiming to take over the church (this splinter group is related to the group that attacked Virag).  

This starts with the party met by city guards, warning against their type entering the city at the moment. Will need to be convinced that they should be allowed to enter. 

Remona will seek them out eager to hear of what occurred in Hu Dao, she will also be able to explain the events of the town, and will recommend meeting with Logar. 

The party will see and experience signs of the escalating conflict. 
- It will be hard to find accommodation, and there will be few other non-human races. 
- There will be heightened security
- There will be rallies and recruit drives for the splinter group

There will be a part where some Biranian Spies will be captured by the splinter group and are going to be put to execution (Hopefully Tom will intervene here) First mentions of Gamyshynx here, plus a bit more info on scale of the ambitions, revealing Shadow Aisle mountains have already fallen, the plan is currently underway to undermine Agroma. 

**Setting the Stage:**

- After the party has gathered significant information and witnessed firsthand the unrest and divisive politics in Flown, tensions reach a boiling point. During a public event, Serena will hold a speech explaining how damaging this unrest is and present personal support of Logars stance, when a sudden and violent attack is launched against her.

**The Attack:**

- The attackers, revealed to be aligned with the splinter group and Gamyshynx's forces, are well-coordinated and heavily armed. The party, having aligned themselves with the current leadership, jumps into action to protect Serena.
- Amidst the chaos, Agron makes a dramatic entrance, attempting to persuade Serena to join their cause, citing deep-rooted corruption and the need for drastic change. His plea, however, is rebuffed by Serena, who stands firm in her commitment to peace and stability.

**The Knight's Intervention:**

- As the battle intensifies, the Hollow Knight intervenes. Depending on the party’s effectiveness in thwarting the attackers, the knight attempts either to kidnap Serena or, failing that, to assassinate her as a final resort. 

**Aftermath and Alignment with Remona:**

- In the wake of the attack, whether Serena is kidnapped or killed, the city of Flown is thrown into even greater turmoil. Remona, seeing the party as capable and trustworthy, aligns closely with them. Fearing for her own safety and the future of Agroma, she urges the party to flee the city.
- Remona proposes they head to Vitaurum, not only to seek refuge but to rally support from Agroma's strongest ally, warning them of the dangerous forces gaining power in Flown.

### **Consequences and Next Steps**

**If Serena Is Kidnapped:**

- Serena returns to power but is now a puppet for Gamyshynx. Together, Agron and Serena initiate efforts to remove Logar from power, framing it as a necessary purge to save the church and the city.

**If Serena Is Killed:**

- Agron takes this opportunity to reshape the leadership by appointing a new Triarch of Commerce, claiming Serena was a traitor. This move is part of a larger plan to consolidate power and eliminate Logar.

Either way Agron declares Remona kidnapped by the party, putting a bounty on their heads and painting them as criminals. The party, now fugitives and framed as kidnappers, must navigate hostile territories and seek allies in Vitaurum. This journey involves not just physical challenges but also the need to strategize and gather support against the forces that have overtaken Flown.
