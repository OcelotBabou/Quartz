As the caravan rolls quietly along the winding forest path, the earlier tranquillity of your journey is shattered by the sudden twang of bowstrings. From the dense underbrush ahead a single arrow flies forward, embedding itself not half a foot in front of  (whoever is leading the caravan).

“Halt you filthy band of misfits, least you wish to see your friend dead!” a gruff voice bellows from the foliage. Stepping onto the path ahead, a rugged man with a dark bandana that conceals his lower face, while his eyes peer out from beneath a hood. Clad in leather armour marked by the symbol of a thorned vine, he has a beaten and bloodied Lieve in a headlock, with a knife drawn to his neck. Around him several bandits step out on either side of the path.

**Korgan's Demand**

Korgan makes his demand with chilling clarity, "Hand over your treasures and supplies, and perhaps your scout's life will be spared." Bradbury, visibly wrestling with his options, addresses the caravan and the party, his voice strained but determined, "A life, holds more value than any material wealth we possess. We must consider this trade."

**Scenario Dynamics**

- **If the Party Complies:** Should the party and Bradbury decide to hand over the goods, Korgan, with a nod that carries a mix of respect and ruthless pragmatism.  "A wise decision," he states coldly as he orders his men to collect the goods and retreat, however as his men disappear into the forest, he will slash Lieve before fleeing into the forest after his men.
- **If the Party Refuses or Negotiates Poorly:** If the caravan refuses or fails to persuade Korgan, he ruthlessly fulfills his grim promise. With a swift and brutal motion, he dispatches Lieve, throwing the caravan into immediate chaos. "So be it," he declares, as the forest erupts into violence.

**Bradbury's Reaction**

Regardless of how the situation unfolds, Bradbury reacts with visceral anger. His eyes fixate solely on Korgan, burning with a mix of grief and rage. As the skirmish unfolds, he charges towards Korgan, his actions driven by a desire for retribution, largely ignoring the broader conflict around him. This single-minded pursuit could leave him vulnerable, adding another layer of tension to the encounter.

**Encounter Details**

There are 14 bandits not including Korgan. 3 using light crossbows from the trees on either side of the road (6 in total). 6 bandits will approach from the front, while 2 will attempt to sneak up on the rear.

- **Location and Environment:** The ambush occurs in a narrow part of the forest path, limiting the caravan’s mobility and providing the bandits with natural cover. The environment can be used strategically by both the bandits and the party.
- Korgan's bandits, while more committed to the confrontation due to his leadership, still value their lives above all. They engage with a readiness to retreat if the battle tilts unfavourably against them.
- **Archers' Strategy:** Archers positioned within the forest utilize the natural cover to their advantage. They aim to maintain a safe distance from the melee, prioritizing shots from concealment. Their main goal is to harass and weaken the caravan's defences without exposing themselves to direct combat.
- **Focus on Elves:** Reflecting a specific animosity towards elves, the bandits attempt to target any elves within the caravan. However, their focus on elves does not override their survival instincts; they will shift their attacks to immediate threats if directly endangered.
- **Retreat Conditions:** As the fight progresses, naturally it will turn against the bandits. Rather than a chaotic retreat however, the bandits to the north (right side) will flee first trying to draw fire. While the bandits to the south will utilise more advanced tactics like a smoke bomb to try mask their retreat.

**Scenario Following Bandit Retreat**

**Tracking the Fleeing Bandits:**

- If the party decides to pursue the fleeing bandits, they find themselves at a strategic decision point. The caravan, although eager to reach Hu Dao safely, will express reluctance at being left alone but won’t actively prevent the party from pursuing.
- The pursuit leads to the lakeshore, where the party discovers several abandoned rowboats, all without oars, suggesting the bandits had a planned escape route across the water.
- No trace of Bradbury or clear paths the bandits might have taken across the lake are evident, posing a dead-end unless players come up with creative strategies to continue the chase.

**Rejoining the Caravan**

**Outcomes Based on Player Actions:**

- **If Bandits Were Significantly Thwarted:** Should the party successfully thwart many of the bandits before they could flee, the caravan remains largely secure. Gratitude and a strengthened resolve mark the group’s demeanour as they continue towards Hu Dao, although they are still clearly shaken by the death of Lieve and the disappearance of Bradbury. Elborn and Tiana take it upon themselves to tend to Lieves body. Elborn also explains that he knowns the owner of the Rising Sun inn, ensuring care and rest for those who are injured.
- **If Bandits Managed to Flee Successfully:** If the bandits from the left side managed to escape, Elborn will be missing upon the party’s return. The group explains that a feint by the bandits drew the party away, allowing the bandits that escaped to the south to double back and take Elborn hostage, although Faelar and his crew managed to protect Tiana who they also attempted to capture.
- **Staying with the Caravan:** If the party never leaves the caravan or quickly rejoins after a brief pursuit, they find the caravan mostly intact. Elborn, if not captured, shares that accommodations have been arranged at The Rising Sun inn in Hu Dao, ensuring care and rest for those who are injured.
