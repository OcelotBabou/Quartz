"Trust Me" is a social game designed to test intuition and the ability to read others, perfect for a diverse group learning to rely on each other. As the game unfolds, players are encouraged to share stories or secrets as part of the gameplay, providing personal insights and fostering a deeper understanding among the group. This activity serves as an informal icebreaker, allowing players to engage with the NPCs in a relaxed setting, revealing their characters' personalities, backgrounds, and motivations.

Rules:

The objective is to earn the most points by accurately judging whether stories are true or lies, or by successfully deceiving the group with a fabricated story.

- On their turn, each player tells a brief story about themselves. The content can involve past experiences, beliefs, or fears and may be true or a fabrication.
- After a story is told, the group discusses whether they believe the story is true or a lie. They have a limited time (e.g., 2 minutes) to debate and must reach a consensus.
- The group then makes a single collective decision: truth or lie.
	- If a player strongly disagrees with the group’s consensus, they can issue a challenge. Each player is allowed one challenge per round.
	- The challenging player then directly contests the storyteller's truthfulness using their Insight against the storytellers persuasion or deception (done in secret).

Scoring:

- If the story was true and the group guesses truth, the storyteller earns 1 point.
- If the story was a lie and the group is deceived, the storyteller earns 2 points.
- If the group correctly identifies a lie, the storyteller earns 0 points.
- If a challenge is successful (the challenger’s guess aligns with the truth): The challenger earns 2 points, while the storyteller receives 0 points.
- If a challenge is unsuccessful (the challenger’s guess is incorrect): The challenger loses 1 point, and the storyteller receives 2 points.
