As dawn breaks over the farmstead—or wherever the party chose to spend the night—the air is filled with the crisp promise of a new day. The journey to Hu Dao resumes, marked by a serene atmosphere that contrasts sharply with the previous day's turmoil. This peaceful travel offers ample opportunity for character development and role-playing among the party and their caravan companions.

**Tiana's Interaction**

- **Trigger Event:** As the caravan meanders through a particularly quiet stretch of the road, Tiana approaches a party member—either the one she feels closest to based on previous interactions or, if no significant bonding has occurred, a randomly selected member.
- **Whispered Concerns:** In a low, concerned tone, Tiana confides that she thought she saw something—or someone—in the bushes to their right. She suggests keeping a lookout without alarming the others.
- **Perception Check:** The player Tiana approached rolls a Perception check (DC 16). On a successful check, they distinctly spot a figure lurking at the forest's edge, observing the caravan with cautious interest.

**Encounter with Shaw**

- **Bandit's Reaction:**

- **If Previous Bandits Escaped:** Should the figure realize they've been spotted, they quickly sprint back into the forest, disappearing among the trees.
- **If No Bandits Escaped:** The man, caught off-guard but realizing confrontation is inevitable, steps out with a sheepish grin and introduces himself as Shaw. He fabricates a story about being left behind by his hunting party after a night of excessive drinking.

·         **Questioning Shaw:** Shaw sticks to his story about hunting and losing his friends, claiming his suspicious behaviour was just an attempt to still catch some game. He inquires if the caravan has seen any other groups, hoping to redirect the conversation and gather information about the area’s activities or the missing bandits he was sent out to find.

·         **If Allowed to Leave:** Assuming Shaw isn’t detained or his story isn’t doubted enough to warrant further action, he departs only to return later that night, claiming to be still lost. He seeks out Lieve, insisting on his help alone to find his friends, citing a need for the kind of tracking skills Lieve possesses.
