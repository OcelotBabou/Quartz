As we draw closer to the Festival of Gahrian, your updates on the wine preparations have been most reassuring. The atmosphere must be one of jubilation and harmony, conducive to our guests fully partaking in all we have prepared for them.

On another note, there have been murmurs about certain adventurous souls who might be inclined to meddle in affairs that do not concern them. To mitigate any potential interference, a special batch has been prepared specifically for the beer—an acknowledgment of the adventurers' known fondness for indulging in drinking competitions during such gatherings. This should sufficiently ensure they remain... preoccupied.

You have done well to avoid disappointing thus far, ensure this continues.
