As we approach the culmination of our preparations for the Festival of Gahrian, it is imperative that we refine our approach to managing the delicate balance of public perception and suspicion. Your role, though performed with adequate compliance thus far, will demand greater attention. You have spent excessive time on your own 'lofty' ambitions, and as the festival nears it is imperative that we set our nets well.

For maximum efficacy, it is essential that Hu Dao’s reputation as a sanctuary for elves not only persists but flourishes. Beneath this veneer of camaraderie, you are to subtly sow seeds of discord between the elven and human populations. This task must be handled with finesse; overt actions are not only discouraged but reprehensible given our current stance. 

Noting the substantial limits of your abilities, I have engaged the services of the [[Crimson Marauders]], as well as incentivizing the local bandits to congregate around us. Their presence will not only drive the elves towards the perceived safety within Hu Dao but also drain vital resources from the already stretched defences. I reluctantly entrust you to ensure these activities are seen as random acts of banditry and not connected to a larger scheme. 

Do not disappoint.
