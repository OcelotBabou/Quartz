**Overview:**

While adventuring in Hu Dao, the party will increasingly encounter whispers and evidence of a pervasive criminal influence. The Thieves Guild, a shadowy organization, appears deeply interwoven with various facets of the city’s life, manipulating events from behind the scenes to serve its enigmatic goals.

**Key Characters**:
- [[Veren Aralith]]: As the head of the Thieves Guild and the Triarch of Commerce, Veren is a pivotal figure in Hu Dao’s underworld and upper echelons alike. His dual roles allow him to manipulate economic and criminal activities with ease.
- The Mullen Brothers - [[Jack Mullens]], [[Jacob Mullens]], [[John Mullens]]: These brothers manage The Drunken Weasel and are known members of the Thieves Guild. Their tavern serves as a front for the guild’s meetings and illicit activities.
- [[Miranda Fleet]]: A notorious slaver with ties to the Thieves Guild. Miranda seeks to expand [[Neodoulas]]'s influence and trade into Western Siar'Morrin
- 

**Plot Hooks**:
- **Mullen** **Brothers**: Successfully befriending or manipulating the Mullen brothers can lead to accidental or deliberate leaks of guild secrets. Engaging with Jacob’s quest regarding the dubious merchant can further deepen the party’s involvement, offering more opportunities to gather intelligence.
- **Calybe’s Thugs**: The thugs attacking Calybe’s herbalist shop can be interrogated for information about their orders and the guild’s interest in disrupting Calybe’s business.
- **Miranda Fleet**: Capturing Miranda Fleet in the slaver quest provides a direct link to the guild’s more nefarious operations. She could be a wealth of information about the guild’s reach and plans.
- 
- **Veren’s Misdirection**: As a contingency plan and to maintain his guise as an ally, Veren may offer 'assistance' to the adventurers by directing them to a decoy guild base, planting false evidence intended to mislead them and secure their unwitting compliance in minor guild operations.


**Related Missions**:
- [[Bandits Stole my Merchandise]]: After a dispute in town, the party is tasked with retrieving stolen good for Lorrim.
- [[Infiltration]]: After a tip off, the party discovers the Thieves Guilds location and must infiltrate it.
- Bump in the Night: After the Festival of Gahrian, the group is rudely awoken to the sounds of chaos in the streets.
