The adventure begins at a campsite roughly two day's journey from Hu Dao. Players may have joined the caravan as guards or travellers, or they may stumble upon the camp and decide to spend the night. Rumours of a new bandit group near Hu Dao have reached the caravan, setting the stage for heightened vigilance.

*Flavour Text*


In an effort to calm nerves and foster camaraderie among the group, Bradbury, invites the players to join a game of “[[Trust Me]]” he has started with a few of the caravans members. Joining the game are Tiana, the bubbly and curios elf cleric;  Faelar, co-leader of Mithrallica and talented bard; and Calvin, the merchant most receptive to the other members of the caravan.

As the evening winds down, whether the players choose to participate in the game or not, Lieve returns to the camp, slightly out of breath and with a look of concern. He pulls Bradbury aside for a private conversation, their voices low but urgent.
