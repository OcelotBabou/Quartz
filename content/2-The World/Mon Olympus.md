### 1. **Overview**

Mon Olympus, a towering mountain that rises from the heart of the Mare Sanctum Ocean, is the most enigmatic and revered locations in all of Staterum. Once recognised as the home of the Gods, when they still resided on the mortal plane, this sacred island has become a realm of mystery and wonder following the gods’ ascension at the end of the Age of Darkness ([[AoD]]). It is guarded by Ooscie’s three most powerful children, Keldria, Onasu, and Thalmon, who ensure that no mortal sets foot on its hallowed shores.

### 2. **Regions**

- **Destiny Beach**: The outer edges of Mon Olympus are a true paradise, boasting golden beaches and verdant gardens that brim with exotic flora. The air is perpetually infused with the sweet perfume of blossoms, creating an otherworldly tranquility. Hidden within the lush forests are cascading waterfalls and crystal-clear streams that wind their way gracefully into the Mare Sanctum, blending seamlessly with the pristine beaches that encircle the island. 
- **The Summit:** Hidden high above the clouds lies the legendary city of the gods. Though no mortal has laid eyes on it since the gods’ departure, legends speak of a radiant metropolis crafted from materials beyond mortal comprehension, with shimmering towers and celestial gardens.

### 3. **Description**

Mon Olympus is a sight unlike any other in Staterum. The island’s golden beaches give way to a narrow ring of impossibly fertile lowlands, where the flora is so abundant it is said to grow faster than it can be consumed. These gardens form a verdant border around the towering mountain, which dominates the island’s landscape.

The mountain itself rises so high that its peak is permanently obscured by clouds, lending it an ethereal and otherworldly appearance. Its slopes are steep and rugged, covered in dense forests at lower altitudes that gradually give way to bare rock and sheer cliffs as one ascends. The very air around Mon Olympus carries a sense of awe and trepidation, as if the island itself recognises its divine origins.

Mon Olympus has always been revered as the divine heart of Staterum, the place where the gods returned to, during the ages when they walked side by side mortals. The mountain served as their celestial abode, and its shores were said to be the meeting place where mortals and gods could commune in harmony.

When the Age of Darkness began, and the gods departed for the heavenly realms, Mon Olympus was abandoned. The city atop the mountain was left untouched, preserved as a sacred monument to their presence. To prevent mortals from defiling the holy land, Ooscie’s three strongest children, Keldria, Onasu, and Thalmon, took up the role of eternal guardians, ensuring that the island remains sealed from mortal interference. 

Mon Olympus is not only a symbol of divinity but also serves as a reminder of the gods’ presence and their departure from the mortal realm. For the devout, it represents hope and the enduring connection between Staterum and the celestial realms. Its beauty and mystery have inspired countless stories, songs, and prayers, becoming a central figure in the spiritual lives of many cultures.
