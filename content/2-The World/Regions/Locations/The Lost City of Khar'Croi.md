**Overview:** 

The Lost City of Kharz'Croi is a legendary ancient city hidden deep within the valleys of [[Phemus Reach]]. Believed to have been built by a forgotten civilization of giants, Kharz'Croi is shrouded in mystery and intrigue, with tales of incredible wealth and powerful artifacts said to be hidden within its ruins. The city's elusive nature and the dangers of Phemus Reach make it a tantalizing yet perilous destination for adventurers and treasure hunters.

**Description:** 

Kharz'Croi is rumored to be an expansive city carved into the cliffs and caverns of Phemus Reach. The city's architecture is said to be both grand and imposing, featuring massive stone structures, towering pillars, and intricate carvings that reflect the strength and artistry of its giant builders. The city is thought to be overgrown with dense vegetation, with crumbling ruins and hidden passages creating a labyrinthine environment. Many believe that Kharz'Croi's central plaza houses a colossal statue of [[Talam]], the Earth titan, watching over the city.

**History:** 

According to legend, Kharz'Croi was established by a powerful civilization of giants, descendants of Talam, who sought to create a majestic city that would stand the test of time. The city thrived for centuries, becoming a center of culture, power, and wealth. However, internal conflicts, external threats and the de-evolution of giants eventually led to the city's downfall. Over time, Kharzhum was abandoned and forgotten, its location lost to the shifting landscape and the encroaching wilderness. The city's history is preserved only in the myths and stories passed down through generations.

**Significance:** 

The Lost City of Kharz'Croi holds immense cultural and historical significance. It is believed to be a repository of ancient knowledge, powerful artifacts, and untold riches. The city's legends and the artifacts it contains could offer valuable insights into the history and culture of the giants and their impact on Phemus Reach. Additionally, the potential wealth hidden within Kharz'Croi makes it a highly coveted destination for treasure hunters and adventurers, despite the dangers involved in seeking it out.

**Notable Features:**

- **Colossal Statue of Talam:** A massive stone statue of Talam, the Earth titan, believed to stand in the central plaza of Kharz'Croi, serving as a symbol of the city's grandeur and power.
- **Grand Stone Structures:** The city is said to feature towering stone buildings and intricate carvings, showcasing the architectural prowess of its giant builders.
- **Hidden Passages:** Kharz'Croi is rumored to be filled with hidden passages and secret chambers, creating a complex and maze-like environment for explorers.
- **Ancient Artifacts:** Tales of powerful artifacts and treasures hidden within Kharz'Croi's ruins attract adventurers and scholars alike, eager to uncover the city's secrets.

**Local Legends or Stories:**

One legend focuses on the mysterious location of Kharz'Croi, speculating that it actually resides at the very bottom of [[The Echoing Abyss]]. Such legends tell of how the city of giants, ruled by wise and powerful giants became arrogant and challenged their creator, Talam. Angered Talam cast their city into a bottomless pit, and cursed their once proud race to descend into mindless brutes. Other versions tell, that the giants excavating the land came across [[Niphar]] as their quarries dug deep into the ground. Niphar, offered his help and a location protected from the meddling of the outside world, taking them deep into his realm. Here at the base of the Echoing Abyss, their mighty city was constructed. 
Other legends focus on the riches and artifacts said to be held within Kharz'Croi, talking of seas of gold, and artifacts once held by the Gods themselves when they still walked the planet. If such a city truly does exist, it can be expected that such stories of treasure and wealth have not be overestimated.
