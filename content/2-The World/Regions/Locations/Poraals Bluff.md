**Overview:** 

Poraal's Bluff is a notorious location in Northern [[Portum]], where the [[Ridgeback Mountains]] meet the Periculosus Ocean. It is infamous for its never-ending storm and perilous waters, making oceanic travel nearly impossible. It acts as a natural border between the Southern and Northern reaches of the Periculosus Ocean. 

**Description:** 

Poraal's Bluff features dramatic cliffs rising sharply from the turbulent waters of the Periculosus Ocean. The area is constantly battered by violent storms, with lightning illuminating the dark, swirling clouds and thunder echoing off the rugged mountains. The sea below is equally treacherous, with massive waves crashing against the cliffs and swirling currents posing a constant threat to any vessels daring to approach.

![[Poraalbluff.webp]]

**History:** 

Historically, Poraal's Bluff has been a place of awe and fear. Mariners and travelers have long avoided this dangerous stretch of coast, and numerous shipwrecks lie at the bottom of the ocean, testifying to the perilous nature of the waters. Local lore has always linked the unending storm to the presence of [[Poraal Oir]], the God of Storms, believed to dwell within the heart of the tempest.

**Significance:** 

Poraal's Bluff is significant for its natural and mythological importance. The constant storm serves as a powerful natural barrier, mirroring the Ridgeback Mountains in separating the North from the South. Additionally, the association with Poraal Oir adds a layer of cultural and spiritual significance, making the location a place of reverence and fear.

**Notable Features:**

- **Endless Storm:** The perpetual storm that shrouds the area, with fierce winds, lightning, and thunder creating a constant spectacle.
- **Rugged Cliffs:** The dramatic cliffs that rise from the ocean, providing a stark and imposing landscape.
- **Shipwreck Graveyard:** The remains of countless ships that have succumbed to the treacherous waters, visible during low tide or from safe vantage points on the cliffs.

**Local Legends or Stories:** 

The predominant legend of Poraals Bluff, speaks of Poraal Oir, the God of Storms, who is said to reside within the heart of the tempest. It is believed that Poraal Oir controls the weather, and his wrath manifests as the unending storm that engulfs Poraal's Bluff. 
Another story tells of "The Lost Invasion," an orc armada set on escaping the [[Sceel wastelands]], through an invasion via the sea. This armada was destroyed to the last orc as they recklessly attempted to cross the ocean surrounding Poraals Bluff.  

**Visitors' Experience:** 

Visiting Poraal's Bluff is an awe-inspiring but perilous endeavor. The view of the endless storm and the crashing waves is both terrifying and mesmerizing. The rugged cliffs offer vantage points where visitors can observe the fury of the elements. However, the area is not recommended for the faint-hearted or unprepared, as the unpredictable weather and dangerous terrain pose significant risks. For those that wish to view it from the sea, such recklessness is forbidden, as the locals of Portum consider such a task a waste of a boat.
