**Overview:** 

The Forgotten Pools are a mysterious and secluded area in Donatus Stultitia, located near the northern edges of the [[Shadow Aisle Mountains]]. Known for their serene beauty and the enigmatic aura that surrounds them, the pools are a hidden gem within the harsh and arid landscape of the Mirage Plains.

**Description:** 

The Forgotten Pools are a series of natural springs and small ponds, fed by underground rivers originating from the Shadow Aisle Mountains. The water in these pools is crystal-clear and cool, creating an oasis-like environment amidst the otherwise dry and unforgiving terrain. The area around the pools is lush with greenery, including tall grasses, flowering plants, and ancient trees that provide shade and shelter. The pools vary in size and depth, some being shallow enough to wade through, while others are deep and mysterious, their bottoms hidden from view.
