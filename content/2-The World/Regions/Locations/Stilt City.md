**Overview:** 
Stilt City is a significant Ratfolk settlement known for its impressive stilt architecture and the resilience of its inhabitants. It is one of the oldest and most established cities in the Paloto Swamplands.

**Description:** 
Stilt City is built entirely on tall stilts to protect against the swamp’s flooding and unstable ground. The buildings are interconnected by a network of bridges and platforms, creating a multi-layered cityscape that rises above the swampy waters.

**History:** 
Stilt City was one of the first major Ratfolk settlements and has long been a center of innovation and adaptation. Its architecture and engineering techniques have been crucial in developing methods used throughout the Paloto Swamplands. The city has grown and evolved, maintaining its importance as a hub for knowledge and community.

**Significance:** 
Stilt City is essential for its architectural innovations and as a center of trade and knowledge. It is also significant for its library and educational facilities, which attract scholars and researchers from across the region.

**Notable Features:**

- **The Sky Market:** An elevated marketplace where Ratfolk trade goods and services.
- **Observation Tower:** A tall structure offering panoramic views of the surrounding swamplands.
- **The Great Library of Stilt City:** A repository of Ratfolk knowledge and innovations, overseen by Chief Librarian Mara Longtooth.

**Local Legends or Stories:** 
One legend speaks of the "Founders' Bridge," the first bridge built in the city, believed to bring good fortune to those who cross it.

**Visitors' Experience:** 
Visitors can enjoy the bustling Sky Market, explore the Observation Tower for stunning views, and delve into the vast knowledge available at the Library of Inventions. The city's unique architecture and vibrant community offer a memorable experience.
