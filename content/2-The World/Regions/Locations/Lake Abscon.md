**Overview:** 

Lake Abscon is a serene and mysterious body of water located at the Eastern base of the [[Shadow Aisle Mountains]], known for its clear, still waters and the secrets it is believed to hold beneath its surface. The lake is surrounded by dense forest and steep mountain slopes, creating a secluded and tranquil environment that has captivated explorers and locals alike.

**Description:** 

Lake Abscon is characterised by its tranquil waters that reflect the surrounding mountains and forests, creating a stunning mirror-like effect. The lake is deep, with its depths shrouded in mystery, and the water is often described as having an almost otherworldly quality. The shores of Lake Abscon are lined with ancient trees and lush vegetation, providing a natural habitat for a variety of wildlife. The area is often enveloped in a gentle mist, adding to its mystical and secluded atmosphere. 

**History:** 

The history of Lake Abscon is steeped in legends and tales of hidden treasures and ancient secrets. Among the most captivating stories is that of an underwater city, said to exist in complete isolation in the lake's mysterious depths. Over the centuries, Lake Abscon transformed from a place of solitude and enigma to a bustling trade route that connected the eastern and western realms of Siar'Morrin. However, under the current regime, Lake Abscon has reverted to its secluded state. The lake's remote location and the dense forests surrounding it have ensured that it remains largely untouched by human activity, preserving its natural beauty and mystique.

**Significance:** 

Prior to the isolationist approach of the Shadow Aisle Mountains, Lake Abscon played a major role in transport between eastern and western Siar'Morrin. Its tranquil water providing far easier opportunities for travel than the dense forest surrounding it, it once served as the main passage to [[Iterdeep]] from the east. 

**Notable Features:**

- **Reflective Waters:** The lake's clear, reflective waters create a stunning visual effect, mirroring the surrounding landscape and enhancing its serene beauty.
- **Dense Forests:** The shores of Lake Abscon are lined with ancient trees and lush vegetation, providing a rich habitat for wildlife and contributing to the area's secluded atmosphere.
- **Hidden Depths:** The lake is deep and mysterious, with its depths rumored to hold ancient secrets and hidden treasures.
- **Mist-Covered Shores:** The area around the lake is often enveloped in a gentle mist, adding to its mystical and tranquil ambiance.

**Local Legends or Stories:**

The reflective waters of Lake Abscon have fueled the imagination of many curious locals. One story tells of an ancient underwater city, shrouded in mystery and protected from the outside world. It is said that the city, nicknamed Abyssal, was built by a long-lost civilization that sought refuge from the turmoil on the surface. The inhabitants of Abyssal are believed to live in harmony, their lives untouched by time. Occasionally, explorers claim to have seen faint lights glowing beneath the water, hinting at the presence of this hidden city.
Another enchanting legend speaks of "The Mirror of the Night." Lake Abscon's surface, known for its crystal-clear reflection, is said to capture the night sky in such stunning detail that it acts as a perfect mirror. According to the legend, on certain nights when the stars align just right, the reflection on the lake's surface becomes a portal to the heavens. It is believed that those who gaze into the "Mirror of the Night" can see visions of the past, present, and future, or even communicate with Gods in their realm removed from Staterum.


**Visitors' Experience:** 

Visitors to Lake Abscon are immediately captivated by its serene and mystical beauty. The clear, reflective waters and the surrounding forests create a tranquil environment, perfect for relaxation and introspection. Those interested in the local legends can seek out the hidden depths or wait till night and attempt to communicate with the Gods. Lake Abscon offers a unique and peaceful retreat, allowing visitors to connect with nature and the mysteries of the past.
