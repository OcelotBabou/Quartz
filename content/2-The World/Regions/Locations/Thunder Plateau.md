**Overview:** 

Thunder Plateau is a flat, elevated expanse in [[Phemus Reach]] where storms frequently gather. The plateau's turbulent weather and its central landmark, an ancient tower stretching into the skies, make it a place of awe and danger. Thunder Plateau is revered and feared for its raw elemental power and the mysteries surrounding the ancient tower.

**Description:** 

Thunder Plateau is characterized by its expansive flatlands, which provide a stark contrast to the rugged terrain of the surrounding region. The plateau is often shrouded in dark storm clouds, with lightning striking frequently and thunder echoing across the landscape. At the center of the plateau stands an ancient, weathered tower that reaches high into the sky, seemingly touching the storm clouds often seen above. The tower, made of dark stone and etched with ancient runes, is a focal point of both curiosity and dread.

**History:** 

The history of Thunder Plateau predates the modern giants of Phemus Reach. According to legend, the original giants—intelligent, powerful, and proud descendants of [[Talam]]—took issue with [[Gaoth Oir]] and his offspring, who frequently flaunted their power over the skies of the giants' domain. The giants created the ancient tower as a device to capture the elusive air elementals. However, the endeavor seemingly failed, as suggested by the tower's dilapidated state and the continuing legends of Gaoth Oir's dominance. The true outcome remains shrouded in mystery, but it is clear that the giants' plan did not succeed.

**Significance:** 

Thunder Plateau holds significant cultural and mystical importance for the giants of Phemus Reach, though it is often avoided due to its turbulent history and constant storms. The plateau is believed to be a site of immense elemental power, attracting those seeking to harness or understand this raw force. The ancient tower, rumored to be a relic of the giants' failed endeavor to capture air elementals, stands as a symbol of their past ambitions and their struggle against Gaoth Oir. The frequent storms and the tower’s mysterious nature make the plateau a revered yet feared place, where only the bravest giants dare to venture, offering insult and challenges to Gaoth Oir and his descendants.

**Notable Features:**

- **Frequent Storms:** The plateau is known for its constant stormy weather, with lightning and thunder creating a dramatic and dangerous environment.
- **Ancient Tower:** The central feature of Thunder Plateau is the ancient tower, a tall structure made of dark stone and inscribed with mysterious runes. The tower is a focal point of both reverence and mystery.
- **Flat Expanse:** The plateau itself is a vast, flat area, providing an unobstructed view of the stormy sky and the surrounding landscape.
- **Ritual Sites:** Scattered around the plateau are various sites where giants perform rituals, marked by stone circles, altars, and other ancient structures.

**Local Legends or Stories:**

One of the most enduring legends is that of The Stormbringer's Tower. According to the tale, the ancient tower was constructed by a powerful giant known as the Stormbringer, who desired the ability to control the weather. It is said that the Stormbringer used the tower to channel the energy of the storms, wielding lightning and thunder as weapons. 
Another popular story speaks of The Eternal Storm. This legend claims that the storms on Thunder Plateau are the result of a curse placed by [[Poraal Oir]] after a great betrayal. According to the tale, the plateau was once a peaceful and fertile land, frequented by Gaoth Oir who enjoyed its towering plateau. The giants who had lived her under blessing of Gaoth Oir however, turned on Gaoth Oir seeking to attain his power for their own. Angered by this attack on its creator Poraal cursed these lands, his vengeance still seen in the frequent storms that persist here.

**Visitors' Experience:** 

Visitors to Thunder Plateau are greeted by a dramatic and awe-inspiring landscape. The flat expanse and the towering storms create a sense of grandeur and danger. The ancient tower at the center draws the eye, its dark stone and mysterious runes evoking both curiosity and reverence. The frequent lightning strikes and thunderclaps add to the sense of raw elemental power, making the plateau a thrilling yet perilous destination. For those interested and capable of undertaking such a perilous adventure, the secrets held within the solitary tower could hold untold power.
