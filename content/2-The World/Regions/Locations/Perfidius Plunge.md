**Overview:** 

Perfidius Plunge is a breathtaking natural wonder located along the Perfidius River. This section of the river was once an underground waterway flowing through the mountains. However, long ago, the ground above was cleared, transforming it into a spectacular waterfall that seemingly falls into the earth itself. At the base of Perfidius Plunge lies the port entrance to [[Iterdeep]], carved through the mountains, which once served as a crucial passage for travelers crossing from Eastern to Western Siar’Morrin.

**Description:** 

Perfidius Plunge is a dramatic and awe-inspiring sight. The river, which flows robustly from [[Lake Abscon]], cascades over a sheer cliff and plunges into a vast chasm, disappearing into the earth. The roaring waterfall is surrounded by steep, rocky cliffs and lush vegetation, creating a stunning natural landscape, as well as a formidable barrier for those that seek passage along the river.

**History:** 

Historically, the Perfidius River flowed underground, hidden from sight as it carved its way through the mountains. However, due to a significant geological event or deliberate engineering by ancient inhabitants, the ground above the river was cleared, exposing the river and creating the magnificent waterfall known as Perfidius Plunge. This transformation turned the area into a strategic and picturesque landmark. The port entrance to Iterdeep, located at the base of the waterfall, was carved through the mountains and served as a critical gateway for travelers and merchants journeying between the eastern and western regions of Siar’Morrin.

**Significance:** 

Perfidius Plunge holds immense natural and historical significance. The presence of this dramatic waterfall, with its sheer drop into the earth, created a natural barrier that necessitated the establishment of Iterdeep. Merchants and travelers were compelled to stop at Iterdeep, utilizing its strategically located harbor at the base of the plunge to continue their journey. This port entrance became a crucial hub for trade and travel, facilitating the movement of goods and people between Eastern and Western Siar’Morrin.

**Notable Features:**

- **The Waterfall:** The most prominent feature, the waterfall of Perfidius Plunge, is a spectacular sight, with the river dramatically disappearing into a chasm in the earth.
- **Port Entrance to Iterdeep:** At the base of the waterfall lies the historic port entrance to Iterdeep, an intricately carved gateway through the mountains that once served as a crucial passage for travelers.
- **Viewing Platforms:** Strategically placed platforms provide visitors with safe vantage points to view the waterfall and the surrounding landscape.

**Local Legends or Stories:**

Long ago, an ancient and forgotten evil, one that dared to challenge the gods themselves, retreated beneath the Shadow Aisle Mountains. This malevolent force threatened to emerge and wreak havoc upon the world. In response, the deity [[Statera]] sent [[Talam]] to uncover and vanquish this threat. Driven by a powerful rage, Talam not only demolished the ancient evil but also destroyed significant sections of the mountain in the process. His wrath tore open the earth, revealing the hidden underground river system that would become the Perfidius Plunge.

**Visitors' Experience:** 

Visitors to Perfidius Plunge were once treated to a majestic natural spectacle. The sound of the roaring waterfall, combined with the sight of water cascading into the earth, creating a mesmerizing experience. The viewing platforms offering safe spots to take in the beauty and power of the plunge.
