*[layk noh-vus mah-ray]*

**Overview:** 
Lake Novus Mare is a vast freshwater lake on the eastern border of [[Agroma]], serving as a vital resource for the region.

**Description:** 
Lake Novus Mare is renowned for its crystal-clear waters, surrounded by rolling hills and fertile plains. The lake is a hub of activity, with fishing boats dotting its surface and bustling markets along its shores. On its eastern edge, it is fed by the Perfidus River, which flows from the [[Shadow Aisle Mountains]], while the northern part is nourished by the Dao River.
A distinctive feature of Lake Novus Mare is its trifurcation, where three distinct rivers flow out from it. The Vilcilous River and Minatus River flow westward to the Periculosus Ocean, while the Sacer River flows south into the Mare Sanctum Ocean.

**History:**
Lake Novus Mare has been central to Agroma since its earliest days. It was a crucial factor in the establishment of the region’s first settlements and has remained a key resource for irrigation and fishing.

**Significance:** 
The lake supports the agricultural needs of Agroma by providing a reliable source of freshwater. It also plays a significant role in local trade, with goods transported down its numerous rivers. 

**Notable** **Features**:

- **Perfidus and Dao Rivers:** The Perfidus River feeds the lake from the east, originating from the Shadow Aisle Mountains, while the Dao River flows in from the north, contributing to the lake’s rich ecosystem.
- **Trifurcation:** Lake Novus Mare features a unique trifurcation, with three distinct rivers flowing out from it. The Vilcilous and Minatus Rivers flow west to the Periculosus Ocean, and the Sacer River flows south into the Mare Sanctum Ocean, facilitating extensive trade routes.
- **Bustling Markets:** Along the shores, particularly near the fishing docks, bustling markets offer a variety of goods, including fresh fish, local produce, and handcrafted items.


**Local Legends or Stories**:
Some believe that an ancient, submerged city lies beneath the lake's surface, home to treasures and artifacts from a long-lost civilization. Divers and explorers often seek out this mythical city, hoping to uncover its secrets.
Another story speaks of the glowing lights seen over the lake at night, thought to be the spirits of ancient guardians watching over the waters. These lights are often seen as a sign of good fortune and protection for fishermen and travelers.

**Visitors' Experience:**
The lake’s clear waters are perfect for boating and fishing. Visitors can rent boats, join local fishermen and travelling merchants, or if wealthy enough purchase their own boat to explore the expansive waters and adjoining rivers that make up Lake Novus Mare. 
The bustling markets along the shores offer a lively atmosphere where visitors can shop for fresh fish, local produce, and unique crafts. While the surrounding hills and fertile plains provide beautiful and for this age relatively safe opportunity for exploration and connection to nature.
