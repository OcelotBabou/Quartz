**Overview:** 
Mozkull Pass is a treacherous route through the Ridgeback Mountains, providing the only known pathway from Ridgeback Forest to the [[Sceel Wastelands]].

**Description:** 
The pass is a narrow, winding trail that cuts through the rugged terrain of the Ridgeback Mountains. The path is lined with steep cliffs and rocky outcrops, with frequent landslides and unpredictable weather adding to its dangers. Despite its perilous nature, the pass offers breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape, particularly for those seeking to escape the hardships of the Sceel Wastelands.
