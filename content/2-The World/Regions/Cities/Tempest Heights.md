**Overview:**

Tempest Heights, perched among the ancient giants of Sabhaile Forest, is a showcase of the ingenuity and spirit of the Tempest Pack. This sprawling treetop village, seamlessly integrated into the canopy, serves as both a sanctuary and a strategic vantage point for its inhabitants. Known for its breathtaking views and harmonious existence with nature, Tempest Heights stands as a testament to what can be achieved in tandem with nature.  

**Description:**

Constructed on the sturdy branches of Sabhaile's oldest trees, Tempest Heights is a network of platforms, bridges, and dwellings that sway gently with the forest's breath. The architecture is an organic blend of natural materials and Cia'an craftsmanship, featuring wood harvested from the forest floor and ropes made from local vines. The village's layout is designed to enhance community interaction while also providing privacy and tranquility for its residents.

Each dwelling is uniquely tailored to harness the natural light and views of the forest, with skylights and open terraces built above the dense canopy allowing some of the only natural light into this ancient part of the forest. The central plaza is the heart of the village, , built into the hollowed out trunk of a behemoth of a tree, where community decisions are made, celebrations are held, and stories are shared.

The design of Tempest Heights also incorporates various environmental protections, such as rainwater collection systems and wind barriers, ensuring the village not only survives but thrives in the dynamic forest ecosystem. This treetop village not only showcases the Cia'an's deep respect for their environment but also their ability to adapt and flourish in what many would consider a challenging living situation.

**Notable Locations:**

- **The Great Hollow:** This central plaza is built within the hollowed-out trunk of the largest tree in Sabhaile Forest. The Great Hollow is spacious enough to easily harbor the entire clan, serving as a communal meeting place, with sectioned off area's built in to accommodate private meetings, and other higher up business. 
- **Lunar Tower:** Positioned to rise above the forest canopy, Lunar Tower is a beloved lookout and observatory within Tempest Heights. This elevated platform is perfect for stargazing and monitoring celestial events, offering unobstructed views of the night sky.
- **Whispering Path:** This secret entrance to the village is known only to the Tempest Pack members. The Whispering Path is a concealed, winding staircase carved inside an ancient tree, leading up to the heart of the village.
- **Sky Orchard**: This area of the village has been heavily reinforced to support soil beds and water systems, allowing for the cultivation of various fruits and vegetables high above the ground. The orchard not only provides sustenance but also adds to the village's self-sufficiency, reducing the need to descend to the forest floor for supplies.
