**Overview:** 

Furrowton is the northernmost Ratfolk city in the Paloto Swamplands, situated right on the edge of the swamplands bordering Agroma. Established with the express desire to foster relations with the wider Siar’Morrin communities, Furrowton serves as a gateway between the Ratfolk and their neighboring regions.

**Description:** 

Furrowton is a bustling city characterized by its unique blend of traditional Ratfolk architecture and elements inspired by its neighboring regions. Elevated walkways and stilted buildings are common, reflecting the city’s swampy surroundings. The city is strategically located to facilitate trade and communication, with well-maintained roads leading into Agroma and beyond. Furrowton is known for its welcoming atmosphere, vibrant markets, and numerous embassies and trade offices.

**History:** 

Founded by Garon Reedwhistle a visionary Ratfolk leader, Furrowton was established as a bridge between the Ratfolk communities and the broader world of Siar’Morrin. The city has been slow to grow, but remains focused on its role as a center for diplomacy and trade, attracting merchants, diplomats, and adventurers from various regions. Its history is marked by successful treaties and trade agreements, in defiance of traditional bigotry towards Ratfolk, that have enriched both Furrowton and its neighboring communities.

**Significance:** 

Furrowton holds significant strategic and diplomatic importance. It is the primary point of contact between the Ratfolk and the rest of Siar’Morrin, playing a crucial role in fostering peaceful relations and mutual cooperation. The city’s markets and trade networks have become vital for the local economic prosperity, and its diplomatic efforts have helped to integrate the Ratfolk more fully into the wider community.

**Notable Features:**

- **Diplomatic Quarter:** This area houses embassies and consulates from various regions, facilitating diplomatic relations and negotiations.
- **Grand Market:** A bustling marketplace where traders from Furrowton, Agroma, and beyond come to exchange goods. It is particularly loved for its rare access to Ratfolk inventions and progressive medicine. 
- **Furrow Zoo:** A dedicated section of the city showcasing the wonders of the Paloto Swamplands to visitors, featuring a variety of exotic and native wildlife.
- **Furrow Expo:** A large hall designed to showcase the latest Ratfolk inventions and technological advancements, offering a glimpse into their innovative spirit and creativity. Although patent and the secrets of such advancements are still well kept, offering only a glimpse at their potentials.

**Local Legends or Stories:** 

One popular legend in Furrowton is that of "The First Envoy." According to the tale, the first Ratfolk envoy to venture into Agroma was guided by a mysterious, glowing light through the treacherous swamps. This light is believed to have been a blessing from the Tree of Sideron, ensuring the envoy’s safe passage and successful mission. Another story speaks of "The Guardian," a giant catfish that is said to protect the waters around Furrowton, ensuring safe and prosperous trade routes. The irony of the guardian being a _cat_ fish, is a long running joke enjoyed by many in Furrowton.

**Visitors' Experience:** 

Visitors to Furrowton can expect a warm and vibrant welcome. The Grand Market offers a sensory feast of sights, sounds, and smells, with merchants eager to share their unique goods. The Diplomatic Quarter provide a glimpse into the city’s important role in regional politics and diplomacy. 
Most Ratfolk in Furrowton go out of their way to offer hospitality to visitors. While the broader perception of Ratfolk may remain largely negative, the local regions surrounding Furrowton have developed much better relationships and a deeper understanding of their Ratfolk neighbors.
