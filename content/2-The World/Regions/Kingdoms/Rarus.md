*[rah-roos]*

### 1. **Overview**

Rarus is the most secretive and isolationist region within the Shattered Kingdoms, strictly enforcing a no-foreigners policy since the early days of the [[Schism Wars]]. With its borders defined by the Inarith River to the east and the Delath River to the south, and shielded by the rugged [[Protoramus Range]] to the north, Rarus has effectively cut itself off from the outside world. Its rivers are patrolled with unnerving vigilance by guards who seem tireless in their duties, creating an impenetrable barrier to outsiders. Under the rulership of Queen Ximena Balaska, the borders were closed, and since then, a shadow, whether metaphorical or literal, has been said to loom over this mysterious land. 

### 2. **Geography**

Bordering the arid [[Pilliga]] Plains, Rarus shares much of the flat terrain characteristic of the region. However, its natural enclosure between the Inarith and Delath Rivers provides a consistent source of moisture, creating a stark contrast to the parched expanses of the plains. This vital access to water allows Rarus to sustain a level of agricultural productivity sufficient to support its isolated population, with fertile riverbanks providing an essential lifeline for crops and livestock. The Protoramus Range to the north adds a rugged backdrop, shielding the region from harsher climates and creating a natural fortress that complements its staunch isolationist policies.

### 3. **History**

Before the [[Schism Wars]], Rarus was a steadfast advocate for reuniting the Shattered Kingdoms under New Birania’s banner. Its commitment to this cause was resolute, with Rarus deploying significant forces in the early stages of the Schism Wars. However, Mesokini, wary of a pincer maneuver between Pistos and Rarus, focused its efforts on neutralizing Rarus early in the war. A pivotal moment came when Mesokini forces executed a devastating ambush in the central spurs, decimating a significant portion of the Rarus army. The loss of experienced commanders and troops in this single blow crippled Rarus’ military efforts and morale.

Following this defeat, Rarus adopted its now-infamous isolationist stance, sealing its borders and ceasing all external communication. Since then, Rarus has effectively vanished from the political landscape, shrouded in secrecy and leaving the rest of Siar’Morrin to speculate about the shadow that now looms over this hidden region.

### 4. **Political Structure**

Before its self-imposed isolation, Rarus was ruled by Queen Ximena Balaska, a resolute leader deeply committed to the ideals of unity under New Birania. However, that was 76 years ago, and what has become of its political structure since then is shrouded in mystery. Whether Queen Ximena still rules, if her line continues, or if another form of governance has emerged, remains entirely unknown. Outsiders are left to speculate, with rumors ranging from a tyrannical monarchy to a complete collapse of governance, but no credible information has surfaced to confirm any of these theories.

### 5. **Economy**

The current state of Rarus' economy is entirely unknown. Some rumors suggest the land has become barren, long abandoned by its people. However, the unwavering and highly organised patrolling of its borders suggests otherwise. This vigilance implies that whatever is occurring within Rarus is at least sustainable enough to maintain its borders and support the patrols. Whether the region relies on subsistence agriculture, hidden trade routes, or some unknown resource remains a matter of speculation.

### 7. **Military**

The highly disciplined and ever-present border patrols indicate that Rarus has maintained a functional military force. These patrols are remarkable for their precision and vigilance, suggesting significant training and organization. However, whether this border force represents the entirety of Rarus' military or merely a fragment of a greater, unseen army remains unknown.

### 8. **Notable Locations**

- **Rarus:** Shrouded in mystery, the city of Rarus is perched perched high on the slopes of the Protoramus Range, offering a commanding view of the surrounding land. Tales of its grandeur and architectural beauty persist from before the Schism Wars, describing it as a jewel of the shattered kingdoms. Yet, its current state remains unknown—whether it thrives as the capital, stands as a shadow of its former glory, or lies in ruins is a matter of speculation.
