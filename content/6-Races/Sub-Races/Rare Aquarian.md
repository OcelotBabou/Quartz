### 1. **Jellyfolk**

- **Appearance:** Humanoid with a translucent, gelatinous body resembling jellyfish. Their limbs are long and flowing, trailing tendrils and bioluminescent frills.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Bioluminescence:** Can emit light from their body, illuminating dark areas or blinding enemies.
    - **Toxin Touch:** Their touch can paralyze or poison enemies with a gentle caress.
- **Role:** Often serve as healers or sages, using their calming presence and toxin-based abilities to protect their kin.

### 2. **Crabkin**

- **Appearance:** Broad, armored bodies with large pincers instead of hands. Their shells are covered in barnacles and seaweed.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Natural Armor:** Their thick shells provide them with enhanced natural armor, making them difficult to harm.
    - **Crushing Grip:** Their pincers can lock onto a target, dealing significant crushing damage.
- **Role:** Typically act as guardians or warriors, using their resilience and strength to defend their homes.

### 3. **Raylings**

- **Appearance:** Humanoid with large, wing-like fins extending from their arms and back, resembling manta rays. Their skin is smooth and dark, helping them glide through the water.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Glide:** Can glide short distances in water or air, using their fins to maneuver.
    - **Electric Surge:** Can release a burst of electricity, stunning nearby foes.
- **Role:** Often serve as scouts or messengers, using their speed and agility to cover vast distances quickly.

### 4. **Lampyrids**

- **Appearance:** Resembling deep-sea anglerfish, they have bioluminescent lures extending from their foreheads and sharp, needle-like teeth.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Lure:** Can hypnotize or attract prey with their glowing lure.
    - **Shadow Dive:** Can disappear into the shadows of the deep sea, becoming invisible for a short duration.
- **Role:** Act as ambushers or spies, using their lures to draw enemies into traps or to scout unnoticed.

### 5. **Anemonoids**

- **Appearance:** Their bodies are covered in soft, tentacle-like appendages, resembling sea anemones. They can change color to blend into their surroundings.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Chameleon Skin:** Can alter their coloration to become nearly invisible in their environment.
    - **Venomous Tentacles:** Can use their tentacles to sting and poison enemies, causing paralysis or hallucinations.
- **Role:** Often serve as guardians of sacred sites or hidden treasures, using their camouflage and venom to protect their homes.

### 6. **Urchinnes**

- **Appearance:** Small, round bodies covered in sharp spines, similar to sea urchins. Their eyes are beady, and their movements are slow but deliberate.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Spiny Defense:** Enemies that strike them with melee attacks take piercing damage from their spines.
    - **Spike Barrage:** Can shoot their spines as a ranged attack, dealing piercing damage to foes.
- **Role:** Serve as defenders of their communities, using their spines to deter invaders and protect their kin.
