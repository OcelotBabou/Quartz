**ASI:** +2 Cha

**Echolocation:** You can use a bonus action to emit a focused sound wave. For 1 minute, you gain blindsight out to 40 feet and disadvantage on Stealth checks. This ability can be used once per short or long rest.

**Peaceful Presence**: Cetacians exude a calming aura that soothes others. You gain proficiency in one of the following skills: Persuasion, Performance, or Insight. Additionally, you have advantage on checks made to calm or negotiate with creatures of the sea.

**Description:** 
Deep within the crystal-clear waters of the [[Coral Coast]], the Cetacians thrive as the wise and serene diplomats of the Aquarian world. Cetacians are known for their deep intelligence and innate ability to communicate, not just with their own kind, but with a vast array of sea creatures. Their societies are built on cooperation and mutual respect, with a focus on maintaining the delicate balance of their underwater world. The city of [[Corallos]], where the Cetacians primarily dwell, is a hub of knowledge and diplomacy, where disputes are resolved through dialogue and understanding rather than conflict.

Among the Aquarians, the Cetacians are revered as one of the most powerful and influential subraces, standing alongside the formidable Selachians. With their whale subspecies, Cetacians are typically the largest of their kind, their towering presence matched by their imposing physical strength. Yet, their power is not merely a matter of size; Cetacians possess a unique combination of knowledge, agility, and natural charisma that makes them well-rounded leaders and formidable opponents.

This blend of intellectual prowess, physical might, and graceful agility often propels Cetacians to the upper echelons of Aquarian society. Many Cetacians hold positions of royalty or high status, serving as rulers, advisors, or diplomats within their underwater cities. Their wisdom and balanced approach to leadership make them natural choices for guiding their people through the challenges they face, whether in times of peace or conflict.
