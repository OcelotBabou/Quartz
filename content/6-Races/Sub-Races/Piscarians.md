**ASI**: +2 Dex

**School Tactics**: When you are within 10 feet of at least one ally, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on an attack roll made against you by a creature you can see. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses after a long rest.

**Streamlined Swimmer**: Piscarians have a natural affinity for swift movement in water. Their swimming speed is increased by 15 feet.
