**ASI**: +2 Dex

**School Tactics**: When you are within 10 feet of at least one ally, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on an attack roll made against you by a creature you can see. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses after a long rest.

**Streamlined Swimmer**: Piscarians have a natural affinity for swift movement in water. Their swimming speed is increased by 15 feet.

**Description:** 
Known for their agility and versatility, Piscarians navigate the complex currents and intricate ecosystems of their oceanic domain with ease. Piscarians are distinguished by their streamlined forms and vibrant scales, which shimmer in a kaleidoscope of colors. These dynamic and varied forms come in all shapes and sizes, showcasing the subrace's rich genetic diversity. Their physical agility is matched by their social adaptability, making them excellent all-rounders capable of thriving in numerous roles within Aquarian society. From underwater agriculture to running lively taverns, Piscarians form the robust middle class that drives the community's economy and cultural life.

Despite their crucial role, there exists a cultural snobbery from other Aquarian clans, particularly the [[Selachians]], who often view the Piscarians as 'common masses.' However, what Piscarians might lack in prestige, they more than make up for in resourcefulness and communal spirit. Known for their strong sense of teamwork, Piscarians believe in strength in numbers and are adept at mobilising collectively to achieve common goals.

Their ability to adapt quickly to changes and challenges makes them invaluable, to Aquarian society. In times of peace, they maintain the balance and health of their underwater ecosystems, while in conflict, they form the bulk of any Aquarian force.
