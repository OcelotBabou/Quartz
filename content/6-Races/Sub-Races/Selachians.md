**Fearsome Bite:** Your bite attacks deal 1d8 piercing damage, and you gain proficiency with your bite as a weapon.

**Blood Frenzy:** Once per short or long rest, if you see a creature at or below half health within 60 feet, you can enter a frenzy as a bonus action. For 1 minute, you gain advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature that doesn’t have all its hit points. (needs a draw back like must attack nearest enemy)
