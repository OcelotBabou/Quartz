As the revelation of Quawerah's silent ascent into power dawned upon Tyerikst, tension rippled through the cosmic continuum. Tyerikst, enraged by this perceived slight, and the audacity of one the draw power from his divine being approached this mysterious being. Quawerah, still cocooned in the embryonic state of its existence, was a reservoir of potential—a crucible where the spilled essences of battling gods had merged and simmered over eons.

Tyerikst, in its essence a creature of conflict, sought to subdue or perhaps even unmake Quawerah, fearing the change it represented. With a profound surge of will, Quawerah channeled the immense power it had quietly accumulated. This energy, drawn from the remnants of countless battles among the incorporeal gods, was now directed to a singular purpose: the creation of a guardian who could protect the burgeoning material universe. Thus, the matter that had slowly coalesced over unimaginable eons was rapidly pulled together, forming the core of what would become Gahrian.

However, the intense concentration of matter created an unforeseen consequence—a void, a vacuum left by the displaced material energies. As Quawerah imbued Gahrian with vitality and purpose, some of this energy spilled into the void, inadvertently giving rise to a second entity. This was not a mere division of power but a dichotomy born of the universe's fundamental forces: creation and absence, excess and void.

From this dramatic genesis emerged Gahrian, a being of light and excess, whose essence was the radiant energy of creation, ready to expand and nurture the material worlds. Simultaneously, Alache was born —a being of darkness and void, a shadowy counterpart, embodying introspection, reserve, and the necessary balance to Gahrian's boundless enthusiasm.

Tyerikst, ancient and unyielding, found himself facing a new kind of adversary. The duel that ensued was unlike any Tyerikst had engaged in before. Accustomed to the straightforward, brutal clashes that characterized the eternal conflict among the incorporeal gods, Tyerikst was ill-prepared for the nuanced tactics and synergistic harmony of Gahrian and Alache.

Tyerikst, though mighty, found himself outmatched, his raw power, formidable against a singular opponent, was dispersed and diluted by their combined efforts. The battle, while fierce, quickly tipped in favor of Gahrian and Alache. The climax of the battle saw Tyerikst, exhausted and overwhelmed, finally succumb to the combined might of the new guardians. 

As he fell, the immense energy that constituted his being—the same energy that had fueled his existence and his endless battles—was not dissipated into the cosmos as it had always been. Instead, Gahrian and Alache, acting with deliberate intent, harnessed this energy directly. They absorbed it, channeling Tyerikst’s vast power into themselves, and through themselves so into Quawerah.
