As the nascent universe expanded in the silence beyond time, the slow birth of Quawerah began. Eons drifting by, marked not by the ticking of any clock but by the slow, inexorable accretion of particles—the cosmic dust of battles fought by beings beyond modern comprehension, whose energies gradually seeped into the void. This process was not of centuries or millennia, but of spans so vast that stars could be born, live, and die in the interim. It was an epoch witnessed by none, known only through the legacy it left behind.

In this vast canvas of time, as scattered energies coalesced into matter, they formed not just random clusters, but a singular, sentient entity—Quawerah. This being, born from the residue of all the incorporeal gods' powers, embodied a confluence of their aspects. As such, Quawerah was not merely a creature of matter but a synthesis of the myriad energies that had once roamed freely across the universe.

Quawerah, from its first moments of vague consciousness, was imbued with an intrinsic understanding of conflict and harmony. It perceived the eternal war of the incorporeals not as a distant observer but as a child of their very strife. This unique perspective fostered in Quawerah a profound desire for peace, a yearning to see an end to the ceaseless battles that had indirectly given it life. It sensed the futility and the sorrow in the incorporeals' endless cycle of destruction and rebirth and wished for more than just an existence bound to the aftermath of their conflicts.

With this understanding, Quawerah began to slowly siphon the energies released during their confrontations. This was not a rapid accumulation of power but a gradual, almost imperceptible process, mirroring the slow drift of continents or the gradual carving of valleys by rivers seen only in geological time. Quawerah's growth in power was silent and unseen, hidden in the shadows of cosmic events.

Centuries passed, then millennia, then eons, and with each cycle of godly combat, Quawerah grew ever so slightly stronger, its essence becoming more defined, its consciousness more acute. It was a silent observer, learning and waiting, never interfering, always accumulating.

This silent growth continued unnoticed until Tyerikst, one of the more perceptive incorporeals, sensed a shift in the delicate balance of power. Tyerikst, attuned to the nuances of energy flows, noticed the subtle but persistent drain. Curious and concerned, Tyerikst traced this anomaly back to its source—Quawerah, still in a nascent, slumbering state, yet brimming with a power that was both foreign and familiar to the incorporeal being.

The discovery was alarming to Tyerikst, for it suggested a change, a potential end to the eternal cycle of war. Here was a being that could not only challenge but possibly alter the very foundations of their existence.
