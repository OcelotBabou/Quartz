As Gahrian reveled in the newfound power and influence of Matiodox, Alache observed the unfolding events with growing concern. The creation of Matiodox, a being of pure pride and vanity, marked a significant shift in the cosmic balance, one that Alache felt compelled to address. Duty-bound and ever vigilant of the universe's stability, Alache saw his brother's actions not just as worrying sign of Gahrians mental trajectory, but as a potential threat to the harmonious order they were tasked to uphold.

Understanding the need to counterbalance both the imbalance to power Gahrian had introduced, and the overwhelming pride introduced by Matiodox, he conceived his own Vice, one that would embody the antithesis of pride: Doubt. Following the procedure established by Gahrian, Alache created the second vice, Fivolam, the Vice of Doubt. This new entity was designed not only to counteract Matiodox’s influence but also to introduce a necessary caution and questioning into the cosmos, a way to temper Gahrian’s unchecked expansions. However, as with all vices, composed of singular traits taken to extremes, Fivolam's presence introduced its own challenges. Doubt, while a counter to blind pride, could also stifle decision-making and foster hesitation where action is needed.

The creation of Fivolam did not sit well with Gahrian. He perceived Alache's actions as an affront, an implication from his perceived inferior brother that his decisions needed correction. Worse yet, the successful creation of Fivolam suggested that Alache might indeed be Gahrian’s equal. This realization not only wounded Gahrian’s pride but also ignited a competitive streak within him. 

In a bid to reassert his dominance and fueled by the slight to his pride, Gahrian created another Vice, Pesmin, the Vice of Spite. Yet Alache would not back down so easily, desperate to maintain the delicate balance between them. He responded in kind, initiating a sequence of creation where each Vice by Gahrian was met with a calculated counter by Alache, each set reflecting their diverging approaches to power and control.

When Gahrian brought forth Spatalos (Profligate), embodying reckless extravagance, Alache responded with Listunos (Greed). To Gahrian's Paysius (Flagrance), Alache introduced Chyntun (Shame). To Gahrian's Aperkepsi (Rashness), Alache balanced with Pathupem (Apathy). 

Finally, pushed to his wit's end by the relentless tit-for-tat and the escalating intensity of their creations, Gahrian channeled his mounting frustration and fury into the creation of Ormos, the Vice of Rage. This new Vice was a raw expression of Gahrian’s own tempestuous emotions, a powerful entity bursting with the uncontrollable urge to act and react, mirroring the chaos that had begun to consume him.

Exhausted and increasingly dismayed by the spiral of escalation they had become trapped in, Alache witnessed his brother's latest creation with a heavy heart. The relentless cycle of action and counteraction, once begun as a means to maintain cosmic balance, had instead led them down a path of mutual destruction and resentment. In a profound moment of weariness, Alache’s deep-seated despair and disillusionment began to seep into the fabric of the cosmos. This intense outpouring of emotion, unique in its rawness and depth, coursed through the increasingly unstable universe, inadvertently seeking out one of the few remaining incorporeals.

From this intense and unintended convergence, Lepsis was born. This new Vice was not merely a product of divine will but a somber reflection of the cosmos’s fraying edges, a stark embodiment of the inevitable consequences of their unchecked rivalries.

Lepsis stood as a poignant symbol of the looming doom that could follow if the gods continued on their current path. More than any other Vice, it brought with it a palpable sense of finality and a potent reminder of the heavy emotional and cosmic toll their actions had exacted. The creation of Lepsis marked a turning point, serving as a bleak testament to what their once-noble efforts had become: a series of actions leading inexorably towards potential ruin.
