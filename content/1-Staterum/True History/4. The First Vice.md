With Tyerikst defeated and their power significantly augmented, Gahrian and Alache set upon their monumental task: to bring an end to the eternal conflict of the incorporeals. Armed with new strengths and a direct mandate from Quawerah, they embarked on a mission that was as daunting as it was crucial for the future of the cosmos.

Gahrian, ever the embodiment of excess, approached this task with zeal and growing confidence. Each encounter with the incorporeals not only tested his abilities but also reinforced his belief in his own invincibility. As they progressed, Alache, more introspective and vigilant, maintained a strategic approach, ensuring that their actions remained balanced and that the energy absorbed from each defeated incorporeal was evenly and dutifully,  distributed amongst the three Gods, to prevent any one aspect from overwhelming their combined purpose.

The process, was no small task, yet it began to show signs of acceleration as Gahrian and Alache grew adept at their roles. The once formidable incorporeals, so used to an endless cycle of unchallenged existence, found themselves outmatched by the synergistic tactics of the twin guardians. One by one, they fell, their energies harvested and repurposed to fortify the material realm and empower Quawerah further.

As Gahrian and Alache continued their divine mission, Gahrian's confidence swelled with each victory. His ambition, always a facet of his radiant persona, now pushed him towards a more assertive approach. He believed that they were powerful enough to confront the incorporeals individually, and that by doing so, they could significantly hasten the resolution of their cosmic task. Driven by this burgeoning pride and a fervent desire to demonstrate his prowess, Gahrian proposed a bold new tactic: to split their efforts and tackle the incorporeals one on one.

Setting off alone, Gahrian sought to test his mettle against the incorporeals in singular combat. This solo endeavor marked a significant shift in their methodical approach, introducing a new phase of their campaign where speed and individual strength took precedence. Gahrian's initial encounters were fraught with challenges, but each victory only fed his confidence.

It was during this period of bold and solitary confrontations that Gahrian faced Toukproy, an incorporeal whose existence would have faded into the obscurity of the cosmos had it not been for Gahrian's consequential decision. As Gahrian overpowered Toukproy, the incorporeal, fearing its imminent dissolution, pleaded for mercy. Gahrian, influenced by his own burgeoning pride and the persuasive pleas of Toukproy, conceived a grandiose idea. Rather than simply absorb Toukproy’s energy, Gahrian decided to transform it, to create from it a reflection of his own power and personality.

This act of creation was a profound demonstration of Gahrian’s capabilities and a stark manifestation of his vanity. He poured a part of his essence into Toukproy, however, the essence imparted was not balanced. The pride that had been swelling unchecked within Gahrian, poured forth into this new vessel, twisting Toukproy into the first Vice: Matiodox, the Vice of Pride. 

Matiodox emerged not merely as a reflection of Gahrian's pride; these qualities were distilled and intensified, resulting in a being of pure pride and vanity. Designed to amplify and propagate Gahrian’s influence, Matiodox was imbued with the drive to expand and assert the dominance of its creator’s will across the cosmos. With this new power, a formidable entity was born into the material plane, heralding the beginning of a new era.
