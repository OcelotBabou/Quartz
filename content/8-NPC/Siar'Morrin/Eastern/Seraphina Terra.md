*[sɛr'æfɪnə 'tɛrə]*

**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Seraphina Terra
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Female
- **Age**: 34 years
- **Occupation**: Co-ruler of Pistos
- **Affiliation**: Diarchy of Pistos
- **Home Region**: [[Pistos]]

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Lady Seraphina Terra exudes an elegant and serene aura that commands respect. She is often attired in flowing robes that reflect the traditional styles of Pistos, intricately embroidered with symbols of the [[Church of Statera]]. Seraphina is tall and slender, with long, flowing chestnut hair that cascades in loose waves down her back. Her skin is fair, often giving her a luminescent quality under the ceremonial lights during religious festivals. Her eyes, a deep shade of hazel, reflect a calm and thoughtful nature, and her gentle smile is both comforting and inspiring to those she leads.  
- **Height**: 178 cm
- **Weight**: 66 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: Seraphina's soft features are accentuated by her warm, inviting smile and the compassionate gaze that reflects her deep spiritual connection.

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Compassionate, approachable, and deeply spiritual.
- **Strengths**: Exceptional diplomacy, genuine empathy towards her people, and a strong spiritual leadership.
- **Weaknesses**: Her open-hearted nature sometimes leaves her vulnerable to political machinations.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To foster a thriving, united community within Pistos and to promote peace across the Shattered Kingdoms.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Born into the ruling Terra family of Pistos, Seraphina was sent to live in [[Vitaurum]] with her distant relative [[Albus Terra]].
- **Education**: Educated both in Vitaurum, attending Marcatura academy as well as receiving private tutoring at the Grand Temple of Statera, under [[Konrad Mercina]]. 
- **Key Events**: Rose to co-leadership under the diarchy system after her father's passing, alongside her brother Malcard, to balance the kingdom's spiritual and strategic needs.
- **Relationships**: Seraphina is deeply cherished by her people for her warmth and approachability. She shares a strong and supportive relationship with her brother, [[Malcard Terra]], relying on his attention to logistics and strategy to allow her to focus wholeheartedly on the well-being and needs of the populace the knowledge that she can focus on the peoples needs while her brother tends to logistics. 

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Like Malcard, Seraphina relies on her magical prowess for defence. Her strong ties to the Gods offers her surprising and powerful magical capabilities. 
- **Technical Skills**: Skilled in negotiation and fluent in several languages, enabling effective communication with various diplomats and leaders.
- **Special Abilities**: Possesses an innate ability to sense the emotional and spiritual needs of others, enhancing her diplomatic effectiveness.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: None. She prefers the power of words and diplomacy over force.
- **Gadgets**: Carries artifacts and symbols that represent her spiritual beliefs, which she uses in various rituals and public ceremonies.
- **Personal Belongings**: A pendant containing water from the Star pools in the [[Temple of the Lost God]], a gift from Konrad, symbolising her powerful connection to the Gods.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Seraphina’s leadership has been instrumental in maintaining the cultural and spiritual fabric of Pistos, fostering resilience amidst external pressures and internal challenges.
- **Important Missions**: Continuously engaged in peace-building efforts, particularly focused on mediating the escalating conflict between [[Vesae]] and [[Avatolika]], striving to prevent it from destabilising the region further.

**Quotes**

"In the hearts of our people lies the true strength of Pistos."
