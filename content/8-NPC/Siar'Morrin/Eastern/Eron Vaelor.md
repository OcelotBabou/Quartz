### Basic Information

- **Name**: Eron Vaelor
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 68 years
- **Occupation**: Legatus of the [[Biranian Guard]]
- **Affiliation**: New Biranian Empire
- **Home Region**: New Birania

### Physical Description

- **Appearance**: Eron is an elderly man with a lean, slightly stooped frame that hints at a once more muscular and formidable physique. His face is marked by deep lines and a stern expression, softened slightly by the gray at his temples and beard.
- **Height**: 182 cm 
- **Weight**: 78 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: Eron often wears the distinguished uniform of the Biranian Guard, adorned with medals and insignias from past campaigns. His piercing blue eyes reflect a lifetime of command and the burden of leadership.

### Personality

- **Traits**: Disciplined, wise, and no-nonsense. Eron has a commanding presence that demands respect. He is known for his strategic mind and unwavering commitment to New Birania.
- **Strengths**: Experienced military strategist, highly respected leader, and adept at managing complex defense operations.
- **Weaknesses**: His advanced age sometimes slows him down physically, and his impatience with incompetence can make him seem harsh.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To safeguard New Birania’s borders, mentor the next generation of military leaders, and ensure the security and stability of his homeland.

### Background

- **Early Life**: Born into a family with a strong military tradition, Eron was raised in the disciplined environment of New Birania’s military elite.
- **Education**: Educated at the military academy in Vitaurum, where he excelled in tactics and leadership.
- **Key Events**: Rose through the ranks to become Legatus, led several critical campaigns to secure New Birania's borders, and played a key role in securing the road through the bandit riddled hills of leading to [[Pilliga]].
- **Relationships**: Respected by his peers and subordinates, though his demanding nature can be intimidating. He has mentored many younger officers who now hold significant positions within the military. Remains close friends with King [[Alaric Terra]], whom he personally trained. 

### Skills and Abilities

- **Combat Skills**: Skilled in both hand-to-hand combat and strategic warfare, though now more involved in strategic planning than active combat.
- **Technical Skills**: Expert in military logistics, defense strategy, and emergency response.
- **Special Abilities**: Has an almost intuitive grasp of both enemy tactics and navigating the complex world of subterfuge.

### Equipment

- **Weapons**: Carries a well-maintained long sword that has seen many battles, and a ornate shield, marred by numerous scars.
- **Gadgets**: Uses military-grade tactical gear and communication devices to stay connected with his units.

### Significant Events

- **Major Plot Points**: Eron Vaelor played a pivotal role in restoring stability to the Shattered Kingdoms during the 'Schism War.' As the Legatus of the [[Biranian Guard]], he led his forces to quell the rampant infighting and re-establish a semblance of peace within the Shattered Kingdoms. 
- **Important Missions**: Currently focuses on the training and deployment of the Biranian Guard to counter rising threats along the eastern frontiers.
