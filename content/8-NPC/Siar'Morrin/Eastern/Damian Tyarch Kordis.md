*[deɪ.mi.ən taɪ.ɑrk kɔr.dɪs]*

**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Damian Tyarch Kordis
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 47 years
- **Occupation**: Dominarch of Neodoulas
- **Affiliation**: Dominion Assembly of Neodoulas
- **Home Region**: [[Neodoulas]]

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Damian Tyarch Kordis is a tall and imposing figure, with a weathered face that reflects the harshness of his homeland. His hair, thin and wispy, is carefully maintained in a slicked-back manner. His eyes are deep-set under a permanently furrowed brow, adding to his stern demeanor. Typically seen wearing a heavy black cloak that stretches' the length of his body. 
- **Height**: 192 cm
- **Weight**: 85 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: Notable for his piercing gaze and a heavy trench coat. 

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Calculating, pragmatic, and resilient.
- **Strengths**: Exceptional strategic acumen, adept in diplomacy and crisis management.
- **Weaknesses**: His ruthlessness in policy-making can alienate allies and provoke dissent among his people.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To fortify Neodoulas’ position as a dominant economic and military power while managing the delicate balance of internal stability and external threats.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Born into a family of influential traders in [[Doulas]], Damian was steeped in the politics and economics of trade from a young age. 
- **Education**: Educated by private tutors in the arts of leadership, economics, and military strategy.
- **Key Events**: Damian was a pivotal figure in the separation of Neodoulas, orchestrating critical economic strategies from behind the scenes to ensure the new kingdom's viability post-independence. Following the ousting of his predecessor for blatant corruption, Damian ascended to the position of Dominarch, seen as the natural choice. 
- **Relationships**: Damian maintains a network of loyalists who respect his leadership style, though his relationships with international leaders are marked by caution and respect born of his reputation for cunning.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Skilled in strategic combat planning rather than direct combat; relies on his military commanders to implement his strategies.
- **Technical Skills**: Expert in economic manipulation and resource management.
- **Special Abilities**: Has a keen sense for exploiting political and economic trends to benefit Neodoulas.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Rarely carries more than a ceremonial dagger, confident in his guards ability to provide protection instead.
- **Gadgets**: Utilises sending stones to maintain tight control over his domain. As well as other crueler gadgets used to keep 'forced laborer's in their place. 
- **Personal Belongings**: His heavy duty trench cloak, said to be made from the hide of a displacer beast. 

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Damian's ascension as Dominarch marked a pivotal shift in Neodoulas' trajectory, redirecting its focus from territorial establishment to aggressive economic exploitation and strategic trade. 
- **Important Missions**: Played a crucial role behind the scenes in the lead-up to Neodoulas' separation, ensuring the continuity of economic and military resources. Recently, he masterfully orchestrated an ongoing and lucrative trade agreement with Fort Logos, bolstering Neodoulas' military capabilities and securing its economic future.

**Quotes**

"Power is not given; it is taken and held by those with the will to dictate their own destiny."
