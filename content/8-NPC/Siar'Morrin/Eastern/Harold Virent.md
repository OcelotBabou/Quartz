### Basic Information

- **Name**: Harold Virent
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 70 years
- **Occupation**: Viceroy of Zakrada
- **Affiliation**: New Biranian Empire
- **Home Region**: New Birania

### Physical Description

- **Appearance**: Harold presents an authoritative figure, with silver hair and a well-maintained beard. His posture is slightly stooped, betraying his age, but his demeanor remains dignified.
- **Height**: 178 cm (5'10")
- **Weight**: 75 kg (165 lbs)
- **Distinguishing Features**: Harold is often seen with a cane, although he claims this a symbol of his status rather than a necessity.  

### Personality

- **Traits**: Honorable, dedicated, but increasingly perceived as out of touch with the current realities of his city.
- **Strengths**: Strong leadership skills, deeply committed to the welfare of Zakrada and its citizens.
- **Weaknesses**: Signs of cognitive decline are beginning to impair his decision-making; struggles with the evolving complexities of urban management.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To secure Zakrada’s prosperity and maintain its significance within New Birania, despite growing challenges.

### Background

- **Early Life**: Harold Virent's origins trace back to the vibrant streets of [[Vitaurum]], where he was born into a distinguished noble family. During his formative years, he formed a close friendship with [[Albus Terra]]. Both scions of prominent families, they grew up sharing a deep camaraderie and a rigorous education tailored for future leaders.
- **Education**: Educated at Mercatura Academy, a renowned academy in Vitaurum, focusing on law, governance and commerce.
- **Key Events**: Has served as Viceroy of Zakrada for over four decades, overseeing periods of peace and prosperity as well as times of strife.
- **Relationships**: Respected by many for his long service, though some newer voices in politics question his current effectiveness. 

### Skills and Abilities

- **Combat Skills**: Minimal; Harold's experience is primarily in administration and governance.
- **Technical Skills**: Skilled in bureaucratic management, legal structuring, and crisis resolution.
- **Special Abilities**: Possesses a keen ability to mediate disputes and forge compromises among diverse political factions.

### Equipment

- **Weapons**: Rarely carries a weapon; relies on his security detail for physical protection.
- **Gadgets**: Uses various administrative tools and has access to advanced communication devices to manage city affairs.
- **Personal Belongings**: A signet ring gifted to him by former King [[Albus Terra]]. 

### Significant Events

- **Major Plot Points**: Harold's leadership was tested during the '[[Schism Wars]],' however, received much praise for his benevolence and acceptance towards refugees fleeing the [[Shattered Kingdoms]].
- **Important Missions**: Currently focuses on curbing the rising crime rates and addressing the infiltration of cult activities within Zakrada.

### Quotes

"The true measure of our city’s strength lies in how we face our darkest times together."
