**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Idris Elara
- **Species**: Elf
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: Over 1000 years (exact age unknown)
- **Occupation**: Keeper of the Temple of the Lost God
- **Affiliation**: Temple of the Lost God
- **Home Region**: New Birania

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Idris Elara presents an ageless visage that belies the millennia he has witnessed. His features are finely chiseled, with an ethereal quality that reflects his elven heritage. His hair is a silvery white that cascades to his shoulders, framing his face in a halo of wisdom.
- **Height**: 164 cm 
- **Weight**: 65 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: Idris's eyes hold a depth like ancient forests, shimmering with a mix of green and gold. His posture is impeccable, carrying the grace of the elves with the gravity of his years.

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Mysterious, profoundly knowledgeable, contemplative.
- **Strengths**: Vast understanding of religious lore and divine history, enigmatic wisdom that guides his guardianship of the temple.
- **Weaknesses**: His detachment from worldly concerns and cryptic manner can make him seem inaccessible or aloof.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To preserve the knowledge and sanctity of the Temple of the Lost God, ensuring that the wisdom of the past is not forgotten amid the tumults of the present.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Born in an era long passed, Idris’s early life is shrouded in the mists of history. He has lived through epochs and watched empires rise and fall, becoming a repository of forgotten lore and divine secrets.
- **Education**: Unknown
- **Key Events**: Ascending to the role of Keeper was a pivotal moment for Idris, chosen for reasons known only to the innermost circles of the temple. His life since has been dedicated to the guardianship of sacred knowledge and the mysteries of the divine.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: While not a warrior, Idris is closely aligned with [[The Old Gods]] and capable of channeling their power to devastating effect. 
- **Technical Skills**: Master of religious rituals and ancient ceremonies.
- **Special Abilities**: Unknown, but undoubtedly powerful.  

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: None.
- **Gadgets**: Uses artifacts and relics of the temple to perform rituals and protect the sacred grounds.
- **Personal Belongings**: Carries a tome, always at his side, filled with scriptures and prophecies that are incomprehensible to most.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: His tenure as the Keeper has seen the Temple of the Lost God turn into a pilgrimage site for those seeking ancient wisdom or divine intervention.
- **Important Missions**: Idris's most enduring task is the maintenance of the temple's archives and relics, protecting them from decay and the ravages of time.

**Quotes**

"In the silence of the temple, the gods whisper still, if one only knows how to listen."

**History**

Idris Elara is more than just the Keeper; he is a living bridge to the past, a custodian of truths that many believe were lost to time. His leadership at the temple has made it a beacon for scholars and the faithful alike, drawing those who seek understanding of the divine and the mystical underpinnings of the world.
