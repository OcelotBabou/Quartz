### Basic Information

- **Name**: **Name**:
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 38 years
- **Occupation**: Lord and Council Leader 
- **Affiliation**: Avatolika
- **Home Region**: [[Avatolika]]

### Physical Description

- **Appearance**: Fionn is a commanding presence, with a towering stature typical of the Avatolika people. His skin is sun-touched and tanned, bearing the marks of many years spent under the open sky. His features are rugged, with a stern yet charismatic expression and his body bears the scars from numerous fights.
- **Height**: 195 cm 
- **Weight**: 109 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: His imposing physical presence and the distinct blue stone bangle he is always seen wearing. 

### Personality

- **Traits**: Charismatic, pragmatic, and decisively strategic.
- **Strengths**: Natural leadership ability and combat prowess make him both a respected and feared figure.
- **Weaknesses**: His open distain of [[Vasae]] makes some question is objectivity in leading Avatolika, particularly with the potential for war looming.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To maintain the independence and stability of Avatolike. He would love nothing more than to see the smug Vasaeans crushed under his armies.

### Background

- **Early Life**: Born into the nobility of Avatolika, Fionn was raised to value strength and leadership. His upbringing was marked by rigorous training in both governance and combat.
- **Education**: Trained martially in Avatolika, but educated by the finest tutors in [[Pistos]], a luxury rarely afforded to the people of Avatolika. 
- **Key Events**: Fionn Domhail's rise to prominence was marked by his strategic leadership at Barrenhold, where he organized defences against incursions from the west. He not only fortified the city but also led daring campaigns to dismantle a large warband of remnant demons that had long been targeting the outskirts of Avatolika. 
- **Relationships**: Widely respected among his peers in the council, he has formed a particularly strong friendship with Laufi Haolan having fought in Barrenhold side by side.  

### Skills and Abilities

- **Combat Skills**: An accomplished warrior, skilled in both hand-to-hand combat and strategic warfare.
- **Technical Skills**: Skilled in diplomacy and governance, with a keen understanding of political manoeuvring. Although he tends to pursue the simplest and most direction options. 
- **Special Abilities**: His charismatic leadership naturally rallies people to his cause, enhancing his effectiveness as a leader.

### Equipment

- **Weapons**: Typically carries three swords, showcasing his proficiency in dual swordsmanship. He keeps a spare, more ornate sword ready in case one is lost during battle.
- **Gadgets**: None.
- **Personal Belongings**: Wears a distinctive bangle made of stone beads in various shades of blue, a cherished gift from a [[naiad]] of [[Lake Serenith]].

### Significant Events

- **Major Plot Points**: Successfully led the defence of Avatolika’s western borders. Is currently preparing the region for what he views as the inevitable confrontation with Vasae. 
- **Important Missions**:Re-establishing Avatolika rule of Star Fall Island, and finding an economic way forward for the region following the harsh tariffs placed upon it. 
### Quotes
