- **Name**: Miranda Fleet

- **Affiliation**: Independent traders
- **Home Region**: [[Neodoulas]] 

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: 

Miranda is a tall, imposing figure with a sharp gaze and an air of authority. Her dark hair, always meticulously styled in a sleek, tight pullback, accentuates the sharp contours of her face and her piercing, calculating eyes. Her complexion is pale, often giving her a stark contrast against the darker attire she prefers. 

Her wardrobe features professionally tailored dark jackets, paired with crisply pressed shirts and form-fitting trousers. While these clothes sacrifice some practicality, they exude an air of sophistication and pomposity, underlining her foreboding presence and meticulous attention to detail.  
- **Height**: 178 cm
- **Weight**: 65 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: Always seen with her personal guard, a mute giant who shadows her every move

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Calculating, secretive, and commanding
- **Strengths**: Sharp business acumen, excellent strategist, formidable negotiator
- **Weaknesses**: Her secretive nature and illicit activities might isolate her; lacks trust in others
- **Goals and Motivations**: Acting as an envoy for a wealthy benefactor in Neodoulas, she seeks to secure trade deals with Agroma. 


**Background**

Miranda's demeanor is marked by a cold efficiency and a keen sense of control over her surroundings. She interacts with others in a manner that is both direct and distant, keeping most at arm’s length while managing to maintain healthy relations with the other merchants. 

Her distance and lack of interactions with those she doesn't see as directly beneficial makes it hard to get a grasp on her motivations her purpose. However, there is one exception that has piqued Miranda's interest – a rumored Kitsune. Uncharacteristically, she has shown a keen interest in getting to know [[Jondo]], employing a charm and engagement that she seldom displays.
