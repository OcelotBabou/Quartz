**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Jane Doe
- **Gender**: Female
- **Age**: 28

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Jane has an understated elegance, her long black hair meticulously tied back to reveal the sharp intelligence in her eyes, magnified slightly by her glasses. Despite her short stature, she carries herself with a quiet confidence that is as inviting as it is professional. Her face is gentle and warm, with a soft smile that she seems to perpetually wear. She is rarely if ever seen out of her guild uniform, comprised of a crisp blouses paired with a smart vest, almost resembling a butlers attire. Her overall demeanor and style create a harmony of professionalism and warmth, inviting confidence and conversation from those who pass through the guild’s doors.

![[Jane Doe.webp]]

- **Height**: 165 cm
- **Weight**: 58 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: Her glasses are not just a vision aid but also serve as a subtle accessory that complements her organized and meticulous nature.

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Friendly, professional, and disarming. Jane has a natural charm that makes her easy to talk to, and she uses this to her advantage in gathering information.
- **Strengths**: Excellent communicator, perceptive, and adept at handling confidential information discreetly.
- **Weaknesses**: Her dual life can sometimes lead to conflicts of interest, and maintaining her cover requires constant vigilance which can be mentally taxing.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To gather vital information for the Biranian Guard while maintaining her cover at the guild. She aims to contribute to the safety and security of her region through her intelligence work.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Grew up in Hu Dao, familiar with local dynamics and the community, which aids in her role as both a receptionist and informant.
- **Education**: Received a formal education in communication and espionage, secretly trained by the Biranian Guard in intelligence operations.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Social Skills**: Expert at social engineering, able to engage people in conversations that reveal more than they intend.
- **Intellectual Skills**: Quick thinker, adept at analyzing and synthesizing information from casual chats.
- **Special Abilities**: Trained in coded communication and surveillance techniques to pass on information without arousing suspicion.

**Equipment**

- **Tools**: Utilizes a variety of hidden recording devices and encrypted communication tools to relay information securely.
- **Personal Belongings**: Always carries a notebook disguised as an appointment log, where she records coded notes on interactions that might be of interest to the Biranian Guard.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Successfully thwarted a potential threat to Hu Dao by uncovering and relaying information about a rogue group plotting within the Adventurers Guild.
- **Important Missions**: Regularly provides intelligence reports to the Biranian Guard, playing a crucial role in preemptive security measures and strategic planning.

**Relationships**

- Maintains professional relationships at the guild but holds a deep-seated loyalty to the Biranian Guard. Her dual role remains a closely guarded secret, known only to her direct contacts within the Guard.
