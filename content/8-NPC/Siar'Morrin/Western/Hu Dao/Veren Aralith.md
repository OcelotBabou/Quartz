**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Veren Aralith
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 38
- **Occupation**: Triarch of Commerce
- **Affiliation**: [[Hu Dao]]
- **Home Region**: [[Agroma]]

**Appearance**

Veren Aralith stands tall with a very slender build, his physical presence marked more by his attire than his stature. His head is bald, and his face is clean-shaven, enhancing the sharpness of his features and the perpetual tiredness visible under his eyes. Despite this weariness, Veren maintains a meticulous appearance, dressing in professional and upscale clothing that signifies his status and wealth. His suits are well-tailored, often in dark, rich colors that convey both authority and sophistication.

**Background**

From what you have heard en route to Hu Dao, Veren Aralith is a figure of significant economic influence within the city, known for his shrewd negotiating skills and a keen eye for business opportunities. His rise to the position of Triarch of Commerce is a topic of mixed opinions; while some commend his wealth as a testament to his suitability for the role, others whisper that his position was bought rather than earned. Despite these rumors, Veren is undeniably dedicated to his role, spending most of his time managing and expanding his numerous business ventures.

His involvement in trade and commerce leaves him perpetually tired, yet he is invigorated by the possibilities of the upcoming festival. Veren sees the event not just as a celebration but as a strategic opportunity to cement Hu Dao's status as a commercial hub. He envisions the festival becoming a tradition that will attract merchants and traders from far and wide, providing ample opportunities to boost his ventures and expand his economic reach.
