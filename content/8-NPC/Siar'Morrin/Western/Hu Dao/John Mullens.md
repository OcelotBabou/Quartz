**Appearance**

John presents a composed and serious demeanor, befitting his role as the de facto leader among the trio at The Drunken Weasel. He has a slender build, with a bit of a hunch and slouched shoulders. His hair is neatly kept in a centre part, and his attire typically consists of a smart waistcoat over a clean shirt, paired with durable trousers—practical for his managerial duties. His expression often carries a mixture of seriousness, annoyance and tiredness, reflective of his constant need to correct his idiot brothers errors.

**Background**

As the oldest and most composed of the trio, John is perceived as the leader at The Drunken Weasel. While he may not always be seen behind the bar, his presence is integral to the smooth operation of the tavern. He tends to focus on the administrative and organizational aspects of the business, ensuring everything runs seamlessly from behind the scenes. However, when serious situations occur, his brothers do not hesitate to call on him, trusting his judgment and ability to manage crises effectively. While he is the most put-together and capable of the group, his leadership often involves corralling the misadventures and mishaps caused by Jacob and Jack. Despite his attempts at maintaining professionalism, his own bumbling tendencies occasionally surface, especially when he's drawn into the brothers' shenanigans.

Rumors abound regarding the true nature of the relationship between John and his so-called brothers, Jacob and Jack, hinting that they may not be actual brothers. Despite these speculations, the trio displays a close-knit, albeit chaotic, dynamic. Their interactions, marked by harsh words and frequent scuffles, suggest a deep, albeit unconventional, bond.
