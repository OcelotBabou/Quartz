**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Jacob Mullens
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 21
- **Occupation**: Barman, Security, and General Helper
- **Affiliation**: The Drunken Weasel
- **Home Region**: Hu Dao

**Appearance**

Jacob Mullens is the largest of the trio, his broad and robust frame , suited to his role as security. Despite his youth, his rough, mature features make him appear much older, often surprising those who learn his actual age. He is completely bald, adding to the seasoned look that contrasts sharply with his actual experience. While his size and appearance could be intimidating, Jacob carries a persistently friendly demeanor and a warm expression that softens his tough exterior—except perhaps when engaged in the frequent squabbles of his brothers. His attire is the epitome of simplicity and practicality, chosen for durability and comfort over style.

**Background**

Jacob Mullens, despite being the youngest, carries a multitude of responsibilities at The Drunken Weasel. He is the primary barman, tasked with serving drinks and engaging with patrons, who find his friendly nature and ready smile the most welcoming of the trio. Beyond tending the bar, Jacob also doubles as the tavern’s security, his formidable size and strength making him well-suited to handle any disturbances.

Jacob's endearing personality and readiness to help with any task—no matter how unglamorous—make him particularly popular among the tavern's regulars. However, his low intelligence and natural clumsiness sometimes lead to mishaps, causing minor chaos in an otherwise smooth operation. These incidents, are a source of extreme frustration for his brothers, who are always ready to drop what they are doing to reprimand him.
