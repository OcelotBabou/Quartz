*[roh-nan storm-sing-er]*

**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Ronan Stormsinger
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 52 years
- **Occupation**: High Priest of [[Rezza Oir]]
- **Affiliation**: Temple of Rezza Oir
- **Home Region**: Portum

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Ronan has an imposing presence, with stormy gray hair that matches his name and a long, flowing beard.His eyes are a striking blue, echoing the clear sky on a serene day. He wears light gray robes adorned with patterns that mimic whirling winds, elegantly spreading outward along the fabric.
- **Height**: 187 cm 
- **Weight**: 80 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: Carries a staff topped with an emblem of a wind spiral.

![[Ronana.webp]]


**Personality**

- **Traits**: Reverent, charismatic, and deeply contemplative.
- **Strengths**: Powerful orator, spiritually insightful, and highly respected by the community for his wisdom and calming presence.
- **Weaknesses**: Ronan's unyielding optimism and boundless positivity, while uplifting to many, can occasionally wear thin on some residents of Portum, especially those worn down by generations of hardship. 
- **Goals and Motivations**: To guide and uplift the spirits of Seagate's populace through spiritual teachings, and to invoke Rezza Oir’s blessings for the restoration and protection of the community.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Raised by a family of fishermen, Ronan felt a spiritual calling from a young age, drawn to the tempestuous beauty of the sea and the lore of the winds.
- **Education**: Trained at the seminary of Rezza Oir, where he excelled in theological studies and ancient history.
- **Key Events**: Became High Priest after his predecessor’s passing during a particularly fierce storm, which many in Seagate saw as a divine sign.
- **Relationships**: Holds a pivotal role within the community, acting as both spiritual advisor and counselor to many.  His closest ally and friend is [[Eldrin Starwatcher]], with the two often discussing religion and ancient history. While he loces and is loved by much of Seagate, he is particularly close to [[Orla Sartosa]], with many attributing his influence being the only thing that kept her from a darker future. 

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Limited to ceremonial rites, though he possesses knowledge of defensive spells that call upon the power of winds and storms.
- **Technical Skills**: Proficient in spiritual rituals, capable of performing complex ceremonies that are said to influence weather and sea conditions.
- **Special Abilities**: Believed to have the ability to communicate directly with Rezza Oir, receiving visions that guide his decisions and actions.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: His staff, which serves more as a symbol of his office and a tool for channeling elemental energy.
- **Gadgets**: Utilizes various ritualistic items, such as chimes and orbs, to enhance his spiritual ceremonies.
- **Personal Belongings**: His staff passed down from the previous high priest. 

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: His leadership was instrumental in calming the populace following [[Toren Blackwater]]'s failed attempt to retake [[Watcher’s Isle]]. His obvious connection to Rezza Oir is also believed to be essential should Portum ever return to it's seafaring ways. 
- **Important Missions**: Actively involved in a project to build a series of beacons along the coast, which are intended to serve as warning systems, spiritual symbols of guidance as well as shrines dedicated to Rezza Oir.
