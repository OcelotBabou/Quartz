**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: 

Tiana carries the unmistakable grace and elegance of her Lux Elf heritage. Her skin possesses a natural luminescence, subtly glowing like the early morning light filtering through dew-kissed leaves. While her petite frame wrapped in an aura of youthful vitality and boundless energy. 

Tiana’s hair, a striking shade of silver, flows freely down her back, indicative of her carefree nature. The vibrancy of her hair contrasts beautifully with her deep blue eyes, which sparkle with curiosity and a fervent desire to understand the world around her. These eyes, wide and inviting, draw others into her orbit, reflecting a soul eager to connect and learn. 

She wears robes that are both elegant and functional, coloured in shades of soft blues and greens, adorned with subtle embroidery that depicts the lore of Gahrian. The fabrics are chosen not only for their beauty but also for their comfort and ease of movement, allowing her to perform her duties without hindrance.

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Curious, enthusiastic, and a bit naive
- **Strengths**: Highly sociable, quick to make friends, and passionate about her duties
- **Weaknesses**: Her naivety about worldly affairs can sometimes put her at a disadvantage; her eagerness can overwhelm more reserved personalities
- **Goals and Motivations**: To learn as much as she can about the world during her travels, and to use her role to foster positive relations between different cultures

**Background**

Tiana presence in the caravan is like a breath of fresh air. Her vivacious personality and boundless enthusiasm make her a central figure among the travelers. Always eager to engage, Tiana’s laughter and lively conversations echo through the camp, drawing others into her circle. Her innate curiosity about the world and its myriad cultures drives her to seek out stories and experiences from every new acquaintance, though her interactions are marked by a warmth and sincerity that endears her to most.

As Elborn's junior assistant, Tiana holds a position of significant responsibility, especially given her young age among elves. She views her role not only as a duty but as a profound opportunity to learn and embody the teachings of the Sun God, [[Gahrian]]. Her excitement about participating in the Festival of Gahrian in [[Hu Dao]] is palpable; she sees it as a crucial step in her spiritual and personal development. Tiana is particularly enthusiastic about the potential for the festival to improve elf-human relations, an issue close to her heart, despite her current lack of success with the caravan’s human merchants.

Tiana has quickly formed bonds with many in the caravan, with a special affinity for the entertainers, especially [[Brialla Silversong]]. Their shared love for art and expression has fostered a deep friendship, and Tiana often spends her evenings by the entertainers' fire, soaking in the stories and songs that travel through the air. Her mentorship under Elborn also shapes much of her interactions; she respects and admires him deeply, often looking to him for guidance on navigating the complexities of their mission and handling the more complex relationships within the caravan.
