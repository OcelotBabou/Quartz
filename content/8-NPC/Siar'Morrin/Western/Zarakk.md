**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Zarakk, Mouth of [[Spatalos]]
- **Species**: Gnoll
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 35 years
- **Occupation**: Shaman 
- **Affiliation**: Bloodfang Clan
- **Home Region**: Donatus Stultitia

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Zarakk is slightly smaller than the typical gnoll warrior, standing at about 6 feet tall. His fur is a dark, matted brown, streaked with ritualistic paint in red and black hues. His eyes glow a fiery red, a sign of his deep connection to the spiritual realm.
- **Height**: 183 cm 
- **Weight**: 95 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: Zarakk is noted for his intricate body markings and the ceremonial bones he wears. It most distinguishing feature is his muzzle, with the skin retracting back from it exposing its skeletal frame, it is lined with countless teeth and perpetually dripping with saliva. 

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Mysterious, deeply spiritual, and somewhat ominous.
- **Strengths**: Profound spiritual insight, skilled in dark rituals, and able to commune with [[Spatalos]], granting him arcane power.
- **Weaknesses**: He is weaker physically then most gnolls. 
- **Goals and Motivations**: To maintain favor with Spatalos through rituals and offerings.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Born as a runt into the Bloodfang Clan, Zarakk was immersed in the dark spiritual traditions of his people from a young age.
- **Education**: Zarakk was marked early on as a vessel for Spatalos’ will, receiving his education through dark commune with Spatalos. 
- **Key Events**: Maintains his position and power within the clan through his prophecies and the clans belief in him as the mouth piece of Spatalos.
- **Relationships**: While revered within his tribe for his spiritual guidance, Zarakk maintains a cautious distance, wary of his clans and their volatile nature. 

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Despite his diminutive size, Zarakk is a ferocious fighter. Coupled with his boons gifted by Spatalos, he is a surprisingly formidable foe. 
- **Technical Skills**: Expert in herbalism and the preparation of ritualistic potions and poisons.
- **Special Abilities**: Can invoke spiritual visions, commune with Spatalos, and perform dark rituals that influence the fate of his tribe.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Carries a staff topped with a skull.
- **Gadgets**: Various talismans and ritualistic tools that enhance his spiritual practices.
- **Personal Belongings**: The scythe of Spatalos, a powerful relic used within his dark rituals.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Zarakk's is responsible for the mysterious rituals performed by the Bloodfang Clan, and is believed to be key to the clans enhanced abilities and strength. 
- **Important Missions**: Continuously works to strengthen the spiritual bonds between the Bloodfang Clan and Spatalos, ensuring their prosperity and protection through divine favor.

**Quotes**

"Strength is not granted; it is taken with claw and fang under the shadow of Spatalos."
