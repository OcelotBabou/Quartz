*[goh-rak]*

**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Gorak
- **Species**: Giant
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: Approximately 150 years
- **Occupation**: Bandit 
- **Affiliation**: None (Independent)
- **Home Region**: Phemus Reach

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Unlike the typically bulky giants, Gorak is unusually lithe, which allows him to maneuver along the steep cliffs of [[Phemus Pass]] with surprising agility. His skin is a rough, stony gray, mirroring the rocky landscape he inhabits. His long limbs and slender build are deceptive, masking a surprising strength.
- **Height**: 480 cm 
- **Weight**: Approx. 560 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: Lithe build rare amongst giants. Dark grey skill that helps him blend into his surroundings. 

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Cunning, solitary, and greedy.
- **Strengths**: Agility uncommon among giants, strategic thinking in ambushes, and a keen ability to exploit opportunities.
- **Weaknesses**: His greed often leads him into risky confrontations; lacks the brute strength of typical giants.
- **Goals and Motivations**: Driven by avarice, Gorak preys on merchants and travelers passing through Phemus Pass, collecting treasures and goods from his ambushes.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Born in the shadowy crevices of Phemus Reach, Gorak was always an outlier due to his unique physique and agile nature, which set him apart from his more cumbersome kin.
- **Education**: Self-taught survival skills, honed through years of living on the fringes of giant society.
- **Key Events**: Established himself as a fearsome figure by successfully ambushing a heavily armed merchant convoy, using the terrain to his advantage.
- **Relationships**: Views other beings as either threats or opportunities, rarely forming bonds, and is considered an outcast among his own kind.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Expert in using the terrain for ambush tactics; proficient with thrown rocks and makeshift spears.
- **Technical Skills**: Skilled in stealth and tracking, unusual for a giant.
- **Special Abilities**: Excellent night vision and a heightened sense of smell, aiding in his nocturnal hunts.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Utilizes a variety of large, sharpened bones and rocks as projectiles and a giant longsword he uses as a dagger.
- **Gadgets**: Primitive traps.
- **Personal Belongings**: Keeps a collection of valuables from his victims stored in hidden caches around his perch.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Notorious for his ambushes based out from [[Goraks Perch]], he has garnered a reputation bordering on legend within [[Portum]].
- **Important Missions**: Continues to seek wealth by targeting any caravan that dares pass his perch, aiming to amass a hoard that could rival the treasures of kings.
