*[Kay-liss Ah-kur-see-ah]*

**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Kaelis Akurcia
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 28
- **Occupation**: Triarch of Defense
- **Affiliation**: [[Hu Dao]]
- **Home Region**: [[Agroma]]

**Appearance**

Kaelis Akurcia is small in stature but possesses a fit physique that speaks to a disciplined lifestyle. Despite not being physically imposing, his presence is accentuated by his meticulous grooming and the ornate military attire he wears, which includes finely crafted uniforms adorned with symbols of his rank and numerous achievements. His appearance is always pristine, reflecting his noble upbringing and the high standards he upholds in both his personal and professional life.

**Background**

From what you have heard en route to Hu Dao, Kaelis Akurci's rise to the position of Triarch of Defense is a testament to his exceptional strategic mind rather than martial prowess. Born into a noble family and given the finest education available in [[Vitaurum]], Kaelis has developed into a leader whose tactical acumen is respected, even if his physical appearance occasionally draws mockery, particularly due to his short stature. However, such jests are quickly silenced by his proven ability to lead and strategize Hu Dao's defenses effectively.

Kaelis is known for his rigid and proper demeanor, embodying the quintessence of military professionalism. His conduct is impeccable, often characterized by a formal, no-nonsense approach to both leadership and daily interactions. Despite the japes regarding his height, none can dispute his capability and the respect he commands across Hu Dao's armed forces.

Kaelis views the upcoming festival as a critical operational period. While it is a time of celebration for many, for him, it represents a heightened state of alert where his strategic planning will be crucial in ensuring the safety and security of all attendees.
