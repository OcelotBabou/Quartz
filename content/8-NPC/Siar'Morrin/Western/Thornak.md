*[thor-nak]*

**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Thornak
- **Species**: Ogre
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: Approximately 80 years
- **Occupation**: Raider
- **Affiliation**: None (Independent)
- **Home Region**: Eastern fringes of Phemus Reach

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Thornak is a hulking brute with a thick, muscular build typical of his kind but marked by an unusually menacing presence. His skin is a mottled greenish-gray, and two gnarled tusks protrude from his lower jaw, although one is broken halfway. His eyes are a fiery red, glaring with malevolence. His hair is sparse and wiry, crudely chopped at the shoulders.
- **Height**: 390 cm 
- **Weight**: Approx. 550 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: His fiery red eyes and one of his tusks is broken halfway. He is also larger than the average ogre.  

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Aggressive, unpredictable, and fiercely territorial.
- **Strengths**: Incredible brute strength, resistance to pain, and a fearsome reputation that often precedes him.
- **Weaknesses**: Limited strategic thinking and a quick temper that can be exploited.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To dominate his territory and expand his influence by looting and pillaging, driven by a deep-seated need for power and control.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Born and raised on the harsh eastern fringes of Phemus Reach, Thornak quickly adapted to a life of survival of the fittest. He was abandoned by his parents at a young age, as is typical for many ogres.
- **Education**: Learned the harsh realities of survival in a brutal environment, with no formal education but a wealth of experience in combat and raiding.
- **Key Events**: Rose to notoriety after leading several successful ambushes on merchants headed to [[Hu Dao]], earning him the hatred of its residents and a fearsome reputation across the neighboring regions.
- **Relationships**: Isolated, with no allies or close contacts, Thornak relies solely on intimidation and strength to interact with others.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Expert in melee combat, using his size and strength to overpower opponents. Skilled with heavy bludgeoning weapons.
- **Technical Skills**: Basic survival skills, proficient in raiding tactics.
- **Special Abilities**: High resilience to physical damage, able to continue fighting even when severely injured.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Wields a massive spiked club, crafted from the trunk of an ironwood tree and studded with metal spikes.
- **Gadgets**: None.
- **Personal Belongings**: Various trophies from his raids, worn as adornments on his armor.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Known for his ambush tactics and brazen raids on small settlements.
- **Important Missions**: Continuously seeks to expand his wealth and resources through raiding, always looking to assert his dominance over more lands.

**Quotes**

- "Thornak smash, Thornak take. Simple."
