*[toh-ren blak-wah-ter]*

**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Toren Blackwater
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 42 years
- **Occupation**: Military Commander and Council Member of Seagate
- **Affiliation**: Seagate Council
- **Home Region**: Portum

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Toren is a commanding figure, standing tall with a broad-shouldered, muscular build that speaks to years of physical training. He has a stern face, marked by a weathered look and deep-set eyes that reflect a life of responsibility and vigilance. His hair is short, peppered with gray, and he often sports a well-groomed beard.
- **Height**: 188 cm (6'2")
- **Weight**: 90 kg (198 lbs)
- **Distinguishing Features**: He lost his left arm, although he tries to hide this by wearing a large cloak draped over his broad shoulders. 

![[Blackwatera.webp]]


**Personality**

- **Traits**: Disciplined, strategic, and unwaveringly loyal to Seagate and its inhabitants.
- **Strengths**: Excellent leadership skills, adept in both tactical and strategic military operations, and one of the few people in Portum with actual maritime experience.
- **Weaknesses**: His commitment to his duties can sometimes make him inflexible, struggling to adapt to unconventional solutions or suggestions.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To ensure the security and stability of Seagate through strong defensive measures and to restore some of the region's former military prestige.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Toren comes from a family with a long military tradition. Raised in Alnwick, south of Seagate, he grew up hearing tales of his ancestors' valor at sea and was trained from a young age in the art of warfare.
- **Education**: Toren was sent to [[Vitaurum]] in his youth, where he attended a military academy. Later, in the comparatively safe waters of Mare Sanctum, he receiving specialized training in naval tactics and sailing. 
- **Key Events**: On returning to Portum, Toren quickly proved his worth, helping organize defenses of Seagate against an organized giant attack. He has also worked closely with [[Aidan Maris]] in attempting to re-establish the Seagate naval forces.
- **Relationships**: Respected by his troops and trusted by his fellow council members, Toren is considered a pillar of strength in the community. His relationship with his family is strong, and he maintains connections with friends made in Vitaurum.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Highly proficient in hand-to-hand combat and knowledgeable in the use of various weapons, particularly swords and naval artillery.
- **Technical Skills**: Expert in defensive strategy and logistics, as well as being a skilled sailor.
- **Special Abilities**: Has an innate sense for the tumultuous weather along the Periculosus coast, and a natural gift for sailing and naval command. 

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Carries a ceremonial sword gifted to him upon graduating from military academy in Vitaurum. 
- **Gadgets**: Navigational equipment.
- **Personal Belongings**: His ceremonial sword gifted to him.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Toren's leadership faced a critical test when he spearheaded a bold mission to retake Watcher's Isle, aiming to dispel the notorious legend of the Demon of Seagate. The mission, driven by a desire to restore confidence in Seagate's military prowess and to secure the strategic island, ended disastrously. Not only did the expedition fail, but Toren also suffered severe injuries, losing an arm in the confrontation.
- **Important Missions**: Currently leading the effort to overhaul Seagate's coastal defenses, incorporating modern technology to protect against both human and supernatural threats.

**Quotes**

- "Vigilance is the price of peace. We will not be caught unprepared again."
