*[goh-rath hah-mah]*

**Basic** **Information** 

- **Name**: Gorath Hamma
- **Species**: Giant
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: Estimated 120 years (in giant years)
- **Occupation**: Tribal Leader
- **Affiliation**: Phemus Reach Giants
- **Home Region**: Phemus Reach

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Gorath stands as a monumental figure among the giants, his immense size and formidable stature making him an iconic presence in Phemus Reach. His skin is thick and leathery, deeply lined and weathered from years of exposure. It bears a multitude of scars from countless battles, each mark telling a story of survival and dominance. His eyes are a piercing gray, sharp (for a giant), set beneath a heavy brow. His hair and beard are wild and unkempt, matted with the remnants bones and splattered food. 
- **Height**: 520 cm
- **Weight**: Approx. 750 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: Gorath's arms are disproportionately large even for a giant, equipped with thick muscles that bulge under his skin. His matted beard filled with discarded or dropped food. 

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Dominant, aggressive, and fiercely territorial.
- **Strengths**: Unmatched physical strength, natural brutish leadership abilities.
- **Weaknesses**: Limited strategic thinking, prone to solving problems through brute force rather than diplomacy or cunning.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To maintain the dominance of his tribe within Phemus Reach and ensure their survival against any threats.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Gorath was born into one of the prominent giant clans of Phemus Reach and rose to prominence through feats of strength and combat prowess.
- **Education**: His learning came from the oral traditions of his tribe, focusing on survival skills, combat, and the legends of their ancestors.
- **Key Events**: Became leader after beating the previous leader to death. 
- **Relationships**: Commands respect and fear from his tribe, viewed as a pillar of strength but also a volatile leader.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Expert in hand-to-hand combat and skilled with his giant clubs and throwing large boulders.
- **Technical Skills**: Knowledgeable in the ways of giant warfare and survival in harsh environments.
- **Special Abilities**: Renowned for his thunderous roar that can intimidate enemies and rally his tribe. He also have big reach, help hamma enemy. 

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Wields a massive club carved from the trunk of an ancient tree.
- **Gadgets**: Utilizes primitive but effective armor made from the hides of beasts and reinforced with bones and stones.
- **Personal Belongings**: Is rather fond of his club, but has a habit of misplacing or destroying other belongings. 

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: His leadership has been marked by violent confrontations with rival giant clans and occasional skirmishes with adventurers or explorers daring to traverse Phemus Reach.
- **Important Missions**: Continuously works to fortify his tribe's territory, ensuring it remains impervious to outsiders and well-stocked with resources.

**Quotes**

"When in doubt, hamma 'em out"

**History**

Gorath’s rule has been one of maintaining the old ways of giant kin, emphasizing strength and dominance as the core pillars of their society. Under his leadership, the giants of Phemus Reach have managed to keep their lands free from outside influences, continuing a legacy of isolation and formidable presence.
