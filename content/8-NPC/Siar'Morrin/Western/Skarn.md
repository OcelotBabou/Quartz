**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Skarn
- **Species**: Gnoll
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: Unknown
- **Occupation**: Guardian of [[The Ruins of Donatus]]
- **Affiliation**: Bloodfang Clan
- **Home Region**: Donatus Stultitia

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Skarn is an exceptionally large and intimidating gnoll, standing far taller and more muscular than his kin. His fur is grizzled and streaked with white, showing signs of age yet maintaining a formidable presence. His eyes are a deep, piercing red, reflecting a lifetime of vigilance.
- **Height**: 236 cm 
- **Weight**: 136 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: His aged appearance rare among gnoll, and his massive size. 

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Stoic, relentless, and fiercely protective of [[The Ruins of Donatus]].
- **Strengths**: Unmatched combat skills, strategic thinking in protection and defense, and an intimidating presence that deters potential threats.
- **Weaknesses**: Unknown
- **Goals and Motivations**: To protect the Ruins of Donatus at all costs, ensuring that the sacred site remains undisturbed.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Skarn's early life is shrouded in mystery, but it is known that he has been the guardian of the Ruins of Donatus for as long as the current members of the tribe can remember.
- **Education**: Skarn has honed his skills over decades, possibly centuries, becoming a master in wielding his halberd. 
- **Key Events**: Has repelled numerous invasions and attempts to plunder the ruins, each time reinforcing his legend within and beyond his tribe.
- **Relationships**: While respected and somewhat feared by his tribe, Skarn is a solitary figure, spending his time guarding the entrance to Donatus' tomb. 

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Master of the halberd and hand-to-hand combat, Skarn is capable of taking on multiple opponents simultaneously.
- **Technical Skills**: Expert in survival skills, and defense.
- **Special Abilities**: Rumored to have mystical endurance and strength, possibly granted by some ancient magic or a blessing from the spirits of the land.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Wields a legendary halberd known as "Ruin’s Edge," reputed to have been gifted to Skarn from [[Spatalos]] himself.
- **Gadgets**: None. Relies on his natural abilities and skills.
- **Personal Belongings**: Carries a set of ancient keys to hidden chambers within the ruins, whose purposes are known only to him.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Skarn’s long watch has made him the stuff of legends, both as a protector of the ruins and as a deterrent to those who would disturb the sacred site.
- **Important Missions**: Continuously monitors and protects the perimeter of the Ruins of Donatus, ensuring that no treasure hunters or invaders desecrate the sacred grounds.
