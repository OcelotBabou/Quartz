*[meer-ah gayl-wind]*

**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Mira Galewind
- **Species**: Human
- **Gender**: Female
- **Age**: 34 years
- **Occupation**: Council Member of Seagate, Diplomat
- **Affiliation**: Seagate Council
- **Home Region**: Portum

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Mira is known for her poised and elegant demeanor, with sharp, intelligent eyes and a commanding presence. She typically dresses in refined, professional attire that reflects her diplomatic role. Her dark hair is often styled in an intricate updo. Some may define her stout, though never in earshot of her.
- **Height**: 168 cm 
- **Weight**: 68 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: Mira carries a hand-made diplomat’s satchel, that she keeps close at all times. 

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Diplomatic, articulate, and highly perceptive.
- **Strengths**: Exceptional negotiation skills, an adept understanding of international relations, and a keen ability to navigate complex political landscapes.
- **Weaknesses**: Sometimes overly cautious, which can delay decision-making; she can also quick to anger, especially when people comment on her appearance whether the comment be positive or negative.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To restore Seagate’s position as a key player in international trade and to foster cultural exchanges that rebuild the city's reputation as a hub of prosperity and innovation.

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Mira was raised in a poor family, learning to work early. Learning through barter and sales, she developed strong people skills early one.
- **Education**: Almost purely self taught Mira is a testament to what one can accomplish when driven. 
- **Key Events**: Negotiated a critical, yet short lived trade agreement with Bullmar, that provided a much needed boost to local economy.
- **Relationships**: Maintains a professional relation with her fellow council member [[Aidan Maris]] and [[Toren Blackwater]], although she finds Aiden's dogged focus on maritime revival tiresome. She is highly respected amongst the peoples of Seagate for her diplomatic finesse. She also maintains a wide network of international contacts that aid in her efforts, particularly along the [[Ridgeback Forest]] and [[Agroma]].

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Basic self-defense training, primarily for personal safety during diplomatic travel.
- **Technical Skills**: Expert in economic diplomacy, proficient in multiple languages, and skilled in cross-cultural communication.
- **Special Abilities**: Has a diplomatic tact that can defuse tense situations and turn negotiations in her favor.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: A concealed stiletto knife for emergencies.
- **Gadgets**: None.
- **Personal Belongings**: Her hand-made diplomat’s satchel, that reminds her of her humble beginnings.

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: Successfully re-established consistent trade with the East via [[Phemus Pass]] for a while, however due to dangers of said pass and rumor's of [[Gorak]], this trade was short lived.
- **Important Missions**: Actively working to discover safer land based routes to the East and reinvigorate the local economy through trade. .

**Quotes**

"Diplomacy is the art of balancing interests with integrity, while ensuring your interests are prioritized."
