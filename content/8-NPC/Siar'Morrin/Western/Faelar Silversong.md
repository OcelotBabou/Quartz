**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Faelar Silversong

- **Age**: 35
- **Occupation**: Entertainer (Leader of the troupe)
- **Affiliation**: 
- **Home Region**: [[Thapsos]]

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: 

Faelar presents a striking figure despite his bald head, which gleams under the sun or lantern light alike. His long, bushy beard, peppered with shades of brown and gray, adds a touch of wisdom and rugged charm to his visage. His eyes, a vibrant green, are often alight with humor and a spark of mischief, reflecting the spirit of a born entertainer.

Faelar favors practicality on most days, opting for plain, well-worn clothes that allow for easy movement and durability through the rigors of travel. His typical ensemble might include a simple tunic, sturdy trousers, and comfortable boots, all chosen for their functionality over fashion. However, Faelar’s practical daily wear starkly contrasts with his performance costumes, which are anything but subdued. During the few performance seen on route, he transforms into a theatrical maestro. His costumes are elaborate and flamboyant, designed to catch every bit of light and draw all eyes to him. 
- **Height**: 185 cm
- **Weight**: 78 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: A tattoo of a A lyre and quill crossed over an open book

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Charismatic, witty, and approachable
- **Strengths**: Natural leader, excellent communicator, highly creative
- **Weaknesses**: Can be a bit self absorbed, and domineering in conversation
- **Goals and Motivations**: Currently, to bring his troupes best performance ever to Hu Dao, spreading his reputation. 

**Background**

While inherently charming, Faelar often dominates conversations, his enthusiasm and slight narcissistic streak leading him to hold the spotlight a bit longer than necessary. This trait, however, is tempered by his genuine passion for his craft and his ability to entertain and engage his audience, making his company more endearing than overbearing.

Born to perform, Faelar views the caravan's journey to Western Siar'Morrin not just as travel but as a grand stage for expanding the influence and reputation of his troupe. He relishes every opportunity to perform, seeing each new village or city as a chance to leave a lasting impression. 

At the heart of his world is his wife and co-leader, [[Brialla Silversong]], with whom he shares a deep and abiding love. Their partnership extends beyond marriage into every aspect of their performances, her calm balancing his exuberance, her creativity enhancing his flair. Faelar's relationship with the rest of the caravan is generally positive, as he is always eager to engage with anyone willing to lend an ear.

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Unknown
- **Technical Skills**: Skilled musician (plays several instruments), exceptional singer, and adept at crafting compelling narratives.
- **Special Abilities**: Has a knack for reading the mood of his audience and adapting his performance to captivate them fully.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Unknown
- **Gadgets**: Various musical instruments including a lute, a flute, and a small drum.
- **Personal Belongings**: A collection of costumes and theatrical masks, a notebook filled with songs and stories gathered during his travels.
