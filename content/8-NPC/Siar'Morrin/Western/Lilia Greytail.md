### Basic Information

- **Name:** Lilia Greytail  
- **Species:** [[Ratfolk]]
- **Age:** 27  
- **Occupation:** Retired  
- **Affiliation:** N/A  
- **Home Region:** [[Paloto Swamplands]]

### Physical Description

- **Appearance:** Lilia shows the wear of a life rich with experiences; her fur is streaked with silver, more pronounced than her husband's, reflecting her age. Her eyes, however, remain bright and curious, mirroring a spirit that's both enduring and welcoming.  
- **Height:** 140 cm  
- **Weight:** 38 kg  
- **Distinguishing Features:** Lilia often wears traditional Ratfolk garments that are both colourful and functional, further enhancing her appearance as a kindhearted grandmother figure within the community.

### Personality

- **Traits:** Lilia exudes warmth and a robust hospitality that makes her home a hub for gatherings. She is insightful, often providing wisdom in community disputes or personal matters, which gives her a respected status among the Ratfolk of [[Furrowton]].  
- **Strengths:** Lilia's major strengths lie in her deep empathy for others and her ability to foster a sense of unity and cooperation in her community. Her worldly knowledge, gained from years of listening to Virag's stories, allows her to be a bridge between different cultures and races.  
- **Weaknesses:** Her advanced age sometimes limits her physical involvement in community activities, and she struggles with a fear of becoming a burden to her daughter, [[Daria Greytail]] and the community as she ages.

### Background

**Earlylife:** Lilia Greytail was born into the vibrant and bustling city of [[Tanglethorn]]. From a young age, Lilia exhibited a charismatic and diplomatic nature, often finding herself alongside Virag, her childhood friend. Their youthful adventures were filled with mischief and exploration, but Lilia's innate ability to mediate and smooth over situations proved essential, especially when Virag’s strong sense of justice led them into trouble.

**Key Events:**
- **Marrying Virag:** Lilia and Virag’s relationship, rooted in deep friendship and shared values, eventually led to marriage. 
- **Raising a Family:** With Virag often away, earning for the family, taking on jobs as a travelling guard or mercenary, Lilia took on the primary responsibility of raising their children in Tanglethorn.
- **Moving to Furrowton:** Upon Virag's retirement from active duty, the couple moved to Furrowton. This transition marked a new chapter in their lives, until the call of adventure once again lure Virag away. 

### Goals and Motivations

- **Primary Goal:** To maintain and pass on the cultural heritage of the Ratfolk, ensuring that the younger generations appreciate their roots and the broader world.  
- **Secondary Goal:** To support Virag's quest indirectly by nurturing a supportive community that upholds the values he fights for.  
- **Motivation:** Lilia's love for her family and community drives her every action; she is motivated by the desire to see her children and grandchildren thrive in a world less divided by hatred and prejudice.
