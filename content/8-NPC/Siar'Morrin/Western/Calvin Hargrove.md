**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: 

Calvin has a robust and stocky build used to the physical demands of travel and trade. His face, weather-beaten and rugged, features deep lines etched by years of bargaining and exposure to the elements, which give him a certain authoritative demeanor. His hair, short and beginning to gray at the temples, is meticulously kept, reflecting his emphasis on maintaining a respectable appearance. His beard, equally well-groomed, adds to his serious and somewhat imposing presence.

For attire, Calvin favors functionality combined with a touch of traditional merchant style. He typically wears a wide-brimmed hat to shield his eyes from the sun. His clothing consists of a sturdy leather vest worn over a durable shirt, both chosen for their ability to withstand the rigors of the road while still presenting a neat, professional appearance. His trousers and boots are similarly chosen for durability and comfort, ensuring he is always prepared for the unpredictability of travel.
- **Height**: 175 cm
- **Weight**: 90 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: Calvin often wears a wide-brimmed hat and a leather vest over a sturdy shirt, his attire practical and suited for long journeys.

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Shrewd, pragmatic, and somewhat reserved
- **Strengths**: Good business acumen, excellent negotiation skills, reliable
- **Weaknesses**: Self-centered and greedy
- **Goals and Motivations**: To expand his trading business and secure profitable deals; deeply interested in discovering new goods and markets to exploit

**Background**

Calvin,  known as the friendliest of the three merchants, approaches his business with a pragmatic and open attitude. While he doesn't exhibit a strong fondness for non-human members of the caravan, he's pragmatic enough to engage with them if there's a potential profit. His demeanor is generally professional, marked by a calm, measured approach to negotiations and interactions. Calvin's willingness to adapt his principles to suit his business interests speaks to his flexible, though fundamentally commercial, outlook on life.

As a merchant, Calvin’s primary goal is to expand his trading empire and capitalize on new opportunities presented by the journey to Hu Dao. He sees the festival and the large crowds it draws as potential goldmines for his diverse array of goods. While he carries the typical merchant’s caution, he's also cautiously optimistic about the new markets and the wealth they could bring.

Among the caravan, Calvin's relationships are mostly professional. He maintains a cordial rapport with his fellow merchants, [[Miranda Fleet]] and [[Thorton Crewe]], sharing with them a common language of profit and trade. His interactions with non-humans are courteous if somewhat reserved, driven more by potential business advantages than genuine camaraderie. However, his pragmatic approach means he's often seen negotiating deals or sharing meals with a wide range of caravan members, always with an eye towards broadening his network and securing his interests.
