**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Thorgash Bloodfang
- **Species**: Gnoll
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: Unknown
- **Occupation**: Chieftain
- **Affiliation**: Bloodfang Clan
- **Home Region**: Donatus Stultitia

**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: Thorgash is an imposing figure, standing over seven feet tall with a muscular build typical of his species. His fur is a mottled grey and brown, scarred from numerous battles. His eyes are a piercing yellow, reflecting a predator’s gaze.
- **Height**: 215 cm 
- **Weight**: 125 kg 
- **Distinguishing Features**: Notable for his massive size and the distinctive blood-red mane that runs down his back, Thorgash also bears the ritualistic scars of [[Spatalos]], which are revered symbols among his tribe.

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Dominant, fierce, and ruthless.
- **Strengths**: Natural leader, formidable in battle, and deeply connected to the ritualistic traditions of his clan. 
- **Weaknesses**: Reflective of his patron, Thorgash lacks foresight and is quick to expend any resources he gains. 
- **Goals and Motivations**: To honor Spatalos and bring sacrifices to to uphold the rituals in his worship. 

**Background**

- **Early Life**: Unknown
- **Education**: Beyond oral tradition telling of Spatalos and his importance to the gnolls lacks any education. 
- **Key Events**: It's hard to pinpoint when Thorgah took over as chieftain of the Bloodfang Clan, all that is known is that he has led them for many years now. 
- **Relationships**: Commands absolute loyalty from his clan and is feared by his enemies. 

**Skills and Abilities**

- **Combat Skills**: Master of the spear and shield, although is skilled in hand-to-hand combat and just as happy utilizing his teeth as any formal weapon. 
- **Technical Skills**: Adept at survival tactics, strategic planning for raids, and managing large-scale tribal affairs.
- **Special Abilities**: Believed to be blessed by Spatalos, possessing enhanced strength and resilience during ritualistic battles.

**Equipment**

- **Weapons**: Wields a massive spear forged from the bones of his enemies, and a shield adorned with the symbols of Spatalos.
- **Gadgets**: Wears armor crafted from the hides of the plains' toughest beasts. 
- **Personal Belongings**: Carries the Horn of Donatus, a relic believed to be imbued with the powers of the ancient deity, used to rally and drive his companions into a frenzy during battle. 

**Significant Events**

- **Major Plot Points**: His leadership has been marked by expanding the raiding territories of the gnolls and enforcing a harsh but effective rule, ensuring the clan's prominence in the region.
- **Important Missions**: Currently focused on quelling dissent within the clan and preparing for an impending large-scale assault on a neighboring settlement as a display of power and to secure more 'resources.'
