### Basic Information

- **Name:** Drogar
- **Species:** [[Cia'an]]
- **Age:** 11  
- **Occupation:** N/A
- **Affiliation:** Skugga Pack
- **Home Region:** [[Sabhaile Forest]]

### Physical Description

- **Appearance:** Drogar is large and solidly built for his age, already showing signs of the formidable strength that runs through his bloodline. His fur is a deep, glossy black, with tufts of silver beginning to appear around his ears, a trait passed from his mother. Though his frame is still growing into its full potential, his stance is proud and his movements already carry the disciplined edge of a future warrior. He typically wears simple training gear, often slightly oversized, and carries a carved wooden practice spear, his own design, modeled after [[Veyna]]’s glaive.
- **Height:** 158 cm  
- **Weight:** 41 kg  
- **Distinguishing Features:** His wooden practice spear he wields with pride. 

### Personality

- **Traits:** Drogar is sincere, determined, and warm-hearted. He idolizes his older siblings, especially Veyna and [[Rhaegar]], and tries hard to live up to what he believes they represent. Compassionate and loyal to a fault, he’s the kind of child who comforts others even when he’s quietly struggling himself.
- **Strengths:** Natural strength and discipline, a quick learner, and deeply empathetic. His ability to connect with others and offer support makes him well-loved and respected within the pack, despite his age.
- **Weaknesses:** Drogar’s desire to please everyone can lead to self-neglect, and he struggles with the pressure of living up to the family’s expectations. His emotional sensitivity, while a strength, can leave him vulnerable when he feels overlooked or forgotten.
### Background

**Earlylife:** As the youngest of the [Placeholder] family, Drogar was born into a household rich in legacy, strength, and expectation. With his siblings already forging strong reputations, Drogar grew up in their shadow, but never with resentment. He saw each of them as a guidepost, especially Veyna, whose stories of valor stirred his imagination, and Rhaegar, who offered patient, grounding advice when the pressure felt overwhelming.

He was closest to Rhaegar during their shared time at home, finding in his brother someone who understood the burden of expectation without judgment. Rhaegar was his confidant, the one he trusted to hear his doubts. When Rhaegar left, Drogar was crushed, and though he’s thrown himself into training and study, he still waits quietly for word from his brother.

**Key Events:**
- **Beginning His Training:** Drogar has recently begun formal training with the pack's warriors, quickly gaining a reputation as a serious and disciplined initiate.
- **The Farewells:** His brother’s departure was his first real taste of loss, losing the one confidant he could truly unburden himself upon within the pack. Veyna's departure occurred while he was still very young, as such he doesn't remember her as well, but is inspired by the stories he hears of her. 

### Goals and Motivations

- **Primary Goal:** To become a protector and future leader of the Skugga Pack, honoring the legacy of his family. Finding honor and purpose while remaining loyal to the pack.
- **Secondary Goal:** To prove to himself and others that he can live up to the example set by Veyna and Rhaegar, without needing to walk the same path.
- **Motivation:** Driven by love for his family and a deep sense of duty, Drogar seeks to become the kind of leader who can carry the weight of tradition without losing sight of kindness.
