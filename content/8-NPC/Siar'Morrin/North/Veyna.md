### Basic Information

- **Name:** Vayna
- **Species:** [[Cia'an]]
- **Age:** 23
- **Occupation:** Unknown
- **Affiliation:** Skugga Pack
- **Home Region:** [[Sabhaile Forest]]

### Physical Description

- **Appearance:** Veyna is a towering figure among the Cia’an, inheriting her father’s size and strength with an even sharper edge. Her pitch-black fur absorbs the light, giving her a shadow-like silhouette on the battlefield. Her movements are precise and deliberate, graceful despite her bulk. She wears minimal armor, preferring speed and impact, with a large two-handed glaive always within reach.
- **Height:** 206 cm  
- **Weight:** 104 kg  
- **Distinguishing Features:** Her trade mark glaive, far too large for any other to wield. 

### Personality

- **Traits:** Confident, commanding, and unapologetically ambitious. Veyna believes in making her mark on the world, and refuses to let tradition or territory limit her. Yet beneath her warrior’s bravado lies a heart fiercely protective of her kin. As the eldest sibling, she often played the role of quiet comforter, offering strength and solace when their mothers harsh lesson became to much. 
- **Strengths:** Unmatched martial skill, natural battlefield leadership, and immense physical strength. Veyna’s strategic mind makes her as effective as a tactician as she is a front-line force.
- **Weaknesses:** Her ambition often puts her at odds with tradition. She can be dismissive of those she perceives as overly cautious, and her independence sometimes reads as coldness, even to her family.

### Background

**Earlylife:** From the moment she could walk, it was clear Veyna was born for war. She towered over her peers and outpaced them in every test of strength or skill. With each passing year, her potential became harder to ignore, not just by her family, but by the whole Skugga Pack. Yet as praise piled on, so too did pressure. Expectations from elders, whispered comparisons, and the unspoken hope that she would become a future leader.

Though proud of her roots, Veyna came to see Sabhaile Forest as a proving ground too small for what she hoped to achieve. Still in her teens, she said her farewells and left the forest and family behind.

**Key Events:**
- **The Sibling Shield:** Though known for her power, Veyna was always the one to shield her younger siblings when their mother's expectations cut too deep. Her tent became a haven, her stories, a balm.
- **Leaving the Pack:** Her departure was ceremonial yet solemn. The Skugga Pack gave her the honors of a warrior, but the absence of her presence left a noticeable void.
- **Unseen Battles:** While little is known of her current path, the few who’ve encountered her speak of a Cia’an warrior carving a reputation far beyond her homeland.

### Goals and Motivations

- **Primary Goal:** To earn a name that reaches beyond the forests—one carved by victory, honor, and deeds worthy of story and legend. 
- **Secondary Goal:** Unknown
- **Motivation:** Veyna is driven by the hunger to be more than what is expected of her. Not out of arrogance, but out of a fierce belief that greatness lies where comfort ends.
