## Basic Information

- **Name**: Xietem
- **Domain**: Anxiety, Paranoia
- **Title**: The Whisperer of Dread, The Shadow of Doubt
- **Alignment**: Chaotic Excess
- **Symbol**: A dark spider, with a skull for an abdomen
- **Primary Worship Area**: 

![[Xietemsymbol.jpg]]

## Physical Description

- **Appearance**: Xietem is depicted as a nightmarish, spider-like figure with long, spindly limbs that skitter and twitch with unnerving speed. Its body is covered in a dark, chitinous exoskeleton that reflects no light, making them appear as a moving shadow. Its head is an amalgamation of a skull and a spider, with multiple eyes that gleam with a frantic, paranoid light. Their mouth is filled with sharp, needle-like teeth, constantly chittering in a whisper of dread. While their limbs end in clawed appendages that seem to grasp at the air, ready to ensnare any who come too close.
- **Height**: 420cm
- **Weight**: Variable
- **Distinguishing Features**: Their most notable features are its multiple, frantic eyes, and his skeletal, spider-like form. 

## Personality

- **Traits**: Paranoid, fearful, manipulative
- **Strengths**: Can incite and amplify feelings of anxiety and paranoia in others, causing them to second-guess themselves and live in constant fear
- **Weaknesses**: Its own pervasive fear makes them unreliable and unstable, easily influenced by stronger wills
- **Goals and Motivations**: To spread anxiety and fear, feeding off the dread and paranoia of mortals to increase its power.

## Mythology and Worship

- **Creation Myth**: In the early days, before Statera created the mortal races, she recognized faults within herself. Desiring to present the best possible world for her creations, she sought to purge herself of these negative traits. However, the universe’s energy imbued these purged traits with a life of their own. Thus, from Statera’s discarded anxiety and paranoia, Xietem was born.
- **Major Cults and Religious Practices**: Those influenced by Xeitem, dedicate no worship to Xeitem. Rather most do not realize they are even influenced. However, there are those that worship Xietem, and use its gifts to manipulate and sow discourse amongst their enemies.  
- **Rituals**: 
- **Clergy and Temples**: Temples to Xietem are dark and foreboding, often located in secluded areas. Xietems priests, known as the Whisperers of Dread, are adept at spreading fear and manipulating the anxieties of others. They guide their followers in embracing their fears and using them as a source of power.
