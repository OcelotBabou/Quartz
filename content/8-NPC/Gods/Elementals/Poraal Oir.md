## Physical Description

- **Appearance**: 

Poraal Oir is depicted as a dynamic and formidable presence, his towering presence is as dynamic as the weather phenomena he controls; his form is woven from the dense, roiling storm clouds that darken the skies. Swirling greys and deep, ominous blues make up his body, shifting and changing with the temper of the storm.

His skin seems to pulse with the energy of thunderclouds, its texture a constant motion of swirling vapors and gusts of wind. At times, his outline blurs into the surrounding mists, only to become starkly defined when illuminated by the brilliant flashes of lightning that crackle across his form. These bursts of electric light reveal a fierce countenance, eyes glowing bright with the yellow-white intensity of lightning bolts, reflecting the raw power of the storm.

Poraal Oir’s hair is a wild, flowing mass of cloud and vapor, extending into the sky and moving with the force of the winds he commands. It is as if each strand is charged with storm energy, occasionally sparking with static electricity that leaps to and fro in unpredictable arcs.

Clothed in a cape of cascading rain and winds, Poraal Oir appears both majestic and formidable. This cape swirls around him, sometimes enveloping him in a shroud of rainfall or flaring out behind him in a gusty tempest.

## Mythology and Worship

- **Creation Myth**: 

In the ageless epoch before the formation of Staterum, the cosmos was a tumultuous battleground where the Primordial forces clashed in an eternal struggle for dominance. These beings, embodiments of the fundamental elements, seldom engaged directly in combat. Instead, they summoned armies of lesser elementals, each reflecting a facet of their immense powers, to wage war on their behalf.

Among these elemental warriors, one figure rose to prominence through his unparalleled prowess and strategic genius—Poraal. His ability to harness the raw fury of wind and lightning set him apart on the battlefield, where he commanded the swirling tempests with unrivaled authority. Poraal's tactical acumen and his capacity to evoke fear and awe in equal measure quickly earned him the title of general in Gaoth Oir's ethereal legions.

Poraal's legend grew with every storm he conjured and every battle he swayed in favor of his master. His name became synonymous with victory, and his presence on the field guaranteed a tempest both literal and figurative. His feats not only secured crucial victories but also inspired his troops, rallying the storm spirits to greater heights of fury and precision.

When the age-old conflicts finally began to wane, and the Primordials sought a resolution to their endless war, the creation of Staterum was proposed as a realm of balance and harmony, a testament to their combined might and a sanctuary for their powers. As the new world took shape through the collective efforts of the Primordials, Gaoth Oir, the master of winds and storms, did not forget the valor and loyalty of his greatest general.

In recognition of Poraal's faithful service and outstanding leadership, Gaoth Oir bestowed upon him a portion of his own divine essence. Poraal was transformed from a mere elemental into a deity, adopting the name Poraal Oir, or 'Valiant Wind' in the ancient tongue of the air spirits. Gaoth Oir took him as a son, entrusting him with the guardianship of the storms and winds that were now part of the greater domain of Staterum.
