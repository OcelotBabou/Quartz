## Basic Information

- **Name**: Niphar
- **Domain**: Caves, Depths
- **Title**: The Deep Dweller, Envoy of Caves
- **Alignment**: True Neutral
- **Symbol**: A cave entrance with a descending path
- **Primary Worship Area**: Caves, underground regions, and places where the depths of the earth are revered

### Physical Description

- **Appearance**: 

Niphar stands as a formidable figure whose towering form is carved from the rugged, enduring rock of the deepest underground caverns. The texture of his skin mirrors the varied surfaces one might find in these hidden depths—from rough, unyielding granite to smoother, moisture-slick limestone. Sparkling veins of precious minerals, such as sapphire and quartz, traverse his body, catching the faintest glimmers of light and reflecting them in mesmerizing patterns.

Niphar's eyes, deep-set and impenetrable, resemble ancient pools of still water found in the darkest recesses of caves. They reflect a profound depth, suggesting an age-old wisdom and knowledge of the earth’s secrets. His gaze is both alluring and unnerving, as it seems to peer into the very soul.

From his scalp grow formations of stalactites and stalagmites, rather than conventional hair, giving his head a crown-like appearance of jagged, dripping stone. These formations are ever-growing, dripping with mineral-rich water that sometimes crystallizes into tiny, sparkling formations along their length.
- **Height**: 430m
- **Weight**: Unknown
- **Distinguishing Features**: His most notable features are his rough stone-like skin, dark eyes, and his hair made of stalactites and stalagmites.

## Mythology and Worship

- **Creation Myth**: 

In the age when Talam, the Primordial of Earth, was sculpting the landscapes of Staterum, he sought aid through the form of progeny to mold and shape the mold this new world. From the deepest minerals and stones, Talam forged five sons, each imbued with a fragment of his formidable power and a specific role in the creation of the earth's features. Among these sons was Niphar, whose curiosity and spirit of exploration were as boundless as the caves he would come to embody.

Niphar, known for his adventurous nature, was particularly drawn to the mysteries lurking in the uncharted depths of the earth. His curiosity made him a target for Gaoth Oir, who had become wary of the power Talam would wield through his sons. Gaoth Oir whispered tales of an ancient wonder hidden within the depths of the earth — [[The Echoing Abyss]], a seemingly bottomless sinkhole rumored to hold secrets from before the dawn of time.

Lured by the promise of discovery and driven by his innate curiosity, Niphar journeyed to the edge of this vast chasm. As he peered into its depths, enthralled by the mysteries it might hold, Gaoth Oir seized his moment. With a sudden and fierce gust of wind, he exploited Niphar’s precarious position at the precipice. The powerful wind caught Niphar off guard, tipping his balance and sending him plummeting into the abyss.

As Niphar fell deeper into the chasm, the light from the surface faded, and he was swallowed by the shadows of Staterum’s deepest reaches. However, rather than meet his end, Niphar found himself in the heart of the earth, surrounded by vast caverns and endless tunnels that stretched into the darkness.

Stripped of the chance to return to the surface, Niphar embraced his fate and began to carve out his realm within these subterranean depths. He shaped the caverns and tunnels, molding them into a complex network that mirrored the intricate beauty of the surface world. Over time, Niphar transformed his accidental prison into a domain of wonder and majesty, ruling over the caves and depths with a steadfast spirit, turning his fall into a rise as the undisputed master of the underground.
