## Basic Information

- **Name**: Kair
- **Domain**: Mountains, Peaks
- **Title**: The Mountain King, Envoy of Mountains
- **Alignment**: True Neutral
- **Symbol**: A towering mountain peak with a winding path
- **Primary Worship Area**: Mountain ranges, high altitudes, and places where the majesty of the mountains is revered

### Physical Description

- **Appearance**: 

Kair, known as the Mountain King, stands as a colossal figure whose very presence evokes the timeless and majestic essence of mountain ranges. His towering form is crafted from the minerals of the earth itself—rugged stone forms his body, each muscle and curve resembling the sheer cliffs and craggy landscapes of high peaks. His skin is a tapestry of rough granite interspersed with veins of sparkling quartz, and patches of snow and ice cling to him, highlighting the crevices and contours like the snow caps that adorn the highest summits.

His eyes, deep and ice-blue, mirror the clear, piercing skies that hang above the tallest mountains. They reflect a sharp, crystalline light, reminiscent of sunlight glinting off icy peaks. His gaze is both awe-inspiring and daunting, capturing the solitary and pristine beauty of the high altitudes.

Kair’s hair is a wild, flowing mane of snow and ice, ever-shifting and shimmering with a life of its own. It cascades around his shoulders like an avalanche, sparkling with frozen droplets that catch the light with every movement. Surrounding him, a perpetual mist and thin clouds form a cloak that billows and swirls, mimicking the mountain winds that sweep through rocky passes and over sharp ridges.
- **Height**: 500m 
- **Weight**: Unknown
- **Distinguishing Features**: His most notable features besides his towering height,  are his ice-blue eyes, and his hair made of ice and snow.

## Mythology and Worship

- **Creation Myth**: 

In the dawn of the world's making, when the earth was still young and malleable, Talam, the Primordial of Earth, sought to impose structure upon the chaotic landmasses that swirled across the globe. To aid him in this monumental task, Talam decided to create progeny who would embody and oversee the various facets of his domain. From the mightiest elements of the earth, Talam forged his first and most powerful son, Kair, destined to become the Mountain King.

Kair was born from the heart of the oldest and most formidable mountains. Talam gathered the essence of granite, the resilience of basalt, and the purity of snow to craft a figure that would personify the strength and permanence of the peaks. As Kair rose from the earth, his form towered above the landscapes, his presence as imposing and majestic as the mountains he was born from.

From the moment of his creation, Kair was instilled with a profound sense of duty. Talam entrusted him with the guardianship of the world's mountain ranges, from the soaring heights where the eagles nest to the deepest caves where ancient secrets lay hidden. Kair worked alongside his father, shaping the peaks and carving the valleys that would define the landscapes of Staterum. With each ridge he formed and each gorge he etched, Kair imbued the earth with a sense of permanence and stability.
