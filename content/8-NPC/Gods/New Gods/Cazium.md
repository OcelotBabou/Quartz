*[kaz-ee-um]*
## Basic Information

- **Name**: Cazium
- **Domain**: Arts, Crafts, Creation
- **Title**: The Master Artisan, The Forger of Wonders
- **Alignment**: Lawful Neutral
- **Symbol**: A hammer and anvil
- **Primary Worship Area**: Dwarven cities, workshops, and guild halls. [[Kair'Croi]], 

## Physical Description

- **Appearance**: Despite having the proportions of a dwarf, Cazium is a towering god. His skin is made of solid stone while his eyes are radiant gems. His beard is long and braided, appearing to be made of intricate chains of metal. He is often depicted wearing a smith’s apron and wielding a massive hammer. His arms are muscular and robust, covered in intricate carvings and runes that glow faintly in a turquoise hue. 
- **Height**: 450cm
- **Weight**: Roughly 6 tonnes
- **Distinguishing Features**: His most notable features are his stone-like skin, gem-like eyes, and his intricately braided metallic beard.

## Personality

- **Traits**: Reserved, contemplative, and enigmatic. Cazium has an aura of mystery and is known for his focus on the beauty and artistry that can be drawn from the world's resources. 
- **Strengths**: Mastery over the creation of art and craftsmanship from the earth’s resources. His massive bulk and hardiness make him a formidable foe in close quarters.
- **Weaknesses**: Cazium's detachment from the other gods and his tendency to be aloof can make him seem distant and unapproachable. His focus lies purely with dwarves and gnomes and can be seen as hostile to other races. 
- **Goals and Motivations**: Cazium is driven by the desire to uncover and manifest the hidden beauty within the world’s resources. His primary motivation is to inspire creation and artistry through the raw materials of the earth, revealing the potential for magnificence in every stone and metal.

- **Attitude towards Mortals**: Cazium is both a patron and a challenge to mortals. He provides the materials and inspiration for artistic and crafting endeavors but expects that his followers will seek out and appreciate the deeper beauty in their creations. ## Mythology and Worship

- **Creation Myth**: Cazium’s creation is shrouded in mystery. According to dwarven lore, he was born from the heart of the world, embodying the earth’s potential to create beauty from its resources. He is seen as a personification of the world’s artistic essence, emerging to shape and perfect the raw materials found within the earth.
- **Major Cults and Religious Practices**: Worship of Cazium is often found among artisans, miners, and those who work with precious materials. His followers may engage in practices that honor the earth’s resources and seek to enhance their skills in creating beautiful and functional works. Rituals often involve crafting ceremonies, offerings of precious materials, and prayers for inspiration and skill.
- **Festivals**: Festivals dedicated to Cazium celebrate the beauty and craftsmanship of the world. These may include art exhibitions, craftsmanship contests, and ceremonies that showcase the finest works created from natural resources. Such events serve to honor the artistry inspired by Cazium and the beauty drawn from the earth.
- **Clergy and Temples**: Temples dedicated to Cazium are often grand structures made of stone and adorned with intricate carvings and precious materials. The clergy are typically skilled artisans and craftsmen who dedicate their lives to perfecting their craft and creating works that reflect Cazium’s divine inspiration. These temples also serve as centers of learning and creativity for those who wish to follow in his footsteps.

## Significant Historical Impact

- **Major Plot Points in World History**: Cazium has influenced significant advancements in art, architecture, and craftsmanship throughout history. His impact is particularly noted in periods of great artistic and cultural development, where his followers have achieved remarkable feats of creation and beauty.
- **Artifacts**:
    - **Heart of the World**: A legendary artifact said to be the source of Cazium’s divine power, capable of transforming raw materials into extraordinary works of art.
    - **The Artisan’s Hammer**: A mythical tool used by Cazium himself to shape and perfect the earth’s resources, believed to enhance the skills of any craftsman who wields it.
