**Physical Description**

- **Appearance**: 

Elborn Luxrise has golden blonde hair, long and flowing, that cascades over his shoulders like a waterfall of light, framing his delicate, fair-skinned face. Elborn’s facial features are finely drawn, with high cheekbones and a gentle jawline that complement his thoughtful, introspective gaze. His eyes, a striking shade of green, are deep pools of wisdom and compassion. hese eyes light up with an inner glow when he speaks of his faith or when performing his clerical duties, captivating those who look upon him.

His attire is a mix of functionality and ceremonial significance, consisting of robes that shimmer with subtle, woven symbols of [[Gahrian]]. These robes, though lightweight, are crafted from fabrics that seem to capture and play with the light. Around his neck, he wears an intricately carved amulet, a sacred artifact that not only serves as a symbol of his devotion but also as a focal point for his prayers and blessings.
- **Height**: 175 cm

**Personality**

- **Traits**: Wise, serene, and introspective
- **Strengths**: Deeply spiritual, empathetic, and an adept healer
- **Weaknesses**: Can be judgmental of those less versed in the ways of the Gods. 
- **Goals and Motivations**: Currently, to ensure the festival in [[Hu Dao]] is a success and follows correct protocol

**Background**

Elborn rarely delves into conversations about his own past, preferring instead to redirect any such discussions towards the deity he serves, Gahrian, or to learn more about those he converses with. His current journey to Hu Dao is in serves of the Clergy of the Sun God, sent to oversee the spiritual aspects of the upcoming festival.

Elborn's anticipation for the festival is twofold. Professionally, it presents an ideal platform to propagate the teachings and blessings of Gahrian, allowing him to reach a broader audience and reinforce the spiritual significance of the event. Personally, he sees this event as a strategic opportunity to foster and repair the strained relations between humans and elves. Despite his enthusiasm for this mission, Elborn's approach remains measured and somewhat reserved, contrasting with the more outgoing demeanor of his fellow cleric, Tiana.

While Elborn initially made concerted efforts to engage with the human merchants, his experiences led him to a quiet resignation regarding these interactions. After several attempts at conversation that perhaps did not go as hoped, he has chosen to distance himself from the merchants, focusing instead on those more receptive to his spiritual messages and company.

- **Combat Skills**: While not a warrior, Elborn is capable of using his staff for defensive purposes.
- **Technical Skills**: Expert in ancient rituals and theological lore, proficient in the use of divine magics for healing and protection.
- **Special Abilities**: Elborn can channel divine energy to heal wounds or ward off evil, and he has the ability to communicate with celestial beings.
