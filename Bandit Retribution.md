### **Intro Letter**

To Whom It May Concern,

I am reaching out in a time of dire need. My traveling companions and I were ruthlessly attacked by a group of bandits while en route to Hu Dao. Tragically, this encounter resulted in the death of my dearest friend. I seek assistance to bring these villains to justice.

The attack occurred just outside the perimeters of Lake Dao. In the chaos, I managed to track the culprits to the lake's edge but unfortunately lost their trail as they escaped into the lake by boat.

While my current finances are limited, I have earned a significant credit within the guild and would happily offer my current reserves. Furthermore, I pledge to find additional means to compensate your brave efforts adequately.

If you are willing to aid in this matter, please meet me at the Adventurers’ Guild between 6 and 7 AM. I am at the guild every morning during these hours to seek assistance. Outside of these times, I will be scouting the surrounding forests for any further clues that might lead us to these foul miscreants.

Your help in this matter would not only bring peace to my friend’s restless spirit but also help secure the roads for future travellers.

Yours sincerely, Bradbury Raturian
