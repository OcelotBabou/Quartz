*[ Or-ey-LEE-us Vay]*

A legendary master of False Life and Simulacrum magic whose unparalleled mastery of Excess has allowed him to survive for centuries, quietly pursuing the impossible dream of creating true life beneath the Paloto Swamps.



Aurelius no longer possesses a true body.

Centuries of pushing Excess magic beyond every conceivable limit have destroyed any semblance of his humanity.

His original form has become an ever-growing organism buried beneath his laboratory.

It resembles neither man nor beast.

A sprawling mass of living muscle, eyes, mouths, redundant organs, vestigial limbs and endlessly regenerating flesh has gradually merged with the architecture itself. Bones reinforce walls. Veins run beneath floorboards. Nervous tissue stretches through corridors like roots.

The building is, quite literally, becoming part of him.

The Ratfolk seen by visitors is merely one of many living vessels directly puppeteered from within.

He has not walked upon his own feet for centuries.

## Overview

Hidden beneath the tangled waterways of the Paloto Swamps, Aurelius Vey is regarded by the Ratfolk as an eccentric but brilliant scholar whose understanding of biology borders on miraculous. Travelers fortunate enough to earn an audience find an unusually polite and thoughtful mage more interested in exchanging ideas than displaying power. His work has saved countless injured explorers and advanced Ratfolk understanding of medicine, though many leave his laboratory with the uncomfortable feeling that they have witnessed knowledge not entirely meant for mortal eyes.

# Appearance

Aurelius is a slight Ratfolk whose age is difficult to determine. His fur, once a deep charcoal grey, has faded almost completely white around the muzzle and ears, giving him the appearance of a kindly old academic. Fine brass spectacles rest perpetually on the bridge of his nose, often sliding lower as he becomes absorbed in conversation or research.

Rather than the robes expected of an archmage, he dresses in practical clothing better suited to a physician than a wizard. A dark waistcoat sits over a cream shirt with neatly rolled sleeves, while an equally well-maintained leather apron is often stained with ink, alchemical reagents, or traces of herbs gathered from the surrounding marsh. Numerous pockets carry notebooks, scalpels, magnifying lenses and other curious instruments whose purpose is rarely explained unless asked.

Despite his advanced years, his movements remain surprisingly steady. He speaks softly, rarely raising his voice, and greets both strangers and old acquaintances with the same gentle smile. His laboratory reflects much the same personality—immaculately organised, filled with preserved specimens, living plants, anatomical sketches and carefully catalogued research rather than clutter or chaos.

The only truly unusual feature is his eyes. Though warm and attentive, they seem to linger a fraction too long upon those he meets, studying posture, breathing and movement with an almost instinctive fascination before returning to ordinary conversation as though nothing had happened.

# Personality

Aurelius possesses an almost limitless curiosity about the natural world. Every creature, illness and magical anomaly represents another opportunity to understand the remarkable complexity of life. He speaks with genuine enthusiasm and treats even the simplest questions as worthy of thoughtful discussion.

He is unfailingly courteous, believing knowledge is best shared rather than hoarded. Visitors are frequently offered refreshments before conversations drift naturally toward anatomy, ecology, magical theory or philosophy. Students and apprentices are welcomed regardless of experience, provided they demonstrate a willingness to learn honestly.

Yet beneath this warmth lies something difficult to define. Aurelius approaches subjects that most consider deeply personal—life, death, injury and the body—with complete emotional detachment. Where others see tragedy, he often sees unanswered questions. He is neither callous nor cruel; rather, he appears to have spent so long contemplating existence itself that conventional notions of discomfort or taboo simply no longer occur to him.

Those who spend enough time with him often describe an unsettling contradiction: they never once feel threatened, yet somehow leave feeling profoundly uneasy.

# Background

Little is known of Aurelius Vey before his arrival in the Paloto Swamps many decades ago. Ratfolk oral tradition speaks of an elderly scholar who simply appeared one season carrying little more than a collection of journals and an impossible breadth of knowledge.

Rather than seeking wealth or influence, Aurelius established an isolated laboratory beneath the swamp and devoted himself entirely to research. Over the years he has become one of the foremost authorities on anatomy, disease, biological adaptation and the interaction between living organisms and magic. His findings have quietly spread across the continent through former students, copied manuscripts and physicians who have sought his guidance.

Although many scholars attempt to uncover his origins, Aurelius himself rarely speaks of his past. Questions about his early life are met with gentle humour before the conversation inevitably returns to the discoveries yet to be made.

# Skills & Abilities

### Master Arcanist

Widely considered among the greatest living practitioners of Excess magic, Aurelius possesses an unparalleled understanding of spells relating to vitality, restoration and biological manipulation.

### Anatomical Expertise

His knowledge of physiology extends across nearly every known race, making him one of the most accomplished surgeons and healers in Staterum.

### Alchemical Research

Combining traditional medicine with magical experimentation, Aurelius continually develops new treatments, restorative compounds and methods for preserving life.

### Scholar of Life

His greatest strength lies not in combat, but in observation. Aurelius possesses an extraordinary ability to identify diseases, mutations, magical afflictions and unusual biological phenomena simply through careful examination.

# Relationships

**The Ratfolk**

Viewed with equal measures of admiration and bewilderment. Many Ratfolk regard Aurelius as one of the greatest scholars to ever reside within the swamps, though few claim to fully understand his work.

**Medical Community**

Physicians and healers throughout Siar'Morrin often cite his research, even if few have met the man himself.

**Arcane Scholars**

Respected as an unparalleled authority on biological magic, though some consider his research unusually... ambitious.
