*[veh-NAH-mee kreh-nos]*

### Basic Information

- **Name**: Venami Krenos
- **Species**: Human (Demigod)
- **Gender**: Female
- **Age**: Approximately 310 years
- **Occupation**: Advisor and Defender of Wall Paleomyna
- **Affiliation**: [[New Birania]]
- **Home Region**: [[Troianos]], [[Amyna]]

### Physical Description

- **Appearance**:

Venami is an imposing woman, standing considerably taller than most humans with a broad, powerful frame that immediately betrays her divine heritage. Her shoulders are wide, her arms heavily developed and her legs carry the dense musculature of a lifelong warrior. Yet there is little brutish about her appearance; she carries her considerable size with effortless poise, possessing a statuesque beauty and commanding presence that has inspired countless artistic depictions over her long life.

Although Venami ages far more slowly than a mortal, more than three centuries of life are finally becoming visible. Fine lines gather around her eyes and mouth, while streaks of silver increasingly run through her otherwise dark hair. Her features remain striking and strong, with a pronounced jaw, high cheekbones and dark, warm-toned skin. Rather than attempting to disguise her advancing age, Venami wears it comfortably.

Away from the battlefield, Venami favours elegant gowns and draped garments inspired by the traditional styles of old Birania. Whites, creams and deep royal tones are commonly accented with gold and carefully worked gemstones, allowing her considerable physique to remain apparent without sacrificing the refined appearance expected of a royal advisor. Crystalline jewellery is particularly prominent, both reflecting her magical affinity and providing material she can manipulate should the need arise.

In battle, this elegance gives way to magnificent crystal-reinforced armour. Plates of metal are interwoven with magically shaped gemstones and crystalline structures that catch and scatter surrounding light. Combined with her towering stature and glaive, Venami can become almost painfully brilliant beneath open sunlight.

- **Height**: 198 cm
- **Weight**: 145 kg
- **Distinguishing Features**: Her exceptional height and muscular build make Venami difficult to overlook. Her eyes possess a subtle crystalline quality, particularly noticeable under bright light.

### Personality

- **Traits**: Confident, dignified, warm, competitive and remarkably difficult to unsettle. Venami makes a conscious effort to conduct herself with the grace expected of both a demigod and prominent advisor.
- **Strengths**: Fearless, exceptionally experienced and unwavering under pressure. Her tremendous self-confidence allows her to remain composed even when insulted, threatened or confronted by seemingly impossible circumstances.
- **Weaknesses**: Her confidence can border on excessive, making it difficult to convince her that a challenge is genuinely beyond her abilities. She also struggles to resist opportunities to personally test herself when a difficult opponent or dangerous expedition presents itself.
- **Goals and Motivations**: To safeguard [[Wall Paleomyna]] and the people living beneath its protection, while continuing the heroic legacy traditionally associated with the children of Krenos.

### Background

- **Early Life**: Born near the end of the [[Age of Independence]], Venami is the daughter of [[Krenos]] and an unidentified mortal woman. Her birth was considered unusual, as surviving traditions overwhelmingly describe Krenos' other mortal children as sons. Venami nevertheless quickly demonstrated the tremendous physical ability associated with her divine lineage and went on to earn a heroic reputation equal to any of her famed half-brothers.
- **Education**: Venami's education was accumulated across centuries rather than within any single institution. She has studied warfare, history, statecraft and magical practice under numerous teachers, while much of her martial expertise was earned through direct experience during some of the most turbulent periods in Siar'Morrin's history.
- **Key Events**: Venami survived the Age of Darkness ([[AoD]]) and participated in numerous conflicts throughout the centuries that followed. In more recent times she has settled in [[Troianos]], where her extraordinary experience has made her an invaluable advisor in the continued defence of [[Wall Paleomyna]].
- **Relationships**: Highly respected among the soldiers and commanders stationed along Wall Paleomyna, Venami occupies an unusual position outside the conventional military hierarchy. Commanders frequently seek her counsel, particularly concerning threats or defensive strategies she has encountered during previous generations. Her divine lineage also maintains her close association with followers of [[Krenos]], although Venami generally prefers to be judged by her own deeds rather than those of her father.

### Skills and Abilities

- **Combat Skills**: An exceptionally accomplished warrior with centuries of combat experience. Venami specialises in the glaive, using her tremendous strength and reach alongside a surprisingly refined and mobile fighting style.
- **Technical Skills**: Possesses extensive knowledge of defensive warfare, battlefield tactics and the creatures inhabiting the lands beyond Wall Paleomyna. Her longevity provides her with first-hand knowledge of events now known to most only through historical records.
- **Special Abilities**: In addition to the immense physical strength and resilience inherited from Krenos, Venami possesses a powerful Earth affinity strongly inclined toward Light. Her Personal Art centres upon gemstones and crystalline materials, which she can reshape and enhance to provide unusual flexibility, resilience and cutting strength. In combat, Venami exploits the interaction between crystal and Light to overwhelm an opponent's senses. Her armour and glaive refract surrounding light at unnatural angles, producing brilliant flashes, distorted silhouettes and lingering afterimages that make judging her movements exceptionally difficult. Fighting Venami beneath the open sun is often described as attempting to duel while staring directly into it.

### Equipment

- **Weapons**: Wields a large crystal-enhanced glaive specifically designed around her magical abilities. Its crystalline blade can be subtly reshaped during combat, allowing Venami to reinforce damaged sections, alter its edge or redistribute material as required.
- **Gadgets**: Carries several carefully selected gemstones and crystals of differing structures and properties, providing additional material through which she can channel her affinity.
- **Personal Belongings**: Is rumoured to have a secret room stocked full of gifts and offerings from hopeful suitors. 

### Significant Events

- **Major Plot Points**: Having survived the Age of Darkness and much of the current age, Venami has become one of New Birania's few living connections to the world that existed before its collapse. Her presence at Wall Paleomyna provides its defenders with centuries of accumulated experience alongside the considerable reassurance of having a daughter of Krenos standing among them.
- **Important Missions**: Currently serves as an advisor to the leadership of [[Troianos]] and the defenders of Wall Paleomyna. She also occasionally accompanies expeditions beyond the Wall into [[Trog]] territory, particularly when circumstances demand someone capable of confronting threats beyond the abilities of ordinary soldiers.
