**Basic Information**

- **Name**: Danthel Elistar
- **Species**: [[Lux]] Elf
- **Gender**: Male
- **Age**: 123
- **Occupation**: Tavern Owner
- **Affiliation**: [[Hu Dao]]

**Appearance**

Danthel is tall and slender, with golden blonde hair typically pulled back into a neat ponytail, emphasizing his sharp, elfin features. His attire blends the traditional elements of a tavern keeper with an upper-class flair, featuring rich fabrics that are both stylish and suited to his work environment. This includes a finely embroidered vest, a crisply tailored shirt, and trousers that balance sophistication with comfort, perfect for overseeing the bustling activity of his tavern.

**Background**

Danthel Elistar is the proud owner of The Rising Sun, a renowned tavern known for its vibrant atmosphere and exceptional service located just outside the Merchant’s Villa in Hu Dao. At 123 years old, he has spent several decades perfecting the art of hospitality, making The Rising Sun one of the largest and most well-established taverns in the area. His pride in his establishment is evident in every detail, from the carefully curated menu to the impeccably trained staff that includes his son, who helps him run the day-to-day operations.

[[Elborn Luxrise]], an old acquaintance of Danthel,  speaks highly of him, not only as a friend but also as a stalwart member of the community, whose tavern serves as a welcoming haven for travelers and locals alike. Elborn has insisted that there is no better place to stay within Hu Dao, particularly for non-humans, and that Danthel will have accommodations ready for those from the caravan seeking rest and refreshment upon their arrival in Hu Dao.
