**Dwarves**
### 1. **Structure & Syntax**

- Dwarven place and clan names are typically **compound words**, often with a **prefix + root + suffix** structure.
    
- Apostrophes (`’`) are common, marking either:
    
    - a **hard break** in pronunciation (hammered pauses), or
        
    - an **ancient word root** carried into modern dwarvish.
        
- Names are **harsh, clipped, and heavy on consonants**, reflecting dwarves’ stone-and-steel culture.
    

---

### 2. **Common Prefixes** _(often describe geography or symbolism)_

- **Kair-** → “Mountain”
    
- **Zeel-** → “Darkness / Depths”
    
- **Grun-** → “Strength / Weight”
    
- **Dur-** → “Heart / Enduring”
    
- **Bral-** → “Forge / Fire”
    
- **Thar-** → “Legacy / Ancestor / Founder”
    
- **Mor-** → “Great / High / Mighty”
    
- **Khaz-** → “Fortress / City” (traditional, older form, like Khazad-dûm influence)
    

---

### 3. **Common Suffixes** _(often describe purpose or nature)_

- **-Croi** → “Hold / Fortress / Stronghold” (dominant suffix for major dwarven cities)
    
- **-Dur** → “Stone / Hall”
    
- **-Karr** → “Mountain / Ridge”
    
- **-Bral** → “Forge / Smithy”
    
- **-Drakk** → “Peak / Spire”
    
- **-Holl / -Hall** → “Hall / Seat of power”
    
- **-Varr** → “Clan / Family domain”
    

---

### 4. **Clan & Personal Names**

- Dwarven **clan names** are descriptive, usually linking to craft, stone, or ancestry:
    
    - **Ironbrand, Slateback, Emberforge, Stonevein, Goldhammer**.
        
- **Personal names** are usually one or two syllables, blunt and resonant:
    
    - **Tharrek, Borin, Rhyla, Korrin, Gralm, Dain**.
        
- Names often recycle across generations (lineage is revered). Titles are more important than uniqueness.
    

---

### 5. **Tone & Themes**

- Dwarven names should feel **weighty and enduring**. Avoid softness or flowing vowels.
    
- Apostrophes should feel **functional, not decorative** — a mark of dwarven precision.
    
- Place names often combine **a material/geography prefix** with a **fortress/craft suffix**.


# Lux Elves

### . **Structure**

- **First Names**: Typically melodic, flowing, with soft consonants and bright vowels. Often 2–4 syllables.
    
- **Second Names**: Always tied to **light, radiance, celestial, or vigilance motifs.** They can be:
    
    - Direct references to light (Lux, Illumina, Sol, Radi, Dawn).
        
    - Compound words evoking celestial imagery (Lightbearer, Suncrest, Starveil).
        
    - More abstract but still tied to clarity, purity, or guardianship (Illuminor, Vigilas, Aetherion).
        

---

### 2. **Themes & Meanings**

- **Light & Radiance**: Sol-, Lux-, Illum-, Radi-, Auri-
    
- **Celestial Bodies**: -rise, -crest, -flare, -ray, -star, -dawn, -sun, -halo
    
- **Vigilance & Guardianship**: Vigil-, Cust-, Sent-, -ward, -keep
    
- **Divine Splendour**: Aure-, Sanct-, Lumin-, -mir, -elion, -thiel
    

---

### 3. **Stylistic Rules**

- Names tend to be **aspirational or symbolic**, chosen at birth to honour Gahrian or the family’s devotion.
    
- First names are melodic but dignified — e.g. _Elbron, Seraphiel, Tiana, Auris, Kaelen, Lysandra_.
    
- Second names often **blend elvish poetic form with symbolic meaning**. Some are more ornate (_Illuminor, Aetherion_), while others are concise but radiant (_Luxrise, Dawnborn_).
    
- Apostrophes are rare in Lux names (contrast with dwarves), but compound words are common.
    

---

# Tenebrim Elves

### 1. **Structure**

- **First Names:** Often short and grounded, with harder or more guttural sounds than Lux elf names. Two syllables is common, sometimes three.
    
- **Second Names / Epithets:** Descriptive, symbolic, often tied to nature, the hunt, or shadow. They can be literal (“Ashbark, Nightfang”), poetic (“Whisperthorn, Silentbrook”), or metaphorical (“Twilight-Seeker, Shadebinder”).
    
- Names often carry the weight of **deeds, family heritage, or spirit-guides** rather than abstract ideals.
    

---

### 2. **Themes & Meanings**

- **Natural Elements:** Ash, Thorn, Bark, Stone, River, Leaf, Mist, Wolf, Crow, Elk.
    
- **Shadow & Mystery:** Shade, Night, Whisper, Silent, Dusk, Hollow, Veil.
    
- **Predators & The Hunt:** Fang, Talon, Claw, Stalker, Prowl.
    
- **Poetic Imagery:** Silentbrook, Moonveil, Thornsong, Shadow-watcher.
    

---

### 3. **Stylistic Rules**

- **Compound Surnames/Epithets**: Frequently hyphenated or blended, reflecting deeds or traits. (_Silentbrook, Ashfang, Moonveil_).
    
- **First Names**: Less ornate than Lux names, but still elven — a balance of elegance and grit.
    
- Unlike the Lux, **apostrophes may appear** as markers of dialect or heritage (_Ka’ven, Drath’el_).
    
- Some names are **earned** rather than given — particularly epithets like _Nightfang_ or _Shadow-Walker_, bestowed by the community.
