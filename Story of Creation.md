In the boundless expanse of the nascent universe, the incorporeal gods waged an eternal conflict, their immense energies clashing in ceaseless battle. These beings, timeless and unyielding, fought with such ferocity that their skirmishes spilled over into the material plane. From their clashes, specks of matter coalesced over eons, eventually amassing into a sentient entity—Quawerah, the embodiment of Matter.

Quawerah, born from the strife of the gods, sought an end to the endless conflict, aspiring instead to a cosmos filled with creation rather than destruction. Aware that the incorporeal gods could not be swayed from their destructive nature, Quawerah siphoned their energy in secret, amassing power to forge a universe of substance and structure.

As the eons passed, the drain of power did not go unnoticed. Tyerikst, one of the warring gods, traced the loss back to the slumbering Quawerah. In a decisive moment, Quawerah awoke, expending its accumulated might to forge two beings of light and darkness: Gahrian and Alache. Born from the unity of all matter and the vacuum left by its concentration, they swiftly vanquished Tyerikst, absorbing its power directly.

Emboldened, Gahrian and Alache waged a relentless war against their progenitors, the incorporeals, harvesting their essence to fuel the creation of stars, planets, and the very fabric of the universe. As their power grew, so did their ambition. driven by an inherent need to manifest his grandeur, created the first Vice, Matiodox (Vanity), reflecting his excessive pride and ambition. This act was the first spark in a chain reaction of creation and conflict. Alache, bound by a sense of duty to maintain balance, viewed Gahrian's actions with growing concern, fearing the potential upheaval these new beings could bring.

Alache's caution only fueled Gahrian's desire to dominate, leading him to create additional Vices, each more potent and reflective of his worst traits. Alache, in response and disturbed by the escalating threat to cosmic balance, crafted his own Vices, aiming to counteract Gahrian's expansions but inadvertently contributing to the cycle of conflict.

As their rivalry escalated, the universe itself began to bear the weight of their actions. The creation of the Vices, simple yet potent embodiments of negative extremes, started to strain the very essence of Quawerah, which pleaded for unity and cessation of their conflicts. Ignoring these pleas, Gahrian and Alache's actions eventually led to Quawerah's cataclysmic fracture, giving rise to the elemental gods: Lasair (Fire), Ooscie (Water), Talam (Earth), and Gaoth (Air).

The emergence of these elemental forces sparked a battle so fierce it threatened the universe itself. Realizing their folly, Gahrian and Alache united to quell the apocalyptic turmoil. Through immense struggle, they managed to banish these primordial beings to separate dimensions, stabilizing the material universe.

In the aftermath, the twins discovered that Quaderah's core remained. They sacrificed their forms to give birth to Statera, the god of balance, who emerged naive and untutored. Guided by the watchful remnants of Gahrian and Alache, now the ever watchful sun and moon, Statera, along with Talam, began the intricate work of shaping Staterum, their new home.
