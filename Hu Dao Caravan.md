Amidst the rugged expanse of the wilderness, south of the [[Ridgeback Forest]], an eclectic caravan makes its way towards the lake city of [[Hu Dao]]. Leading this caravan is [[Bradbury Raturian]], whose unwavering optimism binds the group together, and his vigilant assistant, [[Lieve Moonsight]], a keen-eyed moon elf ranger. Together this duo are tasked with the safety of the caravan on its long journey.

Among the caravan's members are the entertainers of Mithrallica, now boasting their latest addition—a bonafide Kitsune performer, whose presence has injected a fresh burst of excitement into the troupe. Her talents, alongside her handmaiden, promise a spectacular show at the upcoming Festival of [[Gahrian]], drawing curious eyes and hopeful whispers from her fellow travelers.

The spiritual core of the caravan is held by two elf clerics, [[Elborn Starfall]] and [[Tiana Lightweaver]], whose serene grace contrasts sharply with the troupe's flamboyance. They are joined by [[Virag Greytail]], an elderly ratfolk warrior whose calm wisdom belies the prejudices faced by his kind. His stoic nature serves as a personal guard to the clerics, ensuring their safety and the sanctity of their mission.

A mysterious Cia’an, known for his reserved demeanor and enigmatic aura, has been part of the caravan since its inception. His presence is so subtle and unassuming that other members could hardly be blamed for overlooking him entirely.

The oddest companion, however, is an unusual sentient teddy bear gunslinger, whose very existence challenges the boundaries of the known. This curious figure, both adorable and endearing (despite his attempts to appear intimidation), firmly believes his value will become undeniable should any trouble arise

However, tensions are palpable among the caravan’s members, particularly from the human merchants [[Calvin Hargrove]], [[Miranda Fleet]], and [[Thorton Crewe]], who, despite their financial motivations to reach Hu Dao, often display their discomfort and disdain for their non-human companions.

As the caravan moves through dust-swept roads and verdant valleys, these individuals—each from different walks of life—share the common goal of reaching Hu Dao.
